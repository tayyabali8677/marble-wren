Program Guidelines
The Japan Foundation invites individuals and organizations that are planning international exchange projects and activities to participate in programs of the Japan Foundation.
About the Applicable Programs
The Japan Foundation conducts programs in the three major areas of Arts and Cultural Exchange, Japanese-Language Education Overseas, and Japanese Studies and International Dialogue. There are applicable programs in each of these areas, and support is provided for activities conducted by individuals and organizations that are involved in international exchange.
Please read the General Instructions thoroughly, and then go to the Program List, organized by categories and objectives, for general information of each program.
General Instructions
Program List
If you find in the Program List a program that matches the project that you or your organization are planning, please obtain the respective application instructions and application forms. Complete the forms and submit your application to the specified destination.
The Japan Foundation screens the applications and provides support to successful applicants.

Japanese-Language Education Overseas [Language]
Japanese Language Education for Japanese Children Resident Overseas and for the Descendants of Migrants
Japanese Language Education for Japanese Children Resident Overseas and for the Descendants of Migrants
Article 19 of the Act on Promotion of Japanese Language Education (Act No. 48 of 2019) establishes the goal of “enhancing Japanese language education for Japanese children resident overseas and for the descendants of migrants.” Moreover, the Basic Policy for the Comprehensive and Effective Implementation of Measures to Promote Japanese Language Education, decided by the Cabinet in 2020, clearly states that the Japan Foundation (JF) will make efforts to grasp the actual conditions of the “Japanese language education environment for the descendants of Japanese nationals who emigrated overseas and children with foreign and Japanese parents,” and will provide necessary support. In this section, we present an introduction to the activities proactively undertaken in each country and region in tandem with initiatives implemented by JF.
Project Report
On this page, we introduce the support provided by JF to enhance “Japanese language education for the descendants of Japanese nationals who emigrated overseas and children with foreign and Japanese parents” in each country and region around the world.

Program Guidelines
The Japan Foundation invites individuals and organizations that are planning international exchange projects and activities to participate in programs of the Japan Foundation.
About the Applicable Programs
The Japan Foundation conducts programs in the three major areas of Arts and Cultural Exchange, Japanese-Language Education Overseas, and Japanese Studies and International Dialogue. There are applicable programs in each of these areas, and support is provided for activities conducted by individuals and organizations that are involved in international exchange.
Please read the General Instructions thoroughly, and then go to the Program List, organized by categories and objectives, for general information of each program.
General Instructions
Program List
If you find in the Program List a program that matches the project that you or your organization are planning, please obtain the respective application instructions and application forms. Complete the forms and submit your application to the specified destination.
The Japan Foundation screens the applications and provides support to successful applicants.

Program Guidelines
The Japan Foundation invites individuals and organizations that are planning international exchange projects and activities to participate in programs of the Japan Foundation.
About the Applicable Programs
The Japan Foundation conducts programs in the three major areas of Arts and Cultural Exchange, Japanese-Language Education Overseas, and Japanese Studies and International Dialogue. There are applicable programs in each of these areas, and support is provided for activities conducted by individuals and organizations that are involved in international exchange.
Please read the General Instructions thoroughly, and then go to the Program List, organized by categories and objectives, for general information of each program.
General Instructions
Program List
If you find in the Program List a program that matches the project that you or your organization are planning, please obtain the respective application instructions and application forms. Complete the forms and submit your application to the specified destination.
The Japan Foundation screens the applications and provides support to successful applicants.

Inquiries
Prospective applicants outside Japan should contact the nearest Japan Foundation overseas office , or the nearest Embassy or Consulate-General of Japan if there is no Japan Foundation office in the country.
Prospective applicants from within Japan should contact the section in charge at the Japan Foundation domestic offices .
