Discover scholarships to support your ANU journey. Our scholarship opportunities cater to students from various backgrounds and abilities - and you will be automatically considered you for some when you apply.

Scholarships for future students
Explore entry scholarships, equity grants, and accommodation support to kickstart your journey at ANU. Many are awarded automatically when you apply for admission.
Scholarships for current students
Boost your degree with travel grants, academic merit prizes, exchange bursaries, and emergency financial support throughout your studies.
Scholarships for alumni
Access prestigious postgraduate research stipends, international exchange fellowships, and lifelong learning awards.
Featured scholarships
ANU Vice-Chancellor Scholars Program
SPOTLIGHT
ANU Vice-Chancellor Scholars Program
The ANU Vice-Chancellor Scholars Program is a major investment in making an ANU education more accessible. It provides eligible domestic undergraduate and postgraduate students with financial and practical support to overcome barriers, transition to university and thrive at ANU. Learn more
Tuckwell Scholarship
SPOTLIGHT
Tuckwell Scholarship
The Tuckwell Scholarship Program is the most transformational undergraduate scholarship program in Australia. Every year, we offer 25 talented school-leavers the opportunity to fulfil their potential by actively participating in the very best that ANU has to offer. Learn more
Previous
Next
ANU Vice-Chancellor’s International Achievement Award
Great results deserve to be recognised. Awarded in the Vice-Chancellor's name, this scholarship welcomes academically talented international students with 20% off their first-year tuition — and you're considered automatically when you apply to ANU.
ANU Changing Futures scholarship
Real support to help you get here and thrive. The ANU Changing Futures Scholarship offers domestic undergraduate students who've faced significant challenges $8,000 a year for up to five years — no separate application needed.
ANU All-Rounder Scholarship
Celebrating well-rounded achievers who lead, inspire, and give back. Unlock financial support designed to help you thrive across every aspect of university life.
ANU International Achievement Award
Reward your hard work with up to 50% off your tuition, for up to four years. Open to high-achieving international students and awarded automatically when you apply — no separate application needed.

SHOLARSHIP
Whether you are looking for financial support to start your studies or for help to move away from home for the first time, ANU has scholarship opportunities for you and your situation. Some of our scholarships recognise academic achievement and others athletic performance.
We also offer scholarships that support unique challenges and experiences and recognise leadership, like the Tuckwell Scholarship Program, the most generous and transformative undergraduate scholarship in Australia.
