Fellowships, grants and career support
Postdoctoral Fellowships​
EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships support scientists to carry out research for a period of up to two years. International mobility is a key requirement.
Scientific Exchange Grants
EMBO Scientific Exchange Grants support new, international collaborations, enabling the transfer of expertise unavailable in the applicant&#8217;s laboratory. The grants fund research visits of up to three months.
New Venture Fellowships
In memory of a bright-minded and passionate scientist, the EMBO New Venture Fellowships will help young scientists explore topics outside their current area to allow them to pursue a new research direction in their future work.
Core Facility Fellowships
EMBO Core Facility Fellowships support training for staff of core facilities that provide services to research institutions or universities. The fellowships fund international exchanges of up to one month.
Advanced Collaboration Grants
EMBO Advanced Collaboration Grants are a new scheme for group leaders in Croatia, Czech Republic, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Poland, Slovenia, and Turkey who wish to visit scientists in other EMBC Member States to develop or carry out collaborative projects, or to prepare joint grant proposals.
Young Investigator Programme
The EMBO Young Investigator Programme supports group leaders in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories for a period of four years. Networking is a key aspect of the programme.
Installation Grants
EMBO Installation Grants fund group leaders in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories in Croatia, the Czech Republic, Estonia, Lithuania, Montenegro, Poland, Portugal or Turkey for three to five years. Networking is a key aspect of the programme.
Global Investigator Network
The EMBO Global Investigator Network supports scientists in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories in Chile, India, Singapore or Taiwan for a period of four years. It provides funding for networking, collaboration and training activities.
Maria Leptin l EMBO Science Journalism Fellowships
Supporting life scientists pursuing a career in science journalism and science journalists who wish to further their scientific education.

Find a Young Investigator, Installation Grantee or Global Investigator
The  Young Investigator database  lists all current and former EMBO Young Investigators, Installation Grantees and Global Investigators. Please browse the database to find out more about their research or search by keyword to find a Young Investigator, Installation Grantee or Global Investigator working in a particular area.
If you would like to invite a Young Investigator, Installation s Grantee or Global Investigator as a speaker and obtain a  Young Investigator Lecture Grant  to support their talk, you can use the database to identify eligible scientists.

Funding for research careers
EMBO provides support for the life sciences in Europe and beyond. We fund researchers as well as conferences, training and networking activities to help create an environment in which scientists can excel.
Fellowships, grants and career support
We support outstanding life scientists at all career stages in Europe and around the world. Awardees benefit from funding, career development support and networking opportunities.
Funding for conferences and training
We provide funding and support for scientific meetings across all disciplines in the life sciences, including workshops, practical courses, lecture courses and series as well as keynote lectures.
Lecture, travel and childcare grants
We offer funding for meeting organizers to invite speakers and to cover childcare costs of speakers and participants. Travel grants to attend meetings and fee waivers are available for participants.

EMBO Scientific Exchange Grants support new, international collaborations, enabling the transfer of expertise unavailable in the applicant&#8217;s laboratory. The grants fund research visits of up to three months.
New Venture Fellowships
In memory of a bright-minded and passionate scientist, the EMBO New Venture Fellowships will help young scientists explore topics outside their current area to allow them to pursue a new research direction in their future work.
Core Facility Fellowships
EMBO Core Facility Fellowships support training for staff of core facilities that provide services to research institutions or universities. The fellowships fund international exchanges of up to one month.
Advanced Collaboration Grants
EMBO Advanced Collaboration Grants are a new scheme for group leaders in Croatia, Czech Republic, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Poland, Slovenia, and Turkey who wish to visit scientists in other EMBC Member States to develop or carry out collaborative projects, or to prepare joint grant proposals.
Young Investigator Programme
The EMBO Young Investigator Programme supports group leaders in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories for a period of four years. Networking is a key aspect of the programme.
Installation Grants
EMBO Installation Grants fund group leaders in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories in Croatia, the Czech Republic, Estonia, Lithuania, Montenegro, Poland, Portugal or Turkey for three to five years. Networking is a key aspect of the programme.
Global Investigator Network
The EMBO Global Investigator Network supports scientists in the early stages of setting up their independent laboratories in Chile, India, Singapore or Taiwan for a period of four years. It provides funding for networking, collaboration and training activities.
Maria Leptin l EMBO Science Journalism Fellowships
Supporting life scientists pursuing a career in science journalism and science journalists who wish to further their scientific education.

Effective from the Autumn 2026 Selection Round (i.e., all applications submitted after January 23, 2026, 14:00 CET ): a host laboratory may support a maximum of one candidate per selection round: in case of multiple applications to the same host lab, the office will request the lab to withdraw the exceeding application(s).

EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships support excellent postdoctoral researchers throughout Europe and the world for a period of up to two years. International mobility is a key requirement. The fellowship includes a salary or stipend, a relocation allowance and support for fellows with children. Awardees can attend an EMBO Laboratory Leadership course and become part of the global network of EMBO Fellows.
EMBO considers a first author refereed preprint equivalent to a first author publication as one of the eligibility and assessment criteria for the EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowship scheme. Please carefully read our guidelines for the definition of refereed preprint.
