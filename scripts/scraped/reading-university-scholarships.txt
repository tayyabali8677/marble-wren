Call us on: +44 (0)118 402 0900
We&rsquo;re here to help guide you through every stage of Clearing. Our Clearing hotline ( +44 (0) 118 402 0900 ) will be open during the following times:
Thursday 13 August , 8:00 to 18:00
Friday 14 August , 9:00 to 17:00
From Monday 17 August until the end of August (working days only) , 9:00 to 17:00
Applying for university through Clearing can seem complicated. To help you, we&rsquo;ve split our Clearing application guidance into sections, so you can see what to expect as you move through the process.
When you can apply
Most UK applicants get their results on 13 August 2026 and can apply through Clearing from this date .
If you&rsquo;re an international applicant , we may also need evidence of satisfactory English language proficiency, such as an IELTS (Academic) test. You need to have this evidence to apply via Clearing. Please view our list of acceptable English language tests .
1. Before you apply
Find out what to do before you apply, including setting up a UCAS account, doing your research, and making sure you&rsquo;re in Clearing.
2. Make your application
Discover what to expect when you contact the University to make a Clearing application and hopefully receive an offer from us.
3. Refer yourself (accept your offer)
Find out how to accept your offer &ndash; the process differs depending on when you applied.
4. After you've accepted your offer
Once you&rsquo;ve accepted an offer, you can apply for accommodation, confirm your student finance, and contact our Disability Advisory Service if you need to.

How to apply to the University of Reading
Find a course
Whether you're applying for an undergraduate course through UCAS, looking to begin a master’s degree, or exploring routes into research or teacher training, this page explains how to apply to the University of Reading. You’ll also find key dates, deferral information and next steps once you’ve submitted your application.
Apply for an undergraduate course
You can apply for all undergraduate courses at the University of Reading – including degrees with an Integrated Foundation Year – through the UCAS website. The UCAS application deadline for most courses is in January each year.
Learn more on our undergraduate application information page.
Apply for a master's course
If you're considering postgraduate study at Reading, you can apply through our online application portal.
Application deadlines can vary, so visit our master's application information page for more details .
Apply for a PhD
For doctoral applications, visit the Doctoral and Researcher College website . You’ll find details on topics, supervisors, funding options and the application process at the University of Reading.
International Foundation Pathways
You can apply for our International Foundation Programme or a four-year degree with an integrated International Foundation Year through our online application service .
If you cannot apply online, please phone us to request a paper form: +44 (0)118 378 8372 .
These programmes support international students in meeting academic, subject-specific and English language requirements for progression to undergraduate study at the University of Reading.
Apply for a teacher training programme
For our PGCE and teacher training routes, you must apply through the Department for Education’s Postgraduate Teacher Training portal .
For the PGCert Early Years Practice (EYTS) programme, apply via our online application system .
Can I defer entry or change my offer?
Even if you've accepted an offer from the University of Reading, you can request to delay your entry (defer) or change your course. Read our frequently asked questions about deferring an undergraduate course or deferring a master's course for more information.
Admissions policies and procedures
Admissions at the University of Reading follow a transparent and inclusive policy framework approved by the University Senate and supported by the Board for Teaching and Learning.
You can explore our full policies on our Admissions policies and procedures page , including how we assess qualifications and consider late applications or changes to study preferences.

How to apply
Applying for accommodation
At Reading, you can apply for student housing, and keep track of your application's progress, with our accommodation portal .
Read our helpful 10 steps to apply guide or watch our 'How to apply for accommodation' video to find out everything you need to know about using the portal. You can contact us with any questions you may have while applying.
This section also provides information about paying for your accommodation, and what to expect during the application process.
If you are a current student looking for University accommodation after the semester has started, we have some rooms available. Log into the RISIS portal to apply for a part-year contract.
Play video
This video is hosted by YouTube, which may collect data and use cookies. By playing it, you agree to this.
Application timeline
We have created a timeline of the major steps in the application process. Find out more about the deadlines you will need to meet and what happens after you submit your application.
Fees and instalments
More information about how and when to pay for your student accommodation, and how we can advise you about budgeting concerns you may have.
Accessible accommodation
The University of Reading provides accommodation for students with specialist requirements.
Important information
Pre-arrival information, terms and conditions, data protection and other useful information.
Apply now
Use our accommodation portal to apply for a student room at Reading. This can also be used to track your application.
Contact us
If you have a question and have already applied for a place at Reading, you can log into our application portal and contact us via our live chat .
You can also contact the Accommodation Office on 0118 200 5011 or email
accommodationonline@reading.ac.uk .

English language requirements
The English language levels generally required for our courses are outlined in the tables on this page. You can also find out more information about our pre-sessional English programme.
Please remember that we can help you if you do not yet meet these levels. When you apply, please let us know your current level of English (through your most recent IELTS or equivalent score) and we will advise you on the best route to starting a degree with us.
