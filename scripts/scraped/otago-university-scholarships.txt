Explore scholarships to help fund your study at the University of Otago. Search opportunities for undergraduate and postgraduate study, and available funding for research, conference and travel grants. Gain the financial support you need to achieve academic success.

There are in excess of NZ$18 million worth of scholarships available for students wanting to study at the University of Otago – and for Otago students wishing to study at other institutions around the world.
