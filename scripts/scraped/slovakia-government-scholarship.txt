1 September is Constitution Day in Slovakia
1 September is Constitution Day in Slovakia — a state holiday that marks the adoption of the Slovak Constitution in 1992. It symbolizes the country’s path to independence and democracy. Since 2025, it is no longer a public holiday. This means that schools, shops, and offices stay open. Still, the day carries historical and civic significance, marking the adoption of the Slovak Constitution in 1992.
