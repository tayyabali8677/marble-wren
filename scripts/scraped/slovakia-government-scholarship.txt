Call for applications – deadline 30.04.2026 – list of scholarship holders
Within this call for applications, 860 applicants (376 students, 127 PhD students, 357 university teachers/researchers/artists) have applied for a scholarship in the framework of the National Scholarship Programme of the Slovak Republic.
The Committee has awarded the scholarship to 177 applicants (72 students, 27 PhD students, 78 university teachers/researchers/artists).
List of scholarship holders can be found HERE .
All applicants will be informed about the results via e-mail soon.
