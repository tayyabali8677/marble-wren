For the better you bring
Now, more than ever, communities need people who aspire to lead with empathy, integrity and courage, in every domain from healthcare and business to technology and social change.
The McCall MacBain Scholarships bring together exceptional students who strive to engage in positive change by taking on meaningful leadership roles. McCall MacBain Scholars connect with mentors and participate in an interdisciplinary leadership program while pursuing a fully funded master’s or professional degree at McGill University.
Applications are open for Summer/Fall 2027 entry.

Apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarships
We Want to Meet You!​
Interested in applying for Summer/Fall 2027? Applications are now open!
Each year up to 30 full scholarships and 100 entrance awards will be offered to the top candidates.
Register for an information session to find out more.
We&#8217;re looking for:
Exceptional character
Do you value excellence, hard work, service, and lifelong learning? Do you strive to act with integrity, honesty, and empathy? Do you have the tenacity to pursue long-term goals? Are you willing to take meaningful risks?
Community engagement
Do you value working for and with others to address challenges facing a community? Do you pursue a breadth and depth of interests, through your activities outside the classroom?
Leadership potential
Do you have experience working with and leading others towards a common goal? Are you ready to take on tough leadership roles to advance meaningful change? Are you driven to help others and to develop the potential of those you lead?
Entrepreneurial spirit
Are you able to identify unmet needs and motivate others in developing creative solutions to address those needs? Do you believe in forging new paths and not being held back by fear of failure?
Academic strength and intellectual curiosity
Do you have a deep interest in and curiosity about people, ideas, and the world around you? Are you interested in working with people who have different perspectives? Is there a good fit between your proposed graduate program, your qualifications, and your future plans?
Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible to apply for our 2027  cohort, you will need to meet ONE of the following criteria:
You are a current student on track to earn your first bachelor&#8217;s degree by August 2027
You earned your first bachelor&#8217;s degree in the last five years (January 2021 or later)
You earned your first bachelor&#8217;s degree more than five years ago, and were 30 years or younger on January 1st, 2026
You will also need to meet the minimum degree and language requirements for McGill master’s program admission.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Scholarship Overview
What does the scholarship cover?
The McCall MacBain Scholarship covers tuition and fees for an eligible master&#8217;s program or a second-entry professional undergraduate program at McGill, a living stipend of $2,300 CAD per month during academic terms, a one-time relocation grant, and options for summer funding. Scholars also participate in a leadership development program, connect with mentors and advisors, and attend a breadth of workshops and talks.
Which graduate programs are covered under this scholarship?
The McCall MacBain Scholarship covers over 150 different master’s programs and 3 second-entry professional undergraduate programs at McGill’s downtown and Macdonald campuses. The program must be pursued full-time and consist of 45 credits or more. See this overview of eligible programs .
McCall MacBain Scholarship applicants must apply separately and be admitted to their desired graduate program(s) at McGill. Visit the McGill Graduate Admissions website to review program requirements.
Eligibility
Who is eligible to apply?
To be eligible to apply in 2026 for our 2027 cohort, you must meet ONE of the following criteria:
You are a current student on track to earn your first bachelor’s degree by August 2027
You earned your first bachelor’s degree in the last five years (January 2021 or later)
You earned your first bachelor’s degree more than five years ago, and were 30 years or younger on January 1st, 2026
You will need to meet the minimum degree and language requirements  for McGill master’s program admission. Contact McGill Graduate Admissions if you have questions about the university&#8217;s minimum requirements for admission.
Is there an age requirement?
In general, no. Current students and recent graduates of any age are welcome to apply.
To be eligible to apply in 2026 for our 2027 cohort, you must meet ONE of the following criteria:
You are a current student on track to earn your first bachelor’s degree by August 2027
You earned your first bachelor’s degree in the last five years (January 2021 or later)
You earned your first bachelor’s degree more than five years ago, and were 30 years or younger on January 1st, 2026
I earned my first bachelor’s degree more than five years ago, and I will turn 31 this year. Am I still eligible to apply this year?
Yes, you are eligible to apply.
I started a bachelor’s degree more than five years ago, but never completed it. I am now on track to complete a bachelor’s degree for the first time and I am over 30. Am I still eligible?
Yes. For eligibility, what matters is the date you earned or will earn a bachelor’s degree for the first time.
Is there a grade requirement?
Applicants will need to meet the academic requirements of their desired McGill programs, including GPA.
You will need to apply separately and be admitted to at least one eligible program at McGill University. You should review your desired programs&#8217; admission requirements, including but not limited to GPA, well in advance.
Keep in mind that one of the five selection criteria for the scholarships is &#8220;academic strength and intellectual curiosity&#8221;. We will look for evidence of this throughout your application, including transcripts.
The measure of academic strength is not limited to a narrow focus on GPA. While GPA is important, we are interested in the fit with your proposed McGill programs, as well as the breadth and difficulty of the courses taken alongside your other commitments. Of course, we recognize that some programs offer more opportunity for breadth than others.
Can I apply if I am a part-time student?
Yes. However, if you receive this scholarship, you will need to enrol full-time in an eligible program at McGill.
Can I apply if I am currently enrolled in a graduate program?
You may apply if you are currently enrolled in a graduate program, but intend to start a new eligible graduate program in the following year.
This scholarship is for students who intend to start an eligible graduate program in the following year.
Can I apply for the scholarship if I already have a graduate degree?
Yes, as long as you plan to apply for an eligible McGill graduate program that starts the following year. Like all applicants, you will be asked to show in your application how the proposed program will help lay the foundation for your long-term goals and aspirations.
Can I apply for the scholarship before I have applied for an eligible program at McGill University?
Absolutely. Most people will apply for the scholarship before they apply to a compatible program at McGill. You should apply by the program&#8217;s deadline. If you are selected as a McCall MacBain Scholarship finalist, you will need to apply to McGill programs in December at the latest, even if the program deadlines are later.
Can I apply for the scholarship if I have deferred admission to McGill University?
Yes, you can apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarships in 2026 if you have been admitted to an eligible McGill program and deferred enrolment to Summer / Fall 2027.
Can I re-apply if I applied for the scholarship last year?
In most cases, yes, assuming you still meet the eligibility criteria and you update and resubmit your application.
As of the 2022-2023 cycle, applicants may not apply more than twice to the McCall MacBain Scholarships at McGill. Applications submitted in 2020 or 2021 are exempt from this rule.
Applicants who apply only once may not re-apply in the following situations:
Candidates who accepted a Regional Award in a previous year;
Candidates who accepted an invitation to attend final interviews in a previous year.
These candidates may not re-apply in future years. Other candidates, including those who declined a Regional Award in a previous year, may re-apply once more.
Application Process
When should I apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarship?
The McCall MacBain Scholarship deadline is 12-15 months before the start of your program at McGill. Most people will therefore apply for the scholarship before they apply to a graduate program at McGill.
After you apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarship, you will need to apply to your intended McGill programs by their stated deadlines. If you are selected as a finalist for McCall MacBain Scholarships, you will need to apply to McGill in December even if the program&#8217;s deadline is later in the academic year.
How can I apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarship?
Please attend an information session . Applications will open in June 2026 for our 2027 cohort.
What is the university endorsement or nomination process?
The endorsement process is different for  Canadian  applicants  (Canadian citizens, Canadian permanent residents, or those with accepted refugee status in Canada) and for  international applicants  (all other applicants).
For  Canadian applicants , current students and recent graduates (those who graduated in 2024, 2025, or 2026) must be endorsed by their university.  In most cases, Canadian applicants do not need to contact their university to request the endorsement. Institution-specific instructions may be provided at the start of the McCall MacBain Scholarship application or on the institution’s website.
Canadian applicants who graduated in 2023 or earlier apply directly without endorsement.
International applicants  must seek endorsement if they are current students or recent graduates.
International applicants who graduated in 2023 or earlier from a Canadian university or a  participating university abroad  apply directly without endorsement.  However, international applicants who did not attend a Canadian or  participating university abroad  must seek endorsement from their university, regardless of the year of graduation.
International applicants from Canadian universities or  participating universities abroad  do not need to contact their university to request the endorsement unless instructed, either at the start of the McCall MacBain Scholarship application or on the institution’s own website or other communication channels.
Who is my university representative?
The university representative is typically a senior member of the administration (President, Vice-Chancellor, Rector, or Provost). At universities in the United States, a fellowship advisor may submit the endorsement.
Can I choose to be endorsed or apply directly?
No. If a university endorsement is required in your situation, your application will not be considered without it.
Is there a fee to apply for this scholarship?
You will need to apply separately to compatible programs at McGill University. Visit the McGill Graduate Applicants website or the relevant professional program website ( BCL/JD , DMD , MDCM ) to learn how to apply. The McGill program application fee is not covered by this scholarship.
When should I apply to McGill University?
In most cases, you can start applying to your intended McGill programs in mid-September. Application opening dates and deadlines vary by program, so it is important to verify your program-specific deadlines and submit your applications on time.
Visit the McGill Graduate Applicants website for more information on the university admissions process, including application deadlines.
Reference Forms
What are the reference form requirements?
As part of your scholarship application, you will need to ask two people to submit reference forms. These reference forms must be completed by your reference writers before the application deadline:
One academic reference from someone who knows your academic work
One community reference from someone who knows your community involvement
Please ask people who know you well. A good reference provides concrete examples to help us better understand you, how you meet the selection criteria , current areas of improvement, and how your proposed studies might benefit your continued growth.
How are references submitted?
Once you have started your application, you can request references immediately. There is no need to wait until you have completed your application. Through the online application, you will be able to provide contact details for your reference writers. Once you have added their information and clicked &#8220;Send to Recommender,&#8221; your reference writers will receive an email from [email&#160;protected] . The email contains a unique link to access the reference form in our online system.​​​
It is your responsibility to ensure that your reference completes the online form by the application deadline; otherwise, your application will be considered incomplete. If you submit your application early, you may check the status of your references on the Status Page.
References may not be submitted via email, mail, or any other means. References are only received via the online form.
When are references due?
The online reference forms must be submitted by your reference writers by the application deadline.
Whom can I ask to submit an academic reference?
The academic reference form may be completed by someone who knows your academic work. It is most often completed by a professor.
Please ask someone who knows you well. A good reference provides concrete examples to help us better understand you, how you meet the  selection criteria , current areas of improvement, and how your proposed studies might benefit your continued growth.
Whom can I ask to submit a community reference?
You can ask someone who knows your community involvement to fill out the community reference form. Examples include, but are not limited to, a representative of an organization where you have volunteered, an employer, a university staff member who knows your campus involvement, a community mentor, or a sports coach.
Please ask someone who knows you well. A good reference provides concrete examples to help us better understand you, how you meet the  selection criteria , current areas of improvement, and how your proposed studies might benefit your continued growth.
I am applying for a management program. Do I need an academic reference?
You can submit a professional reference instead of an academic reference. In the application, you can direct the academic reference request to someone who can attest to your professional qualifications.
Note: For the Master of Management in Analytics (MMA), you will need to submit an academic reference.
Can I ask the same people to provide references for the McCall MacBain Scholarship and for my McGill program application?
Yes, you are welcome to ask the same people to provide references for the McCall MacBain Scholarship and for your McGill program application.
Can I ask a family member, fellow student, or friend to submit a reference form?
Please do not ask family members, fellow students, or friends to submit the reference form.
My reference writer has not received the reference request email. What do I do?
Please ensure you entered the email correctly, and ask your reference writer to check their junk mail folder.
To change an active request, click on the request and press the Exclude button. You can then send a new request.
Transcripts and Test Scores
I will apply to a McGill program that requires a standardized test (e.g. DAT, GMAT, GRE, LSAT, MCAT). Do I need my standardized test score to apply?
It is not necessary to submit standardized test scores as part of the McCall MacBain Scholarship application. If you already have a strong test score, you may include it to help demonstrate your academic qualifications.
You may need to submit your score to McGill University at a later date as part of your program application. Ensure you know your intended program’s admission requirements, and contact the relevant McGill program for specific questions.
Do I need to provide English proof of proficiency?
Please read McGill&#8217;s English language proficiency requirements .
If it is required in your situation, you will need to provide it to McGill University when you apply for programs of interest. The test score is not required when you submit your scholarship application, given the early scholarship application deadline.
Do I need to send an official transcript?
You will need to upload an unofficial transcript as part of your online scholarship application. Unofficial transcripts are ones that are for student use, and they are typically available on your university’s student portal.
What if my transcript is not in English or French?
Transcripts in languages other than English or French must be accompanied by a translation. Submit both the transcript and its translation as one PDF. You can use free online tools to merge files.
After Applying
After I apply, when will I be notified about regional interviews?
Shortlisted candidates will be notified approximately two weeks prior to the date of interviews for their region. See the regional interview dates.
May I defer my McCall MacBain Scholarship offer?
No, McCall MacBain Scholarship offers cannot be deferred.
If I am selected as a McCall MacBain Scholar, may I study part-time?
No, McCall MacBain Scholarship offers are conditional upon full-time enrolment in an eligible program at McGill University. It is not possible to accept the scholarship for part-time enrolment.
What are the expectations of McCall MacBain Scholars after they arrive on campus?
McCall MacBain Scholars are expected to maintain satisfactory academic standing, engage in meaningful service, and participate fully in the scholarship program. Scholars renew their scholarships annually.
Do McCall MacBain Scholars receive summer funding?
It depends. Scholars receive a living stipend of $2,300 per month during academic terms. While some scholars remain enrolled full-time during the summer, others pursue full-time work, a combination of paid work and research, entrepreneurial pursuits, or other meaningful activities. Scholars not enrolled full-time may apply for up to $5,000 to partially fund meaningful research or impact work.
What is the McCall MacBain Finalist Award?
Candidates who participate in final interviews, but are not selected as McCall MacBain Scholars, are eligible to receive a one-time Finalist Award. The Finalist Award for Canadian applicants is $10,000, and the Finalist Award for international applicants is $20,000 (reflecting the higher cost of tuition). The Finalist Award for international applicants is renewable for up to two years.
The Finalist Award may be used for full-time enrolment in an eligible master’s or second-entry professional undergraduate program offered by McGill University on its downtown, MacDonald, or Outaouais campuses. For the 2026 application period, recipients will need to enrol for Summer / Fall 2027.
What is the McCall MacBain Regional Award?
Candidates who distinguish themselves at regional interviews, but are not invited to final interviews, are eligible to receive a one-time Regional Award. The Regional Award for Canadian applicants is $5,000, and the Regional Award for international applicants is $10,000 (reflecting the higher cost of tuition).
The Regional Award may be used for full-time enrolment in an eligible master’s or second-entry professional undergraduate program offered by any public university in Canada. For the 2026 application period, recipients will need to enrol for Summer / Fall 2027.
Can I apply only for the Regional Award?
No. You should apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarships if you are interested in eligible programs at McGill University.
Eligible Programs
Is my Qualifying Year an eligible program?
Qualifying Year studies are not covered by this scholarship. However, you can apply around the start of your Qualifying Year. If selected as a McCall MacBain Scholar, you would join the cohort as you enter your intended master’s program.
Is Clinical Psychology an eligible program?
Eligible psychology-related programs include:
MA, Counselling Psychology
MA, School/Applied Child Psychology
MScA, Couple and Family Therapy
MSc, Psychiatry (Mental Health)
MSc, Psychology: Experimental Track
MSc, Neuroscience
McGill University does not offer a master’s program in Clinical Psychology. Students interested in the PhD in Clinical Psychology are encouraged to consult the department&#8217;s guide to funding .
What are the McCall MacBain Scholarships?
The McCall MacBain Scholarships enable students to pursue a fully funded master’s or professional degree at McGill University, while connecting with mentors and participating in an intensive leadership development program.
What does the scholarship cover?
The McCall MacBain Scholarship covers tuition and fees for an eligible master&#8217;s program or a second-entry professional undergraduate program at McGill as well as a living stipend of $2,000 CAD per month during academic terms. Scholars also participate in an intensive leadership development program, connect with mentors and advisors, and participate in a breadth of workshops and talks.
Which graduate programs are covered under this scholarship?
The McCall MacBain Scholarship covers over 150 different master’s programs and 3 second-entry professional undergraduate programs at McGill’s downtown and Macdonald campuses. The program must be pursued full-time and consist of 45 credits or more. See this overview of eligible programs .
For questions about a specific program, please email [email&#160;protected] .
McCall MacBain Scholarship applicants must apply separately and be admitted to their desired graduate program(s) at McGill. Please visit the McGill Graduate Admissions website to review program requirements.
Who is eligible to apply?
For 2023 admission: Applicants must meet the minimum degree and language requirements for McGill master’s program admission. You must have a bachelor’s degree or its recognized equivalent, or be on track to complete it by August 2023.
Is there an age requirement?
No.
Is there a grade requirement?
Applicants must meet the GPA (and other) requirements of their desired program at McGill.
You must apply separately and be admitted to an eligible graduate program at McGill University. You should review your desired program’s admission requirements, including but not limited to GPA, well in advance.
Keep in mind that one of the five selection criteria for the scholarships is &#8220;academic strength and intellectual curiosity&#8221;. We will look for evidence of this throughout your application, including transcripts.
The measure of academic strength is not limited to a narrow focus on GPA. While GPA is important, we are interested in the fit with your proposed graduate program, as well as the breadth and difficulty of the courses taken alongside your other commitments. Of course, we recognize that some programs offer more opportunity for breadth than others.
Can I apply if I am a part-time student?
Yes, but you must apply for a full-time eligible graduate program at McGill. Award recipients must pursue their studies full-time.
Can I apply if I am currently enrolled in a graduate program?
You may apply if you are currently enrolled in a graduate program, but you intend to start a new eligible graduate program in the following year.
This scholarship is for students who intend to start an eligible graduate program in the following year.
Can I apply for the scholarship if I already have a graduate degree?
Yes. You will have to be applying to McGill for an eligible graduate program that starts the following year. Like all applicants, you will be asked to show in your application how the proposed program will help lay the foundation for your long-term goals and aspirations.
Can I apply for the scholarship before I have applied for my eligible graduate program at McGill?
Absolutely. Most people will apply for the scholarship before they apply to a graduate program at McGill. It will be necessary to apply by the program&#8217;s deadline, or in December 2022 at the latest if you are selected for final interviews.
Can I re-apply if I applied last year?
In most cases, yes, assuming you still meet the eligibility criteria and you update and resubmit your application.
There are two exceptions:
Candidates who accepted a Regional Award in a previous year;
Candidates who accepted an invitation to attend final interviews in a previous year.
These candidates may not re-apply in future years. Other candidates, including those who declined a Regional Award in a previous year, may re-apply.
Should I apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarship in addition to programs such as the Rhodes or the Canada Graduate Scholarship?
Yes! You should consider all of the opportunities available to you.
How can I apply for the McCall MacBain Scholarship?
Please attend an information session and start your 2023 application here .
Is there a nomination process?
Current students and recent graduates (those who graduated in 2021 or 2022) must be endorsed by their university.
Canadian universities, American universities, and the partner universities listed on our Campus Contacts page are invited to endorse multiple candidates for this scholarship. The timing and process of the endorsement depends on the university. Please start your application early to see personalized information about the endorsement process for your school. The application will open on June 1st for September 2023 admission.
You may be required to seek an endorsement from a fellowship advisor or a senior member of the university administration. If so, you will need to enter a university contact and submit an online endorsement request when applying. Guidance will be available in the online application.
Alumni who graduated in 2020 or earlier do not require a university endorsement.
Who is my university representative?
The university representative is typically a senior member of the administration (President, Provost, Vice-Provost). At American universities, a fellowship advisor may submit the endorsement. Please email [email&#160;protected] if you have questions regarding the representative at a specific university.
Can I choose to be endorsed or apply directly?
No. Current students and recent graduates (those who graduated in 2021 or 2022) must be endorsed by their university.
Alumni who graduated in 2020 or earlier do not require a university endorsement.
If I am selected as a McCall MacBain Scholar, may I accept additional funding from other sources?
Yes, McCall MacBain Scholars may accept up to $10,000 per year in additional merit-based funding. There is no limit to additional funding McCall MacBain Scholars may accept based on financial need.

MSc, Psychology
MA, Counselling Psychology
MA, School/Applied Child Psychology
MScA, Couple and Family Therapy
MSc, Psychiatry (Mental Health)
MSc, Integrated Program in Neuroscience
McGill University does not offer a master’s program in Clinical Psychology. Students interested in the PhD in Clinical Psychology are encouraged to consult the department&#8217;s guide to funding .

We&#8217;re looking for:
Exceptional character

Thirty Students Earn Canada’s Largest Leadership-Based Scholarships for Master’s Studies
Aspiring leaders in AI, bioengineering, cosmology, rehabilitation, robotics, and urban planning are among the newly selected McCall MacBain Scholars at McGill
Nineteen Canadians and 11 international students will receive Canada’s largest leadership-based scholarship for master’s and professional studies, forming the sixth cohort of McCall MacBain Scholars at McGill. Established with a historic $200-million gift in 2019, the scholarship covers tuition and fees, living expenses, mentorship, coaching, and a leadership program.
Applicants this year came from more than 2,300 universities worldwide. Of those, 280 advanced to first-round interviews held in October and November 2025. Then, 91 finalists were invited to attend interviews in Montreal in March.
The international students hail from 11 different countries, including first-time representation from Jamaica, Japan, Jordan, and Peru. The Canadian students come from 17 cities and towns in eight provinces. See the full list of scholars  here .

2026 McCall MacBain Scholarship Finalists and Regional Award Recipients
JUMP TO FINALISTS / REGIONAL AWARD RECIPIENTS
In March, nearly a hundred Canadian and international students will arrive in Montreal to participate in final interviews for the McCall MacBain Scholarships at McGill University.
Established with a landmark $200-million gift in 2019, the McCall MacBain Scholarships at McGill are Canada’s largest leadership-based scholarships for master’s and professional studies. Applicants came from more than 2,300 universities worldwide, and 280 advanced to a first round of interviews in October and November. Of these interviewed candidates, 66 earned a regional award, and 91 were selected as finalists.
The 38 international finalists will arrive from across Europe, Africa, Asia, and the Americas, with some travelling more than 10,000 kilometres to attend interviews in person. Their travel costs are covered, and for most, this marks their first visit to Canada and Quebec. The newly represented countries where finalists completed their studies include Barbados, Hungary, Jamaica, Japan, Jordan, Kyrgyzstan, and Sri Lanka.
The 53 Canadian finalists include students from 31 different cities, from Sydney, NS, to Yellowknife, NWT.
“We aim to support emerging leaders who demonstrate integrity, kindness, curiosity, and courage,” said Dr. Marcy McCall MacBain, Chair of the McCall MacBain Scholarships at McGill. “These students have already shown remarkable promise in their communities, and we’re grateful to the universities that encouraged and supported them in pursuing this opportunity.”
“We are also deeply thankful to the more than 300 leaders who lend their time to help us select these exceptional students and graduates,” added Natasha Sawh, CEO of the McCall MacBain Scholarships at McGill. “The interviews provide candidates with a space for meaningful reflection, and we hope they leave with a stronger sense of their leadership potential and the impact they can have in strengthening communities.”
