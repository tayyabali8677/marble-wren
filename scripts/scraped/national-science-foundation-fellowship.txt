Organization(s)
U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)
The purpose of the U.S. National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP) is to help ensure the quality, vitality, and strength of the scientific and engineering workforce of the United States.
2027 NSF GRFP Notice of Funding Opportunity
Applicant deadlines
October 16, 2026 by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time
All reference letters due.
October 19, 2026 by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time
Life Sciences
October 20, 2026 by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time
Computer and Information Science and Engineering; Materials Research; Psychology; Social Sciences
October 22, 2026 by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time
Engineering
October 23, 2026 by 8:00 p.m. Eastern time
Chemistry; Geosciences; Mathematical Sciences; Physics and Astronomy
November 15, 2026
You can withdraw your application anytime. However, this is the last day to withdraw your application and not have it count towards the number of times you apply to NSF GRFP.
Program goals
Support outstanding graduate students pursuing research-based degrees
Increase participation in science, technology, education, and mathematics (STEM)
Build the next generation of scientific leaders
Fellowship benefits
The fellowship offers:
Five-year award with three years of financial support:
$37,000 stipend plus $16,000 cost of education (COE) paid to the institution for each year of funding support.
COE payment covers all tuition and mandatory fees (no cost to student).
$53,000 each year for three years over a five-year period.
No post-graduate study service requirement.
Access to supplemental funding to sustain research while on medical leave.

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)
The purpose of the U.S. National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP) is to help ensure the quality, vitality, and strength of the scientific and engineering workforce of the United States.

Program goals
Support outstanding graduate students pursuing research-based degrees
Increase participation in science, technology, education, and mathematics (STEM)
Build the next generation of scientific leaders
Fellowship benefits
The fellowship offers:
Five-year award with three years of financial support:
$37,000 stipend plus $16,000 cost of education (COE) paid to the institution for each year of funding support.
COE payment covers all tuition and mandatory fees (no cost to student).
$53,000 each year for three years over a five-year period.
No post-graduate study service requirement.
Access to supplemental funding to sustain research while on medical leave.
