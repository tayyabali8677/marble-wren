Scholarships
The Vrije Universiteit Brussels (VUB) participates in several scholarship programmes which offer grants at different levels of study, from Bachelor to Academic staff, and in various regions, for long and short stays. You can find an overview of the different scholarship programmes which are currently available below.

After your enrolment
Great! You've finished your enrolment at VUB. Here's everything you need to make a great start.
Starting guide
An overview of the available student services, useful phone numbers, campus facilities and more!
Orientation days
We expect you on campus during the Orientation days for info sessions, campus tours, welcome drinks and fun activities!
Ready for take-off?
Check this &#039;Ready for take-off&#039; module created by Study Guidance, to prepare for the start of the academic year.
Are you ready to start?
Step 1 - Activate your VUB account
Did you sign the online study contract? Then you will receive instructions via e-mail (within the hour) to activate your VUB account . This VUB e-mail address and password gives you access to all VUB platforms and portals .
Step 2 - Certificates / transportation passes
Within 48 hours you will also receive your enrolment certificate via e-mail (pdf) and your student card , along with a paper version of your enrolment certificate, will be sent to your postal address. Official address not in Belgium? Then you can pick your student card up on campus .
Step 3 - Register your courses
Is your VUB account activated? Then you can register your courses online, starting mid August. After they have been registered, they will appear in your Canvas dashboard. Attention: not all students must register their own courses , in some cases this is done automatically.
Step 4 - Pay your tuition fees
The tuition fee invoice is sent at the end of September at the earliest, to your VUB e-mail address. Don't worry, you can start your programme even if you haven't received or paid the invoice yet.
Step 5 - Come to the orientation days
We expect you in the week of 14 September at your faculty's orientation. On the schedule are info sessions, a campus tour, perhaps some mandatory language tests, calibration tests and a welcome drink. Enrol here for the Orientation days (only at the start of the 1st semester).

Entry exam and positioning tests
“Will I be able to do that?” A question that many prospective lose their sleep over. The good news is that no study is too difficult if you are motivated to give it your all. However, certain academic programmes require prior knowledge of maths and science to a given level. VUB has developed a series of informal prior knowledge tests and compulsory positioning tests to give you an idea of your ability.
Events for prospective students
Prepared at the start
Positioning tests
You must take a positioning test before enrolling for a course with demanding maths and science subjects. To give you an idea of what to expect.
Test your prior knowledge
Wondering how much you managed to remember from all those maths and science lessons at secondary school? Take an informal prior knowledge test.
Medicine Entrance Exam
The Medicine Entrance Exam is no laughing matter. VUB can help you via the ikworddokter.be initiative.
