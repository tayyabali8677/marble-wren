## [Biblical Scholar Martti Nissinen Named Academician of Science](https://www.ias.edu/news/biblical-scholar-martti-nissinen-named-academician-science)

October 20, 2026

[Martti Nissinen](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/martti-nissinen), Member (2008–09, 2016) and Visitor (2011) in the [School of Historical Studies](https://www.ias.edu/hs), has received the honorary title of [Academician of Science](https://www.aka.fi/en/about-the-rcf/about-us/academicians-of-science/), the highest honor that can be bestowed on any individual scholar in Finland. His work has "significantly enhanced our understanding of how biblical texts were formed," placing them within the broader context of the ancient Near East.

## [Sabine Schmidtke Receives Honorary Doctorate from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://www.ias.edu/news/sabine-schmidtke-receives-honorary-doctorate-hebrew-university-jerusalem)

## [Charles Simonyi Awarded John von Neumann Professorship](https://www.ias.edu/news/charles-simonyi-awarded-john-von-neumann-professorship)

October 15, 2026

Charles Simonyi, Chair Emeritus of the Institute’s [Board of Trustees](https://www.ias.edu/about/trustees), has been awarded a [John von Neumann Professorship](https://www.bme.hu/en/john-von-neumann-professors) by the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME). Simonyi's receipt of this honor carries special resonance at IAS, where [John von Neumann](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/von-neumann) served as one of the founding Professors in the [School of Mathematics](https://www.ias.edu/math).
