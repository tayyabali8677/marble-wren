## [IAS Scholar Reveals Universal Rule of Black Hole "Burps"](https://www.ias.edu/news/ias-scholar-reveals-universal-rule-black-hole-burps)

September 17, 2026

An international collaboration co-led by [Andrew Mummery](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/andrew-mummery), Martin A. and Helen Chooljian Member (2025–30) in the [School of Natural Sciences](https://www.ias.edu/sns), has uncovered a universal rule governing one of the most powerful astronomical events in the universe. The team has shown that black holes—whether they are “stellar-mass” objects ten times the mass of our sun or supermassive giants millions of times heavier—fire powerful jets of material at the same juncture in their feeding cycles.

## [Alondra Nelson Receives AI2050 Senior Fellowship from Schmidt Sciences](https://www.ias.edu/news/alondra-nelson-receives-ai2050-senior-fellowship-schmidt-sciences)

October 02, 2026

[Alondra Nelson](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/nelson), Harold F. Linder Professor in the[School of Social Science](https://www.ias.edu/sss), has been named by Schmidt Sciences as a[2026 AI2050 Senior Fellow](https://www.schmidtsciences.org/ai2050-fellows-2026-announcement/).
