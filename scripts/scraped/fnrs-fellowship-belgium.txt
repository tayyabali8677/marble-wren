Audacious Medical Grants (AMG)
The Audacious Medical Grant (AMG) is a funding tool to support medical or biomedical research projects that are original and daring but which do not find support in traditional funding channels due to a lack of preliminary data, the absence of previous work or an unusual methodology. The term “bold” means that there is a significant risk that the project will produce little or no tangible scientific results because of its innovative nature or its complexity.
AMG-PEDIATRICS
AMG-Pediatrics is a funding instrument supported by a private sponsor for which the FNRS uses the expertise of the SVS-4 Scientific Commission to carry out the evaluation of the submitted projects. The accumulation between the classic FNRS instruments (PDR, CDR, MIS, etc.) and the AMG-Pediatrics is authorized for filing and granting.
The AMG-Pediatrics call covers all pathologies affecting pediatric or fetal patients/subjects, including infectious diseases and vaccinology, immune deficiencies, cardiovascular, respiratory and metabolic diseases, congenital, developmental, neurological and psychiatric disorders, cancers, etc.
Funding can be requested for a maximum of 24 months.
The maximum funding that can be requested is 120,000 euros.
AMG-NEURO
The AMG-NEURO is intended for a single and eligible promoter of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation and concerns neuroscience . The submitted project must allow a better understanding of the nervous system and its pathologies and/or the development of new therapeutic avenues and/or an improvement in the prevention or detection of nervous system pathologies.
The financing duration is 24 months maximum.
The financing amount is 120,000 euros maximum.
AMG-ONCO
The AMG-ONCO call is intended for a single and eligible promoter of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation and concerns all fields of the oncology . The submitted project must allow: a better understanding of the mechanisms leading to the development of cancers and/or the development of new therapeutic avenues and/or an improvement in the prevention or detection of cancers.
The financing duration is 24 months maximum.
The financing amount is 120,000 euros maximum.
AMG-METABOLICS
The objective of the Audacious Medical Grant – Metabolism & Microbiome (AMG-METABOLICS) call is to support research projects at the interface between metabolism and the microbiome that are original and bold in nature, but which struggle to obtain support through traditional funding channels due to a lack of preliminary data, the absence of prior work, or the use of unconventional methodologies.
A research project is led by a Principal Investigator affiliated with a university of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation (FWB).
To be eligible as a Principal Investigator, applicants must:
hold a permanent F.R.S.-FNRS position as:
Research Associate (CQ) ;
Senior Research Associate (MR) ; or
Research Director (DR) ;
hold a Scientific Impulse Mandate – Ulysses (MISU) ;
hold a FED-tWIN mandate ;
have a permanent or probationary appointment to an academic or scientific position within their institution.
The funding period is two years .
Applicants may request a maximum total budget of €120,000 (Personnel, Operating Costs, and Equipment).

Audacious Sustainability Grant (ASG)
The FNRS is launching a new funding instrument specifically focused on sustainability issues: the Audacious Sustainability Grant (ASG).
ASG-DECARBONATION
This first ASG is dedicated to decarbonization challenges.
The ASG-DECARBONIZATION call is part of the field of decarbonization and mitigation of carbon emissions, with the central ambition to promote the development of concrete solutions to prevent or reduce climate change, as well as limit its impacts . It should therefore not improve our understanding of climate change and its mechanisms, but rather support research based on exact and natural sciences (SEN) and oriented towards operational responses. The research projects should contribute, in a tangible way, to the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions or their sustainable management. It is therefore intended to support research projects developing innovative mitigation approaches, whether it concerns carbon capture, reduction or transformation technologies, the development of green technologies with high disruptive potential, or even effective strategies for managing and strengthening large natural ecosystems.
The objective of this call is to support research projects addressing sustainable issues linked to decarbonization with an original and audacious character, but which do not find support in traditional funding circuits due to a lack of preliminary data, the absence of previous work or an unusual methodology. The ASG is made possible thanks to private patronage.

FRArt
THE Art Research Fund (FRArt) allows the financing of art research projects carried out by artist-researchers on an individual or collective basis, outside of any doctorate and financing of artistic production. These projects are validated by one or more Higher Schools of Arts (ESA).
Financing instrument:
FRArt research project (PDR-FRArt)
As part of a PDR-FRArt, eligible costs are of 3 types: personnel, operation and equipment.
The PDR-FRArt lasts 9 to 24 months and allows you to request funding of up to €50,000.-.
Tax status of the PDR-FRArt grant: the FRArt grant may benefit from a tax exemption given that the project that will be awarded will have been evaluated on the basis of merit and rewards exceptional merit. This tax exemption is the responsibility of the authors.

The WEL Research Institute
THE WEL Research Institute supports strategic research of excellence within its departments with a view to promoting disruptive innovations for impact in the fields of health and sustainable transition.
It has two departments:
WELBIO (Walloon Excellence in Life Sciences and Biotechnology) which supports strategic fundamental research of excellence in the life sciences with a view to promoting discoveries towards industrial applications in the field of health. See the 37 current WELBIO programs ;
- WELBIO INVESTIGATOR 2025 PROGRAM Regulations
- WELBIO INVESTIGATOR 2025 PROGRAM Mini-guide
- Presentation of the WELBIO information webinar (September 22, 2023) .
WEL-T (Walloon Excellence in Technology), which supports strategic research of excellence in engineering, chemistry and physics with a view to promoting disruptive innovations for industrial applications aimed at sustainable transition. See the 14 current WEL-T programs.
Rules and regulations WEL-T Investigator Programme 2026
Mini-guide WEL-T Investigator Programme 2026
The WEL Research Institute’s missions are:
Stimulate the international presence of Wallonia in the areas of the WEL Research Institute;
Develop an autonomous inter-university structure which creates a stable and stimulating environment for strategic fundamental research of excellence, non-oriented and pre-translational, in the fields of the WEL Research Institute in Wallonia;
Conduct strategic fundamental research activities of excellence, non-directed and pre-translational;
Develop rigorous selection and evaluation processes based on excellence;
Actively contribute to the training of WEL Research Institute researchers;
Develop technological transfer of results from research funded by the WEL Research Institute, primarily in Wallonia;
Promote collaboration with industry, ensuring the transfer of technology and knowledge as a priority to the industry established in Wallonia;
Promote industrial activities in Wallonia in the areas of the WEL Research Institute.
The Fund for Strategic Fundamental Research (FRFS)
THE Fund for Strategic Fundamental Research (FRFS) , a specialized FNRS fund, finances excellent fundamental research in these strategic areas. Calls are managed by the FRFS.

At the initiative of the late Mr. Gustave Christin Wernaers, the Wernaers International Fund for research and the dissemination of knowledge was created within the Scientific Research Fund - FNRS.
The Wernaers Fund supports actions to promote research and disseminate scientific knowledge.
This Fund has 3 instruments to achieve its objectives
A specialization scholarship in Scientific Communication and Popularization (every 2 years => no grant in 2026) - Regulations .
Wernaers Prize for popularizing science - Regulations , Form .
Subsidies to media contributing to the development of interest in scientific culture - Regulations , Form .
Fund Advisory Board
The Advisory Council constitutes an advisory body relating to the selection of all actions to be supported by the Wernaers Fund.
It studies the application files submitted and makes reasoned proposals to the Fund Management Committee.
Composition of the Council available here .
