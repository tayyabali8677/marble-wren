Archives, Documentation, and Information
Archives, Documentation, and Information
The Archives, Documentation, and Information (ADI) service is part of the Executive Board Support Division. It is responsible for the management, development, and implementation of document and archive management resources and mechanisms—including the Filing Service—and for the operation of the Science and Technology Archive, particularly in terms of the management, inventory, preservation, and availability of the documentary and bibliographic heritage of the Foundation for Science and Technology. It is also responsible for managing three services related to the management of access to scientific information, namely scientific journals, directories, and repositories.
Learn about the services available:
Science and Technology Archive (ACT)
ACT's mission is to process, preserve, and disseminate the archival heritage held by FCT, as well as other material of scientific and historical interest, thereby contributing to the preservation of the history and memory of science and technology in Portugal. It reserves and manages documentation of undeniable interest and historical quality, reflecting Portuguese cultural and scientific activity, mainly from the second half of the 20th century, largely in the form of thousands of files supporting Studentships, institutions, research projects, among others. A documentary collection representative of the history of institutions, scientific policies, and the organization of science, with interest for all areas of knowledge. Due to the activity that the FCT develops and its responsibility for ensuring the preservation of scientific documentary heritage, the archive has also been integrating some personal collections of renowned personalities linked to scientific activity.
Science and Technology Library
The FCT Library collects and provides access to an important set of information resources, including national and international monographs and journals specializing in science and technology management and policy, institutional history, and subjects such as the history of science and technology. In addition to the collection built up by FCT itself, it includes editions of the now defunct INIC and JNICT, as well as collections from personal estates.
INDEXAR – Directory of Digital Repositories
INDEXAR was created in response to the need for a platform capable of identifying and providing an integrated online reference for accessing digital scientific repositories and journals in the field of science and culture. A directory that allows for comprehensive, credible information search and identification, enhancing the visibility and dissemination of the information recorded.
SciELO – Online digital library
FCT is the SciELO National Unit, responsible for coordinating and operating SciELO Portugal, a service that provides information from different scientific fields, with the aim of promoting Portuguese scientific journals and disseminating high-quality national scientific output worldwide.
Latindex – Network for the dissemination of scientific publications
Since 1999, FCT has been the Portuguese institution coordinating national participation in Latindex, a network for the dissemination of scientific publications. The aim is to disseminate, make accessible, and improve the quality of scientific research, technical-professional, and scientific and cultural journals published in Latin America, the Caribbean, Spain, and Portugal.

2026 Call for PhD Studentships – Regular Line of Application
Call
Results
31.07.2026
FCT announced the provisional results of the Call for PhD Studentships 2026, which foresees the allocation of 1,600 studentships, 600 of which in a non-academic environment.
Applicants may use their right to dispute the decision during the preliminary hearing phase, via the myFCT portal, until 11:59 pm of august 14, 2026.
23.07.2026
Since it was published with inaccuracies, the list with the composition of the 33 Evaluation Panels for the Regular Line of the 2026 Call for PhD Studentships, originally published on June 16, is republished. The constitution of the evaluation panels may be consulted on this page under Call Documents.
21.04.2026
As applicants are aware, the national platform for the recognition of higher education degrees awarded by foreign institutions was unavailable for around 15 days during this call. In response to this situation, and to ensure equal treatment for all applicants and consistency in the rules, FCT and DGES undertook joint efforts to reduce its impact. For this purpose, and after the deadline for candidates to submit all necessary documentation, FCT shared with DGES the list of all those who had applied for this call, so that, based on the requests registered on the platform managed by DGES, it could be determined which applicants, having submitted their request by the 30th of March 2026, had not, by that same date, obtained the relevant degree recognition, so that DGES could return any recognitions that had been issued, even if after the 30th of March 2026 (one day before the final deadline for submitting applications). This approach ensures that candidates should not be held responsible for submitting a document issued late, provided that they, themselves, had also acted diligently in requesting the degree recognition they might require. In this regard, it is worth noting that, under the applicable law, DGES has at least 30 days to process recognition requests; although, it is common knowledge that, in recognitions dependent on DGES only, this period is usually shorter.
For candidates who submitted their recognition degree request to DGES on time, the relevant information will be added automatically by FCT to their application in the coming days, as soon as it is made available by DGES (now Institute for Higher Education). No action is required from applicants.
Finally, it should be emphasised that this approach has been taken to address situations requiring support, given the expectations created by the diligent efforts of all relevant bodies and the candidates themselves. The deadlines for recognising foreign degrees are set by law and are widely known. The requirements applied by FCT in this call have remained the same for several years, as have, in general, the usual timing of the call, which takes place each year in March or, occasionally, between March and April.
13.04.2026
Please note that the 10-working-day deadline for Contracting Institutions to validate the applications ends on 17 April 2026.
09.03.2026
The DGES platform for the recognition of foreign degrees is operational again since the afternoon of 06/03/2026. Accordingly, and considering that the impediment ceased 25 days before the deadline for the submission of applications, it is hereby informed that all rules regarding the submission of proof of recognition of the conversion of degrees or diplomas awarded by foreign institutions, as stated in the Notice of the Call and in the Evaluation Guide, remain applicable.
04.03.2026
FCT is aware of the constraints of the DGES platform for requesting the recognition of foreign degrees. We are monitoring the situation as it develops.
Please note that these constraints do not prevent the submission of applications. We suggest to keep in touch with DGES.

PhD studentships aim to fund research activities leading to the award of the PhD degree.
Open All
Overview and objectives
To fulfil its aim of promoting a national public policy for advanced training with social relevance and impact, the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) opens a Call for granting PhD studentships complying with the provisions of the Regulamento de Bolsas de Investiga&ccedil;&atilde;o da FCT / FCT Regulation for Studentships and Fellowships (RBI) and the Estatuto do Bolseiro de Investiga&ccedil;&atilde;o /Research Fellowship Holder Statute (EBI), according to their current versions.
The regular line of application includes all scientific areas and is aimed at research to be carried out in scientific and academic institutions . A specific line of application targeted at research work plans for the most part carried out in one or more non-academic institutions is also available in this call ( for further details, please go to its specific page ).
Who can apply
PhD studentships in the regular line of application are aimed at applicants that are enrolled or that comply with the requirements to enrol in a PhD program to obtain a doctoral degree. They are suitable for candidates who want to develop PhD research activities in any academic institution, national or international, including public and private higher education institutions, R&D units, Associated Laboratories, as well as other private non-profit institutions whose main purpose is R&D activities.
How to apply
The Call is open from March 2nd to 5:00 pm (Lisbon time) March 31st, 2026 .
Applications, and the supporting documents listed in the RBI and in this Notice of the Call, must be submitted online using exclusively the available application form at https://myfct.fct.pt , selecting the regular line of application and clicking on &ldquo;start application&rdquo;. Applications or any additional information submitted by any other means will not be accepted.
Portuguese citizens, citizens of EU member states, citizens from third-party states, stateless citizens and citizens beneficiaries of political refugee status are eligible to apply.
To apply for a PhD studentship, it is mandatory:
Not to have benefited from any PhD studentship or PhD in Industry studentship directly funded by FCT, regardless of its duration.
Not to hold a doctoral degree.
To live in Portugal on a permanent and habitual basis, if applying to a both in Portugal and abroad or entirely abroad studentship. This requirement applies to both applicants with Portuguese and foreign citizenship.
The remaining admissibility requirements are available in section 4 of the Notice of the Call.
Evaluation
Evaluation of applications is performed by evaluation panels composed by experts with recognized experience and scientific merit. All admissible applications must be graded from zero (0.000, minimum) to five (5.000, maximum) in each of the three evaluation criteria:
Criterion A &ndash; Merit of the Applicant
Criterion B &ndash; Merit of the Work Plan
Criterion C &ndash; Merit of the Hosting Conditions
The relative weights of three criteria are 30%, 40%, 30%, respectively.
The merit of the applicant assessment is based on two sub-criteria: academic career, with a weighting of 50%, and personal CV, also with a weighting of 50%.
The evaluation of the candidate's personal CV is carried out in an integrated way, based on a global view of the merit of their scientific and professional career.
The evaluation of the work plan and hosting conditions is based on the panel's qualitative assessment, duly substantiated.
Applications will be ranked according to the weighted average classification of the three criteria, and in the following order of precedence, for tiebreaker purposes: criterion B (Merit of the Work Plan), criterion A (Merit of the Applicant) and criterion C (Merit of the Hosting Conditions).
Applications evaluated with a final classification lower than three values (3,000) are not eligible for granting a studentship.
The procedures concerning the evaluation process are available in the Evaluation Guide. Other supporting documents for the application, namely the Application Guide, FAQ and Ethics Guide, will be made available by the opening day of the call.
Funding
Selected applicants will receive a monthly allowance, as well as other complementary support in accordance with the amounts set out by the RBI, such as tuition fees, support for additional training activities, presentation of work at scientific meetings, personal accident insurance and, when applicable, travel and relocation costs.
The studentships granted in this call will be financed by FCT using funds from the State Budget and, when eligible, funds from the European Social Fund (ESF), under the Program PESSOAS 2030 , in accordance with the established regulatory provisions.
Contracting Institutions
The contracting institution indicated by the candidate in the application form is responsible for contracting and managing the PhD studentship to be granted. Contracting institutions have a period of 10 working days to validate, through the myFCT platform, the applications that selected them, which will take place after the period for application submission and before the start of the evaluation. This deadline will be announced on this page, so regular checks are recommended.
Applications that are not validated by the Contracting Institution designated in the application will be excluded from this call; therefore, this step is crucial.
Applications for studentships fully Abroad continue to be contracted and managed by FCT.
On this page you will find the Guide for Contracting Institutions that aims to clarify the procedure for validating studentship applications.
Results Disclosure
Provisional results will be published up to 90 working days after the applications&rsquo; submission deadline and are expected to be available in the first week of August.
After the provisional results have been communicated to the applicants, those who have an unfavourable provisional decision may use their right to dispute it during the preliminary hearing phase, which takes place within 10 working days.
The final decision will be disclosed after the analysis of applicants&rsquo; arguments presented in the preliminary hearing. A claim may be filed against the final decision within 15 working days, or, alternatively, an appeal may be filed within 30 working days, both counting from the date of the respective notification.
Contacts
Information about the call should be requested via e-mail at info.bolsas@fct.pt . The email is available from the date the call opens and up to 72 hours before the end of the application deadline.
For questions related to the creation and management of CVs, please use CI&Ecirc;NCIAVITAE&acute;s helpdesk .
The communication channel dedicated exclusively to the interaction between contracting institutions and FCT, in the context of PhD studentship contracts, is the following email address bolsas.instituicoescontratantes@fct.pt .

21.04.2026
As applicants are aware, the national platform for the recognition of higher education degrees awarded by foreign institutions was unavailable for around 15 days during this call. In response to this situation, and to ensure equal treatment for all applicants and consistency in the rules, FCT and DGES undertook joint efforts to reduce its impact. For this purpose, and after the deadline for candidates to submit all necessary documentation, FCT shared with DGES the list of all those who had applied for this call, so that, based on the requests registered on the platform managed by DGES, it could be determined which applicants, having submitted their request by the 30th of March 2026, had not, by that same date, obtained the relevant degree recognition, so that DGES could return any recognitions that had been issued, even if after the 30th of March 2026 (one day before the final deadline for submitting applications). This approach ensures that candidates should not be held responsible for submitting a document issued late, provided that they, themselves, had also acted diligently in requesting the degree recognition they might require. In this regard, it is worth noting that, under the applicable law, DGES has at least 30 days to process recognition requests; although, it is common knowledge that, in recognitions dependent on DGES only, this period is usually shorter.
For candidates who submitted their recognition degree request to DGES on time, the relevant information will be added automatically by FCT to their application in the coming days, as soon as it is made available by DGES (now Institute for Higher Education). No action is required from applicants.
Finally, it should be emphasised that this approach has been taken to address situations requiring support, given the expectations created by the diligent efforts of all relevant bodies and the candidates themselves. The deadlines for recognising foreign degrees are set by law and are widely known. The requirements applied by FCT in this call have remained the same for several years, as have, in general, the usual timing of the call, which takes place each year in March or, occasionally, between March and April.

PhD studentships, to be awarded in the Specific Line of Application, aim to fund research activities carried out in close articulation with non-academic institutions, leading to the award of the PhD degree.

Overview and objectives
To fulfil its aim of promoting a national public policy for advanced training with social relevance and impact, the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) opens a Call for granting PhD studentships complying with the provisions of the Regulamento de Bolsas de Investiga&ccedil;&atilde;o da FCT / FCT Regulation for Studentships and Fellowships (RBI) and the Estatuto do Bolseiro de Investiga&ccedil;&atilde;o /Research Fellowship Holder Statute (EBI), according to their current versions.
In addition to the regular line of application ( for further details, please go to its specific page) , this call opens a specific line of application for PhD studentship in which work plans are partially carried out in one or more non-academic entities . FCT will thus continue to reinforce a closer relationship between doctoral research in academic and non-academic environments such as business companies, public, social, health, cultural institutions and organizations.
Candidates who wish to apply in the specific line in a non-academic environment can look for potential hosting opportunities for their research plans in the list of non-academic institutions available here , and interact directly with the contact point of each institution in order to prepare a work plan that can support their application. This list is not exhaustive, is in continuous updating, and the information provided is the sole responsibility of the respective entities. Therefore, the eligibility of each institutional context in the non-academic line of application should be verified. Applications that include other eligible entities that are not listed may also be submitted in this line.
Who can apply
PhD studentships in the specific line of application in a non-academic environment are aimed at applicants that are enrolled or that comply with the requirements to enrol in a PhD program to obtain a doctoral degree. They are suitable for candidates who want to develop PhD research activities mostly in a non-academic environment, ensuring at least 12 months (consecutive or interpolated) of the studentship in the same non-academic host institution with activity in Portugal. In addition to the research in a non-academic environment, it is mandatory that applicants have a national or foreign academic host institution, thus ensuring the desirable connection between the academic and non-academic environments.
In addition, PhD studentships in a non-academic environment will also be awarded within the scope of Non-Academic Chairs funded under the FCT-Tenure program, to all eligible candidates covered by this program, whose application is evaluated with a final grade equal to or higher than three points (3.000), as provided for in point 5.2 of the Call for Applications of the FCT Tenure &ndash; 1st Edition .
How to apply
The Call is open from March 2nd to 5:00 pm (Lisbon time) March 31st, 2026 .
Applications, and the supporting documents listed in the RBI and in this Notice of the Call, must be submitted online using exclusively the available application form at https://myfct.fct.pt , selecting the specific line of application in a non-academic environment and clicking on &ldquo;start application&rdquo;. Evaluation
Applications submitted in the specific line in a non-academic environment are evaluated by five evaluation panels, corresponding to the thematic areas of Engineering Sciences and Exact Sciences, Natural and Agricultural Sciences, Health and Life Sciences, Social Sciences and Humanities, which are composed by experts with recognized experience and scientific merit .

The evaluation of the candidate's personal CV is carried out in an integrated way, based on a global view of the merit of their scientific and professional career.
The evaluation of the work plan and hosting conditions is based on the panel's qualitative assessment, duly substantiated. In this line of application, the expected contribution of non-academic institutions to the successful execution of the work plan, as well as the relevance of the plan to the objectives and areas of activity of these institutions, is evaluated in sub-criterion C3 - Framing of the non-academic institution(s) in the context of the work plan, weighting 50% within the scope of criterion C - Merit of the Hosting Conditions.
Applications will be ranked according to the weighted average classification of the three criteria, and in the following order of precedence, for tiebreaker purposes: C (Merit of the Hosting Conditions), criterion B (Merit of the Work Plan), and criterion A (Merit of the Applicant).
