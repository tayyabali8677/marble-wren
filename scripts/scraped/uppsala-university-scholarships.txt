Apply to a Master’s programme at Uppsala University
Interested in studying for a Master’s degree at Uppsala University? Applications are made through the Swedish national portal University Admissions, where you can create an account, search for programmes, submit your documents and track your application status.
Next application period
The application for programmes beginning in autumn 2026 is now closed. The next application will open mid-October 2026 and close mid-January 2027 .
How to apply?
Applications are open from mid-October to mid-January. See our step-by-step guide to make sure your application is complete.
Entry requirements
Entry requirements include a Bachelor’s degree and excellent knowledge in English, as well as programme-specific requirements.
Supporting documents
You need to submit several supporting documents – both general and programme-specific – with your application.
Specific requirements
Find information about specific requirements for your country.
Local representatives
In some countries we have partners who can assist you with your application.
Useful information
Application rounds
International admission round
We strongly encourage all international students to apply in the international admission round, which runs from mid-October to mid-January.
Swedish admission round
If you are an EU/EEA citizen (or already have a residence permit in Sweden or the EU/EEA) and missed the international round, you may be able to apply for a few programmes or courses in the Swedish admission round. Apply through Antagning.se (website in Swedish). The process is the same as on University Admissions.
Please note that results are announced very close to the start of the semester. If you need a residence permit, you will not have time to obtain one, and finding housing in Uppsala on short notice is very difficult.
This also applies to applications for freestanding courses.
Late applications
Do not miss the deadlines! To ensure that your application is processed, you must apply, pay the application fee, and submit all required documents (including certified proof of citizenship) by the given deadlines.
See all deadlines at University Admissions .
Applications submitted after the deadline are considered late. These are only processed if there are still places available after all on-time applications have been reviewed. There is no guarantee that a late application will be considered.
For the Swedish admission round, students apply through Antagning.se . When the deadline passes, programmes and courses close for applications. Some may reopen later if places remain available.
Please note that if you submit a late application, you will not be eligible for scholarships.
We therefore strongly recommend submitting a complete and on-time application.
Contact us
Need help with your application? We recommend seeking guidance early to ensure you submit the correct documents.
Email us: study@uu.se
Contact the International Student Support team for questions about your application or entry requirements.
Meet an admissions officer:
Book an individual Zoom meeting to ask about application documents or general eligibility. New time slots are released every Wednesday for upcoming Mondays and Wednesdays.
Register for a meeting

Application guide
Applying to our Bachelor's or Master's programmes and applying for our scholarships are two separate application procedures.
Application closed
The scholarship application for studies starting in autumn 2026 is now closed. The application period was 16 January–2 February 2026, and late applications cannot be accepted. The next scholarship round will open in early 2027 for students applying to Uppsala University.
How to apply
Step 1: Apply for a Bachelor's or Master's programme
Before you can apply for Uppsala University’s scholarships, you must first apply for one of Uppsala University's international Bachelor's or Master's programmes as your first priority at universityadmissions.se.
Uppsala University's programme must be ranked as your first priority of choice. Read more about how to rank your selections.
Do not miss the deadline! If your application on universitydmissions.se is considered late, you will not be eligible for the Uppsala University scholarships. See deadlines .
Once you have submitted your application at universityadmissions.se, you will get a personal application number consisting of eight digits. Save the number as you will need it when applying for our scholarships.
Step 2: Check the eligibility criteria
Before you submit your application(s), be sure that you read the information about each scholarship thoroughly and that you understand the conditions.
You may apply for more than one scholarship, as long as you fulfil the eligibility criteria. However, it is only possible to be awarded one scholarship from Uppsala University. You specify all the scholarships that you are applying for, in one scholarship application form online in the next step.
Step 3: Submit an online scholarship application
You need to submit an online application form during the scholarship application period.
Link to the application will be published on this once the application is open.
Your application should include:
your personal details,
which scholarship programme(s) you are applying to,
the application number that you got at universityadmissions.se .
In the online scholarship application form, you do not need to submit any documents. However, you must write a motivational letter explaining why you should be awarded the scholarship. Your motivational letter should not exceed 350 words and should reflect the eligibility criteria of the scholarship programme.
There is no application fee when applying for Uppsala University's scholarships.
Do not miss the deadline
Make sure you submit your scholarship application on time. We do not accept late applications.
Please do not apply for the scholarship if you:
do not meet the entry requirements for admission to Uppsala University;
have not selected a programme at Uppsala University as your first choice in your application at universityadmissions.se ;
will not be able to secure the funding needed to cover your cost of living, as required by the Swedish Migration Agency , even if you are awarded a scholarship that covers the cost of tuition. See information about the cost of living for students in Sweden .
Selection Process
Uppsala University’s scholarships are for students who are liable to pay tuition fees. The selection is merit-based. The scholarships are aimed at academically talented students who show an interest in being in an educational environment. Information about your academic performance will be obtained from the supporting documents that you submit when applying for Master's or Bachelor's studies at universityadmissions.se. The written motivational letter filled in your online scholarship application is also taken into account in the selection process.
Financial need is not a factor we consider.
The scholarship selection process takes place at the same time as the programme admission selection process. In order to be admitted to a study programme and be awarded a scholarship, you must meet all the general and programme-specific entry requirements for the study programme, as well as all the eligibility criteria for the scholarship programme .
Applicants who submit incomplete applications or do not apply on time will not be considered for a scholarship at Uppsala University.

External funding opportunities
In addition to Uppsala University scholarships, fee-paying international students may be eligible for financial aid from external organisations. These scholarships have different application processes, deadlines, and criteria. Uppsala University does not manage them.
Programme-specific funding
There may be funding options available for those studying a specific programme, or for those who meet certain other criteria. Please see below to check if any of the options apply to you.
Rotary World Peace Fellowship
The Rotary World Peace Fellowship is open for students applying to the Master's Programme in Peace and Conflict Studies.
Learn more
SI Scholarship for Global Professionals
The Swedish Institute (SI), a government agency, has a number of scholarships open for students from countries outside the EU/EEA area. Depending on your nationality and field of study, the scholarships may apply to you. The SI scholarships cover both tuition fee and living expenses to the amount of 12,000 SEK per month for studies at Master's level. A travel grant is also issued to the recipient. Please note that you must have submitted an application for a Master's programme in Sweden at universityadmissions.se before applying for the SI Scholarship.
Learn more
Country-specific funding
There may be funding options available in your country to support your studies abroad. Below, you will find information on scholarships, loans, and financial programs to help finance your education at Uppsala University.
Canada
Canadian student aid programmes – Education Loans
Uppsala University is recognised by Canadian tax authorities and eligible for Canadian student aid programmes. Canadian students can use government loans to fund their studies at Uppsala University.
Learn more
Colombia
Colfuturo – Scholarships & Education Loans
Uppsala University is recognised by Colfuturo, a Colombian organisation that provides financial support for Colombian students pursuing postgraduate studies abroad. The organisation offers a combination of scholarships and loans to help cover tuition and living expenses.
Learn more
India
WeMakeScholars – Scholarships & Education Loans
WeMakeScholars is an initiative supported by the Ministry of IT, Government of India, providing free assistance to students seeking scholarships and education loans for studies abroad.
Learn more
Education Future Foundation – Scholarship
The Education Future Foundation offers the Education Future International Scholarship, which is open to Indian students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate studies abroad, including in Sweden.
Learn more
Indonesia
Lpdp – Scholarships & Education Loans
The LPDP Scholarship offers financial support to Indonesian citizens for postgraduate studies at top universities worldwide, including Uppsala University. LPDP provides full scholarships covering tuition and living expenses, as well as partial scholarships.
Learn more
Mexico
FIDERH – Scholarship
The Fondo para el Desarrollo de Recursos Humanos (FIDERH) is a federal trust managed by the Bank of Mexico, designed to finance graduate studies for Mexican students. Uppsala University is recognised by FIDERH, meaning Mexican students can use this funding option to support their graduate studies at Uppsala University.
Learn more
Peru
Pronabec – Scholarships & Education Loans
The Programa Nacional de Becas y Crédito Educativo provides scholarships and educational loans to talented Peruvian students with limited financial resources. This program supports access to quality higher education, helping students complete their studies.
Learn more
USA
Post‑9/11 GI Bill®, US Veterans Benefits – Funding
Uppsala University is eligible for US Veterans Affairs (VA) funding through the Post‑9/11 GI Bill®. Veterans and their dependants may be able to use their benefits to help cover tuition fees and living expenses for degree‑level, on‑campus study.
Please note that distance‑learning or online programmes are not eligible for VA benefits.
Most programmes require VA approval before benefits can be used. If the programme you are admitted to is not already approved, Uppsala University must submit an approval request to the VA. This process can take time, and benefits cannot be granted until approval is completed.
If you intend to use your Post‑9/11 GI Bill® benefits at Uppsala University, please email your benefits entitlement letter to study@uu.se , addressed to Lina Solander . We will check the status of your programme and, if needed, submit a VA approval request.
Learn more: US Department of Veterans Affairs – Education Benefits
Contact
If you have any questions about the external scholarships, please contact the specific organisations directly.

Uppsala University Master’s scholarships
Scholarships are available each year for fee-paying international students applying for an international Master's programme at Uppsala University. Please note that the scholarships are highly competitive; only about 2% of those who apply are granted a scholarship. The scholarships available for application may vary from year to year.
Application closed
The scholarship application for studies starting in autumn 2026 is now closed. The application period was 16 January–2 February 2026, and late applications cannot be accepted. The next scholarship round will open in early 2027 for students applying to Uppsala University.
Uppsala University Global Scholarship
Open to
Citizens of countries outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
About the scholarship
The Uppsala University Global Scholarship is funded by the Swedish Council for Higher Education, the government agency responsible for overseeing Swedish higher education.
This scholarship covers the full tuition fee for an international Master's programme at Uppsala University. Please note that it does not cover living expenses.
Level of study
Master’s programmes.
Eligibility criteria
You must be a citizen of a country outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
You must demonstrate academic talent and show interest in being in an educational environment.
You can only be awarded an Uppsala University Global Scholarship for a Master’s programme at Uppsala University chosen as first priority (the programme which you have ranked the highest of the four programmes you can choose at University Admissions).
You must have applied for the Master's programme before the University Admissions deadline.
You must meet the entry requirements for the Master’s programme you applied for. The application fee and your complete supporting documents must be received by University Admissions before the deadline.
Anders Wall’s Scholarship for Studies at Uppsala University
Open to
Citizens of a country in Africa.
About the scholarship
The Anders Wall Scholarship is supported by contributions from The Beijer Foundation and Uppsala University. Anders Wall, Chairman of the Beijer Foundation, is a Swedish entrepreneur and businessman. Since its launch in 2012, the scholarship has supported students from various countries and is currently available to students from African nations.
This scholarship covers the full tuition fee for an international Bachelor’s or Master’s programme at Uppsala University. Please note that it does not cover living expenses.
Level of study
Bachelor’s and Master’s programmes.
Eligibility criteria
You must be a citizen of a country in Africa.
You must demonstrate academic talent and show interest in being in an educational environment.
You can only be awarded an Anders Wall Scholarship for a degree programme at Uppsala University chosen as first priority (the programme which you have ranked the highest of the programmes you can choose at University Admissions).
You must have applied for the Bachelor's or Master's programme before the University Admissions deadline.
You must meet the entry requirements for the programme you applied to. The application fee and your complete supporting documents must be received by University Admissions before the deadline.
The King Carl Gustaf Scholarship
Open to
Citizens of Burkina Faso, Central African Republic, Cuba, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Haiti, Lebanon, Malawi, Mexico, Namibia, Palestine, Philippines, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, Zambia, and Zimbabwe.
About the scholarship
Uppsala University established the King Carl Gustaf Scholarship as a gift to King Carl XVI Gustaf on his 70th birthday. It has been offered since 2016 and is aimed towards students whose academic pursuits has been hindered due to natural conflicts or political unrest. The list of eligible countries is reviewed annually to reflect political and geographical changes worldwide.
This scholarship covers the full tuition fee for an international Master's programme at Uppsala University. Please note that it does not cover living expenses.
Level of study
Master’s programmes.
Eligibility criteria
You must be a citizen of one of the following countries: Burkina Faso, Central African Republic, Cuba, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Haiti, Lebanon, Malawi, Mexico, Namibia, Palestine, Philippines, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, Zambia or Zimbabwe.
You must show why you are particularly vulnerable and therefore in need of the educational environment at Uppsala University, as well as that you have the academic talent required.
You can only be awarded a King Carl Gustaf scholarship for a Master’s programme at Uppsala University chosen as first priority (the programme which you have ranked the highest of the four programmes you can choose at University Admissions).
You must have applied for the Master's programme before the University Admissions deadline.
You must meet the entry requirements for the programme you applied to. The application fee and your complete supporting documents must be received by University Admissions before the deadline.
Swedish Chamber of Commerce in Hong Kong Scholarship
Open to
Citizens of the Special Administrative Region of Hong Kong.
About the scholarship
This scholarship is awarded in the name of the Swedish Chamber of Commerce in Hong Kong to students who are citizens of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China.
The scholarship covers part of the tuition fee for one academic year of studies at Uppsala University. The size of the scholarship will be announced once the scholarship holders has been announced. Please note that it does not cover living expenses.
Level of study
Bachelor’s and Master’s programmes.
Eligibility criteria
You must have a valid passport of the Special Administrative Region of Hong Kong.
You can only be awarded a scholarship for a degree programme at Uppsala University chosen as first priority (the programme which you have ranked the highest of the programmes you can choose at University Admissions).
You must meet the entry requirements for the programme you applied to. The application fee and your complete supporting documents must be received by University Admissions before the deadline.
You must have applied for the Bachelor’s or Master’s programme before the University Admissions deadline.
You must demonstrate academic talent and show interest in being in an educational environment.
Restrictions
Students admitted to the following programmes are not eligible for a scholarship:
Joint Nordic Master’s Programme in Environmental Law
Master’s Programme in Biology NABiS Nordic Master in Biodiversity and Systematics
Master's Programme in Evolutionary Biology – MEME
Master’s Programme in Implementation, Transformative Learning and Sustainability
Master’s Programme in Religion in Peace and Conflict
Master’s Programme in Transformative Game Design
Master’s Programme in Turkic Studies
