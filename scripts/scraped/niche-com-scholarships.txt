NCAA Postgraduate Scholarship
Offered by National Collegiate Athletic Association
Amount
$10,000
Deadline
Varies
The NCAA awards up to 126 postgraduate scholarships annually. The scholarships are awarded to student-athletes who excel academically and athletically and who are at least in their final year of intercollegiate athletics competition.
The one-time non-renewable scholarships of $10,000 are awarded three times a year corresponding to each sport season (fall, winter and spring). Each sports season there are 21 scholarships available for men and 21 scholarships available for women for use in an accredited graduate program.
All former student-athletes who earned an undergraduate degree from an NCAA member school are eligible to be nominated by that school for an NCAA graduate degree scholarship, regardless of when they received their undergraduate degree. View Details

Who Can Apply
All high school and college students, as well as anyone looking to attend college or graduate school in the next year. Please note: Not everyone is eligible for this scholarship. Niche sponsored scholarships and sweepstakes are for people with US citizenship or a valid Visa/US passport only. Read the

How It Works
The $2,000 “No Essay” Scholarship is an easy scholarship with no essay required! Only one entry allowed per person. The winner will be determined by random drawing and then contacted directly by Votigo, Niche’s official scholarship fulfillment partner, and announced in Niche's email newsletter and on the Scholarship Winners page.
About Niche Scholarships
We believe cost shouldn’t keep anyone from pursuing a higher education, so we connect students with thousands of scholarships — many of which don’t require an essay — to help them afford college. In 2025 alone, we offered over $234,700 in Niche scholarships.
If you win a Niche-sponsored scholarship, you'll be notified by email from Votigo, Niche's official scholarship fulfillment partner. Votigo handles the paperwork and disbursement process on Niche's behalf, so be sure to watch your inbox — including your spam folder — for an email from the votigo.com domain, and respond promptly to claim your award.

About Niche scholarships
We believe cost shouldn’t keep anyone from pursuing a higher education, so we offer thousands of scholarships — many of which don’t require an essay — to help more students afford college.

What is a Niche Scholarship?
How do I apply and win?
Create an account
Create or log into your free Niche account, then browse all available scholarships.
Apply
Follow the steps to apply — many don’t require an essay, and you can apply to several with just a few clicks!
Wait and see
Winners are selected and notified via email by Votigo, our official scholarship fulfillment partner. Act fast to claim the scholarship! If we don&#8217;t hear back within two weeks, we repeat the process to select another winner.

How to know it’s real
All official communication about Niche scholarships takes place over email, sent either from Niche or from Votigo (@votigo.com), our trusted scholarship fulfillment partner. If you&#8217;ve received an email from one of these sources, it&#8217;s legit! A real winner email will contain your name and the scholarship you applied for. We will never text or DM you on social media about winning a scholarship. Always pay close attention to the sender&#8217;s email address and handle — anything that is not from @niche.com or @votigo.com is not an official Niche or Votigo communication.

FAQs
Are Niche scholarships legit?
Yes! Niche awards prizes on a monthly and yearly basis to students and families all over the United States. You can read about some of our previous winners by visiting the winners page .
Who is eligible for Niche scholarships?
Niche scholarships are open to all US students or students with a valid visa. Eligibility can change depending on the scholarship, so we always recommend reading the rules before you apply.
How do I apply for scholarships?
You will first need to create a free Niche account . You can then go to our scholarships page and use the options on the left of the page to help you find scholarships or sweepstakes that are the best fit for you.
How will I know if I have won?
If you have won a Niche-sponsored scholarship or sweepstakes, you will be notified by email from Votigo, Niche&#8217;s official scholarship fulfillment partner.  If you have applied for a third-party opportunity, they will contact you with a confirmation and/or notification if you’ve won.
