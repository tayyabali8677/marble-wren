NCAA Postgraduate Scholarship
Offered by National Collegiate Athletic Association
Amount
$10,000
Deadline
Varies
The NCAA awards up to 126 postgraduate scholarships annually. The scholarships are awarded to student-athletes who excel academically and athletically and who are at least in their final year of intercollegiate athletics competition.
The one-time non-renewable scholarships of $10,000 are awarded three times a year corresponding to each sport season (fall, winter and spring). Each sports season there are 21 scholarships available for men and 21 scholarships available for women for use in an accredited graduate program.
All former student-athletes who earned an undergraduate degree from an NCAA member school are eligible to be nominated by that school for an NCAA graduate degree scholarship, regardless of when they received their undergraduate degree. View Details

Who Can Apply
All high school and college students, as well as anyone looking to attend college or graduate school in the next year. Please note: Not everyone is eligible for this scholarship. Niche sponsored scholarships and sweepstakes are for people with US citizenship or a valid Visa/US passport only. Read the
scholarship rules
for full eligibility requirements.
How It Works
The $2,000 “No Essay” Scholarship requires no essay. Eligible entrants may receive up to two entries in the October drawing: one Base Entry for entering the scholarship and one Bonus Entry for reordering their My Colleges list on the Niche website during the posted bonus-action window. You can reorder before or after entering the scholarship. Your Bonus Entry will be credited only if you also submit a valid October Base Entry. Reordering more than once will not earn another entry. See
Official Rules
for an alternate way to earn the Bonus Entry.
The winner will be determined by random drawing and then contacted directly by Votigo, Niche’s official scholarship fulfillment partner, and announced in Niche's email newsletter and on the
Scholarship Winners
page.
About Niche Scholarships
We believe cost shouldn’t keep anyone from pursuing a higher education, so we connect students with thousands of scholarships — many of which don’t require an essay — to help them afford college. In 2025 alone, we offered over $234,700 in Niche scholarships.
If you win a Niche-sponsored scholarship, you'll be notified by email from Votigo, Niche's official scholarship fulfillment partner. Votigo handles the paperwork and disbursement process on Niche's behalf, so be sure to watch your inbox — including your spam folder — for an email from the votigo.com domain, and respond promptly to claim your award.
