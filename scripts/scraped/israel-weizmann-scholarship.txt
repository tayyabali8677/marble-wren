## Believing in an academic community devoted to excellence

At the Weizmann School of Science, we’re building the next generation of scientists. Students are selected with proven academic strengths who exhibit intellectual voracity and strong potential for further scientific growth and accomplishment. The Weizmann School of Science is proud of its diverse student body and welcomes students of every religion, race, color, national origin, age, disability and gender.
