Student Awards & Scholarships
Student awards include scholarships and grants, which are non-repayable monetary awards issued based on criteria such as merit, financial need, community engagement, leadership and athletic performance.
Recognizing and supporting you
We celebrate your achievements and support your journey with thousands of scholarships, awards and grants available throughout your undergraduate studies.
As an applicant, you'll be automatically considered for most entrance scholarships and admission awards. Some additional awards may require an application, such as the Awards Profile , which is available to eligible domestic students. You can also use our Award Explorer to browse all available scholarships.

Spotlight scholarships
The National Scholarship Program
The National Scholarship Program is our most notable award for Canadian high school students and recognizes original and creative thinkers, community leaders, and high academic achievers.
Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship
The Pearson International Scholarships reward exceptional academics, creativity, and leadership, highlighting students' impact on their school and community, as well as their global potential.
What is an award, grant and scholarship?
Student awards are non-repayable monetary awards issued based on criteria such as merit, financial need, community engagement, leadership, athletic performance and more. Thousands of awards worth more than $100 million are available each year.
Award
An award can be a scholarship, fellowship, prize, grant or bursary.
Grant
A monetary award based primarily on financial need. Sometimes “grant” and “bursary” are used interchangeably.
Scholarship
A monetary award based on academic excellence alone or in combination with financial need.
External scholarship programs
Looking beyond U of T's own awards, we also partner with organizations to offer scholarships that recognize outstanding students. These programs provide generous funding and other opportunities to help you thrive throughout your undergraduate journey. Expand All
Beland Honderich Awards
The Beland Honderich Award provides four annual $5,000 scholarships (renewable for up to four years) to students in the Pathways to Education Regent Park Program—two entering from Grade 8 and two graduating from Grade 12. Nominees are selected based on academic potential and socioeconomic need, and recipients must typically enroll in an undergraduate program at the University of Toronto. Renewal of the award is conditional on satisfactory academic progress.
Loran Scholar
The Loran Award offers a four-year leadership-enrichment program, financial support for undergrad, diverse opportunities for experiential learning and a long-term supportive community of peers and mentors—people who will inspire and motivate you in your pursuit to make a difference.
Find our more about the Loran Scholar program
Schulich Leader Scholarships
Schulich Leader Scholarships support outstanding Canadian high school students who are pursuing studies in STEM fields. Each year, up to 10 scholarships are awarded to incoming U of T students:
Five scholarships of $120,000 (paid over four years) for students in engineering
Five scholarships of $100,000 (paid over four years) for students studying science, technology or math
(Specific scholarship distribution is confirmed by Schulich each year in March.)
Applicants must be nominated by their school and apply to the scholarship through the Schulich Leader Scholarships website.
Learn more about the Schulich Leader Scholarships
Scholarships for international students
We offer a range of scholarships and awards to recognize outstanding international students. All admitted students, including international applicants, are automatically considered for admission scholarships—no separate application required for most of them.
Learn more about international scholarships

Requirements Canadian Students
Requirements from high school
Admission requirements for students who are attending a Canadian high school.
Requirements from college or university
Admission requirements for students who have attended a Canadian college or university.
If your most recent studies were at an Ontario or other Canadian high school, you can find admission requirements based on your province. Current high school applicants with strong mid-year results may receive a conditional offer of admission, on the condition that they successfully complete their academic year.
If your most recent studies were at a Canadian university or college, you may be considered based on your previous year's results if you're currently enrolled.

Students from around the world call the University of Toronto home—including many Canadians who lived outside of Canada before beginning their studies at U of T. Canadians living abroad may have some different questions and considerations.

Frequently asked questions Expand All
How do I apply?
Applying to U of T is a two-step process. First, submit your application through the Ontario Universities’ Application Centre (OUAC). Then you'll complete your next steps in the Join U of T or Engineering Applicant Portal. Learn more about how to apply.
Do I need to submit an essay, reference letters or a personal statement?
Not for most programs. Most program areas typically don't require essays, reference letters or personal statements as part of the application. However, some programs do ask for a supplemental application to get to know you beyond your grades. If one is required, you'll find the details in Join U of T or the Engineering Applicant Portal after you apply.
When should I apply?
Applications typically open in late September. You should apply as early as possible in the fall of your final year of high school. Some programs have limited space or supplemental applications, and applying early gives you more time to complete next steps, like submitting documents or supplementary applications. Review all application deadlines.
What if English isn&#039;t my first language?
If English isn’t your first language, you may need to provide proof of English proficiency, unless you qualify for an exemption. There are several ways to meet this requirement, depending on your background and studies. See our English language requirements.
Will I get transfer credit for AP or IB courses?
If you’ve taken Advanced Placement (AP) or International Baccalaureate (IB) courses in high school, you may be eligible for transfer credit. Credits are assessed after admission, after we receive your official final results directly from the examining board. Transfer credit policies vary by faculty and campus. Learn more about transfer credits from high school.
Will my application be different if I&#039;m a Canadian citizen who studied outside Canada?
Your application will be assessed based on the curriculum you studied, not your citizenship. If you completed high school outside of Canada, we'll review your application using the relevant admission requirements (for example, A-Levels or a U.S. diploma). Your Canadian citizenship may affect things like tuition or required documents, but it won't change how you apply or how your application is reviewed.

English Language Requirements
English Language Requirements
English is the language of instruction and examination at U of T, and success in our degree programs requires a high level of English language proficiency.
See accepted tests
Who needs to provide proof of English proficiency?
If English is not your first language (i.e. is not the first language you learned at home as a child), you will need to provide evidence of adequate English facility for admission consideration, unless you qualify for an exemption.
Exemptions from our English language requirement
You may qualify for an exemption from the English facility requirement if one of the following applies to you:
You have completed/are completing four or more years of full-time study in a recognized Canadian school (in Canada) that teaches in English
You have completed/are completing four or more years of full-time study in an English language school located in a country where the dominant language is English
Your first language is French and you have completed four or more years of full-time study at a Canadian school
If you meet any of the exemption criteria, you can request an English language test exemption after you apply. Once you receive your application acknowledgement email, you'll be able to access the "English Language Test Exemption Request Form" on the Join U of T applicant portal.
English course requirement for admission
All applicants, including those who must present proof of English facility, are required to present a senior level English course (equivalent to Ontario ENG4U/EAE4U) for admission to all undergraduate programs. The English course result will be considered in the admissions process and calculated as part of an applicant’s admission average.
Accepted English proficiency tests or qualifications
If you are required to present evidence of English language facility, you can satisfy the requirement by completing one of the tests/qualifications listed below with acceptable results.
Expand All
Cambridge English C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency
Requirements
A minimum overall score of 180, with at least 170 in each component. For information, visit the Cambridge English website . Results from the B2 First exam are not accepted (regardless of the result achieved).
Submitting results
Use the "Send Your Result" function of the Candidate Results portal to send your results electronically to the University of Toronto.
Canadian Academic English Language Assessment (CAEL) including CAEL Online
Requirements
The minimum score required to meet our English facility requirement is an overall score of 70 and no part below 60. For information, visit the CAEL website .
Submitting results
Sign in to your CAEL Account and select University of Toronto as a recipient institution. Results are transmitted to us electronically.
Caribbean Examinations Council English courses
Requirements
The minimum requirement is a final grade of A (I) or B (II) in CSEC English.
Submitting results
Upload a copy of your Statement of Results (issued by CXC) to the Join U of T applicant portal or the Engineering Applicant Portal.
Duolingo English Test
Requirements
The minimum requirement for tests completed on or after July 1st, 2024 is an overall score of 120 with a score of 120 in Production. The minimum requirement for tests completed before July 1st, 2024 is an overall score of 120, with no subscore below 100. For more information, visit the Duolingo English Test website .
Submitting results
To avoid processing delays, do not send your results until you have received your U of T applicant number. To send your results, log in to your Duolingo English Test account and use the “Send Results” function to forward your results to “University of Toronto – Undergraduate”. You will be prompted to add your U of T applicant number to your request.
English Language Diagnosis and Assessment/Certificate of Proficiency in English (ELDA/COPE)
Requirements
The minimum requirement is a total score of 86, with 32 in Writing and 22 in each of Reading and Listening
Discretionary Range: total score 80 or higher, with 32 in Writing and 22 in each of Reading and Listening
Test results that fall into the discretionary range will be reviewed automatically. Applicants will be notified if their discretionary range score satisfies the English language requirement. As we cannot guarantee that these scores will be accepted, it is recommended that applicants arrange to take another test if the official results can be submitted to us by the appropriate document deadline.
ELDA/COPE is currently available in Toronto only. For information, visit the COPE Testing website .
Submitting results
Results must be sent to us electronically by the testing service.
GCSE/IGCSE/GCE/AICE English
Requirements
A minimum final grade of “B” (“6” in reformed qualifications) in GCSE/IGCSE/GCE Ordinary Level English, English Language, or English as a Second Language; OR a minimum final or predicted grade of “C” in GCE A Level/AS Level/AICE English or English Language. Note that English as an Additional Language and English Literature are not accepted.
Submitting results
Final results should be sent to us directly by the appropriate examination authority. Predicted results should be sent by your school.
International Baccalaureate (IB) English
Requirements
The minimum requirement is a score of at least 4 (predicted or final) in Higher or Standard Level English A: Literature or English A: Language and Literature. IB English B HL with a score of 7 is also acceptable.
Submitting results
Final results should be sent to us directly by the International Baccalaureate Organization. You can make your request online at ibo.org (select "University of Toronto" as the transcript recipient). Predicted results should be sent by your school.
IELTS Academic (including IELTS Online)
Requirements
The minimum requirement is an overall band of 6.5, with no band below 6.0. Results from IELTS (Academic) for UKVI test sittings are accepted. For information, visit the IELTS website .
Note: We do not accept the IELTS Indicator test or results from the IELTS One Skill Retake. Applicants must achieve the scores listed above on a single date.
Submitting results
We only accept scores submitted electronically by the IELTS test centre. An institutional code is not required. Contact your IELTS test centre directly to arrange for your scores to be sent electronically to our account via the IELTS Results Service. All test centres worldwide are able to transmit your scores to our system. You must provide the following information to your test centre when submitting:
IELTS Results Service Account name: University of Toronto - Undergraduate and Graduate Programs
IELTS Results Service Account address: 172 St. George St., Toronto ON M5R 0A3
Ontario College of Applied Arts and Technology
Requirements
The minimum requirement is a three-year/Advanced Diploma or current enrolment in the final semester of a three-year/Advanced Diploma or Applied Degree program with overall standing of “B+”. A current, updated transcript is required. All three or four years of study must be completed in Ontario.
Submitting results
Official transcripts must be sent to us directly by the OUAC.
Pearson Test of English (PTE) Academic
Requirements
The minimum requirement is an overall score of 65 with no part below 60. Results from PTE (Academic) UKVI test sittings are accepted. For more information, visit the Pearson Test of English website .
Submitting results
We only accept scores submitted electronically through myPTE account. For more information, visit the PTE Help Center: Scoring page .
TOEFL iBT (including Home Edition and Paper Edition)
Requirements
The minimum requirement for tests taken before January 21, 2026 is a total score of 89 with 22 in each of Speaking and Writing. The requirements for applicants who took the test on or after January 21, 2026 is an overall score of 4.5 with 4.5 in Writing and 4.0 in Speaking.
Applicants must achieve the scores listed above on a single date. TOEFL MyBest (super-scored) results will not be used.
We do not accept TOEFL Essentials, TOEFL ITP or TOEFL ITP Plus for China for admission purposes.
Submitting results
Results must be sent to us electronically from ETS – TOEFL. The institution code is 0982-00. For information, visit the TOEFL website .
University of Toronto School of Continuing Studies “Academic English” course
Requirements
The minimum requirement is a grade of “B” at the 60/Advanced level. For information, visit the University of Toronto School of Continuing Studies website .
Submitting results
Upon successful completion of the Academic English Level 60 course, you must ask the English Language Program office to send the results electronically to the Office of University Admissions and Outreach.
University Studies
Requirements
The minimum requirement is one year of full-time study completed at a recognized university in a country where English is the dominant language. At least four full-year transferable courses completed with a grade of “C” in each are required. Courses in progress will also be considered. A current, updated transcript is required.
Submitting results
Official final transcripts should be sent to us directly by the university, or through the OUAC if you attended university in Ontario.
Don't meet our English language requirement?
If you’re academically qualified but don't yet meet our English language requirement, our English language transition programs can help. Offered on all three campuses and ranging from six weeks to eight months, these programs build your English skills and prepare you for university studies.
Browse our English language transition programs
Submitting your results
Your results need to be sent to us as soon as possible and received no later than your deadline for submitting supporting documentation— find the appropriate deadline in Important Application Dates and on your applicant portal. You will not be penalized for an unacceptable result, so we encourage you to send all test results as they become available.
We accept only official test scores sent directly to University Admissions & Outreach by the testing agency.
Be sure that you register for a test using the same first name, surname (last/family name) and date of birth as on your application for admission. We cannot match your results to your application if this information differs.
For verification purposes, the university reserves the right to share English facility test results sent in support of your application for admission with other English facility test provider(s) who have submitted results to University Admissions & Outreach on your behalf. We also reserve the right to require recent, government-issued photo identification for verification purposes.

Frequently asked questions Expand All
Can I submit my Grade 12 English course instead of a language test?
No. A senior-level English course is required for admission, but it does not meet the English language proficiency requirement. If you're required to provide proof of English facility, you must do so using an accepted test or qualification.
Can I send my test scores myself?
No. Be sure to carefully follow the instructions—test scores must be sent directly to the University of Toronto by the testing agency. Scores submitted by the applicant or their school will not be accepted.
What happens if I don&#039;t meet the minimum test scores?
Meeting our language requirement is a condition for admission. If you don't meet the required scores but are otherwise admissible, you may be considered for one of our English Language Transition Programs.
Do I need to meet the English requirement if I&#039;m a transfer student?
Yes. All applicants, whether entering from high school, college or university, must meet the English language proficiency requirement, unless they qualify for an exemption.
