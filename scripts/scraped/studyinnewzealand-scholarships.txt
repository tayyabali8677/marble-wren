## Why study in New Zealand?

![Image 8: Leadership](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/3ryh1ZGyX3l5nqfKFo787d/51595c02731d58953ce88629a148fc43/Leadership.png?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

New Zealand rank first in the English-speaking world and fifth globally for the overall quality of its higher education, according to the [QS World University Rankings 2026.](https://www.studyinnewzealand.govt.nz/how-to-apply/QS%20World%20University%20Rankings%202026.)

![Image 9: Icon - grad cap](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/4Llagw6coW36FGyBasDhJo/9aae1a005b715f36ba5a7df7ece6fb45/ENZ_icon_grad_cap.jpg?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

All New Zealand's universities are ranked in the top 2% of universities in the world ([QS World University Rankings(external link that opens in a new tab)](https://www.topuniversities.com/qs-world-university-rankings)).

![Image 10: Icon Peace Black](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/1jSxrBkXoLEWJQCFhxN1Bd/5a729c453cfceda5cc6953715aedf920/Icon_Peace_Black.png?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

New Zealand is ranked the 2ⁿᵈ most peaceful country in the world, according to the [Global Peace Index 2026. (external link that opens in a new tab)](https://www.visionofhumanity.org/maps/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22116084622&gclid=EAIaIQobChMIzYrHwNaHlQMVqsU8Ah3p4RiQEAAYASAAEgJvuPD_BwE#/)

![Image 11: Scales](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/7944myBxD0InJDc2ZuRg05/a3fc1882cf08e0b4ddb415117b6f642e/Scales.png?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

New Zealand is ranked the 1ˢᵗ in the world for work-life balance, according to the [Global Life-Work Balance Index (2023), Remote(external link that opens in a new tab)](https://remote.com/en-nz/resources/research/global-life-work-balance-index).

![Image 12: Icon Creativity Black](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/7su0pJPSIt1XdCN3EUl6Wq/2e92b29762fc31a643a76bccc7b878bf/Icon_Creativity_Black.png?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

New Zealand's students are ranked the 5ᵗʰ most creative thinkers in the world. [(OECD Programme for International Student Assessment - PISA, 2022).(external link that opens in a new tab)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/06/pisa-2022-results-volume-iii-country-notes_72b418f8/new-zealand_4cdfccbb/e231e15e-en.pdf)

![Image 13: Icon Sustainable Growth](https://images.ctfassets.net/d5o6yop857fx/1NfcMUOJLOlIbR7JVo6Zk9/23ed791bb77c6517a01bd8e70f2ee9f0/Sustainable_Growth__Sustainability.jpg?w=96&fm=webp&q=80&fit=fill)

New Zealand universities are ranked 1ˢᵗ in the world for sustainability stewardship. ([The Times Higher Education Impact Rankings, 2024)(external link that opens in a new tab)](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings/impact-rankings-2024-universities-make-trustworthy-stewards).
