How to apply

## It’s as easy as 1·2·3

The whole application happens inside myNSFAS — on the app or in a browser. There is no fee at any stage, and no one needs to apply on your behalf.

1.   ![Image 5: A student smiling at a library computer workstation](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/images/step-1.jpg)

### Create a myNSFAS account

Go to **my.nsfas.org.za** or open the NSFAS app. Your **ID number is your username**. An OTP is sent to activate your profile — never share it with anyone. 
2.   ![Image 6: A student working on a laptop at a library desk](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/images/step-2.jpg)

### Apply and upload

Select **Apply**, complete your personal and household details, then upload each document the system asks you for. **PDF or JPEG, up to 20 MB** each, clear and readable. 
3.   ![Image 7: Four students outdoors looking at a laptop screen together](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/images/step-3.jpg)

### Submit and track

Review everything, submit, and save your **reference number**. Then open the **Tracker** in myNSFAS to follow your status — you have 30 working days to answer any request for more information. 

### Every applicant

One form is required from everyone, no exceptions.

*   **A signed NSFAS consent form**This lets NSFAS verify your household income.

**Your list is personalised.** myNSFAS generates the exact documents you need from your application type — upload what it asks for, and nothing else. If you receive a SASSA grant, no proof of income is required.

### Only if it applies to you

The portal will tell you which of these it needs.

*   **Disability Annexure A form**For students with disabilities. Available on the NSFAS website.
*   **Vulnerable Child Declaration form**For applicants under 18, completed by a social worker.
*   **NSFAS Declaration form**If you are under 35 and the people you declared do not match Home Affairs records — most often with legal guardians.
*   **ID copies of your parent(s) or guardian**Only if their details differ from Home Affairs records.
*   **SARS ITA34**Only if you appeal your financial eligibility.

## Official NSFAS 2027 Supporting document forms

Print each form that applies to you, complete it by hand and scan it back in. The system generates your own document list based on your application type — always check myNSFAS for what it has asked you for.

![Image 9: Page one of the NSFAS 2027 Consent Form, showing the applicant ID number block and the parent, guardian and spouse signature sections.](blob:http://localhost/4dd4adb28027d9efb1f620547e49b1fb)

Required by everyone FORM APP2027

### NSFAS 2027 Consent Form

Gives NSFAS permission to verify your identity, household and income against third-party sources including SARS and the Department of Home Affairs. Signed by the applicant and by every parent, legal guardian or spouse whose details appear on the application. Without it, your application cannot be submitted.

*   Capital letters only
*   Black ink
*   No electronic signatures
*   No correction fluid
*   1 page

[Download consent form](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/downloads/NSFAS%202027%20Applications%20Consent%20Form.pdf)

### Disability Annexure Form

For applicants living with a disability. A registered medical practitioner completes and signs it. Merge the medical report into the same file as the annexure before you upload — separate files are returned.

Raises the household income threshold to R600 000.

[Download form](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/downloads/NSFAS%202027%20Application%20Disability%20Annexure.pdf)
### Orphan or Vulnerable Child Declaration

Completed only by applicants under the age of 18 who are orphans, or who are vulnerable children in the terms set out on the form.

Replaces parent or guardian income details where no parent or guardian can sign.

[Download form](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/downloads/NSFAS%202027%20Application%20Orphan%20or%20Vulnerable%20Child%20Declaration%20form.pdf)
### NSFAS Declaration Form

Applies to applicants aged 34 and younger. It is used to verify the parent or legal guardian details captured on your application.

Often confused with the consent form — most applicants under 34 submit both.

[Download form](https://www.nsfas.org.za/content/downloads/NSFAS%202027%20Applications%20Declaration%20Form.pdf)

## Documents that depend on your situation

Only upload what applies to your household.

Supporting documents by circumstance, NSFAS 2027 applications.
| If this applies to you | Upload this | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every applicant | South African ID or birth certificate (Smart ID card: both sides) | Yes |
| A parent, legal guardian or spouse earns an income | Payslips for the last three months, or a UIF statement, or an affidavit where the income is informal | Not required |
| Someone in the household receives a SASSA grant | Nothing — Child Support, Foster Child and Care Dependency grants are verified with SASSA automatically | Not required |
| A parent or guardian has died | Death certificate for each deceased parent or guardian | Yes |
| Your parents are divorced or separated | Divorce decree, or the settlement agreement showing maintenance | Yes |
| You are married, or a spouse signs for you | Marriage certificate, plus your spouse's ID | Yes |
| You live with a legal guardian | Proof of legal guardianship issued by a court | Yes |
| A parent or legal guardian was retrenched | Retrenchment letter from the employer — also accepted as supporting evidence in an appeal | Not required |

Step 3 — Upload

## Preparing a file that passes first time

1.   01
### Sign it by hand

Print, complete in capital letters using black ink, and sign. Typed or pasted signatures are rejected. Any correction is initialled by every party who signed.

2.   02
### Scan the whole page/s

Capture every edge of the document in good light. Cropped corners, shadows and blurred ID numbers are the most common reason a document is sent back.

3.   03
### Merge into one file

Multi-page documents go up as a single file — both pages of the declaration form together, and the medical report merged with the disability annexure.

4.   04
### Upload to the right slot

Each requested document has its own slot in myNSFAS portal. Upload it there, then check that the status changes before you leave the page.

## NSFAS will never ask you to pay for an application or share your password

Apply only through the official myNSFAS portal or the NSFAS mobile application. Ignore WhatsApp forwards, social media accounts and agents offering to speed up your application or upload documents on your behalf. ## Systems Security Advisory

NSFAS was recently alerted to a potential security weakness within certain components of its ICT systems. Upon identification, NSFAS immediately activated its information security and incident management protocols, prioritised the matter, and implemented remedial measures without delay.

Actions taken include strengthening access controls, reviewing system permissions, and enhancing monitoring across affected environments. At this stage, there is no indication of an ongoing system compromise.

NSFAS remains committed to transparency and compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This advisory is issued to inform users of the incident and the steps taken to secure NSFAS systems. Direct communication will be undertaken where required, based on verified risk and regulatory guidance.

NSFAS continues to strengthen its cybersecurity environment to protect the personal information of students and stakeholders.

Users are encouraged to remain vigilant, protect their login credentials, and report any suspicious activity via official NSFAS communication channels.

###### Enhanced Access Controls

User permissions and system access have been reviewed and reinforced across affected environments.

###### Continuous Monitoring

Advanced monitoring has been implemented to detect and respond to potential threats in real time.

###### POPIA Compliance

NSFAS remains committed to lawful, transparent, and responsible handling of personal information.

**Who qualifies for the Bursary?**

You qualify for a NSFAS bursary if you are a South African citizen who plans to study in 2026 or you are already studying at a public university or TVET college and you meet the following requirements:

*   SASSA recipients automatically qualify on financial eligibility
*   Your combined household income is not more than R350 000 per year or
*   Person with a disability with a combined household income of not more than R600 000 per year or 
*   Must have email and cell phone number
