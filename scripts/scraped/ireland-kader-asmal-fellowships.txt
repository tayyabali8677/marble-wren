Ireland Fellows Programme
The Ireland Fellows Programme offers people from around the world the opportunity to spend one year studying in Ireland.
Ireland believes that education is central to creating a better world. The programme is fully funded by the Irish Government and is offered under the auspices of the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade.
Almost 4,000 people have studied in Ireland over the past 50+ years as part of the programme.
