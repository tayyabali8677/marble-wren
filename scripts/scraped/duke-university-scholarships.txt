Renew Your Aid
Mark your calendar! If you&#x27;ll be a returning student in the 26-27 school year, please submit your renewal aid application before February 1st, 2026 .
For the 26-27 school year, you&#x27;ll share information on the FAFSA and CSS Profile about your family&#x27;s income from the 2024 tax year . Have your documents handy!
Renew Your Aid for US Citizens & Permanent Residents
Loading...
Step 1: Complete the FAFSA
Submit the  Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) online and have the results sent to Duke.
Be sure to use your tax data as filed with the IRS
Loading...
Step 2: Complete the CSS Profile
Complete the  CSS Profile .
Click here for a tutorial on how to complete the CSS Profile
Loading...
Step 3: Keep an Eye on Your To-Do List
After you complete the CSS Profile, your DukeHub To-Do list may indicate that you need to submit additional documents to complete your financial aid application. Please keep an eye on your To-Do list!
*Please note that proxy users will not be able to upload documents through DukeHub.
Be sure to check your to-do items in DukeHub regularly! If our office needs additional documents from you to complete your aid application, we&#x27;ll list them there.
International Students
If you&#x27;re an international student currently receiving financial aid, you don&#x27;t need to reapply. Your aid will be renewed automatically each year.
International applicants must have checked the financial aid interest box on their original Duke admissions application to receive need-based aid at any point while at Duke. In addition, there are no need-based or merit scholarships available for international transfer students.
