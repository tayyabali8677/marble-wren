Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme
ETH Zurich supports excellent Master's students (no MAS) with the following scholarship programme:
The Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP) supports students with a scholarship, mentorship and a network of the ETH Foundation. The scholarship covers the full study and living costs during the Master’s degree course.
The next application window is open Nov, 1 - Nov, 30 2026, 11.59h, MEZ for a start in the Master programme in HS27.
ESOP applications are supplied via eApply (online Master application).
This also applies for ETH Bachelor students for a consecutive Master programme.
1. Very good result in Bachelor’s degree programme (top 10% of Bachelor’s degree programme = grade A; this is based on self-assessment).
2. Please make sure to be reachable under the telephone number specified in your CV as the selection process might include a phone/VC interview in February. If your are not available during that time, please inform the study programme coordinator.
If you are already enrolled in a Master's degree programme at ETH Zurich, or hold a Master degree, you are not eligible for ESOP.
For an ESOP Scholarship you must apply within the first application window.
November 1 - 30 (12.59 MEZ)
In addition to the documents for the Master admission you will need to upload a pre-proposal for your master thesis ( Download guidelines_preproposal (PDF, 86 KB) ).
The pre-proposal for the Master's thesis must be developed by yourself and use established scientific citation practice.
Plagiarism, negligent or intentional misinformation will lead to exclusion from the admission process or - if discovered later - to the revocation of the admission and of any scholarship.
If your study programme does not ask for references you must name two reference persons (professors) for your ESOP application. These persons should upload (after an email from the system) a reference letter.
The Excellence Scholarship/ESOP consists of a scholarship covering living and study expenses (CHF 12'000 per semester) as well as a tuition fee waiver.
ESOP is awarded for the regular duration of the Master’s programme (three or four semesters). This also applies for the tuition fee waiver.
Only ETH Bachelor students may start the ESOP with a leave of absence.
For the start in the academic year 2027/28 there will be assigned approximately 60 ESOP scholarships. The decision is communicated at the end of March.
The applications are evaluated by the Admissions Committees of the respective Master’s programmes. The final decision for awarding scholarships in the Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme rests with the Rector of ETH Zurich. The exact number of annual scholarships depends on the availability of funds. The decisions are communicated by end of March.

Scholarships
ETH Zurich offers social scholarships for Bachelor and Master studies as well as merit-based excellence scholarships for Master studies insofar the study is your first education and you do not have a degree on the same level.
Need-based social scholarships
Funding studies is primarily the responsibility of the student and the immediate family. ETH Zurich offers social scholarships if financial ressources are available.
Social scholarships are subsidiary funding. They do not cover living and study costs.
Scholarship calculator
Scholarships for study and living costs are awarded on the condition that the applicant can provide evidence of his/her financial circumstances in the form of official documents.
The external page scholarship calculator can help determine the amount of a potential ETH-Scholarship. There is no entitlement to benefits on the basis of this estimate sum. It calculates with the single tuition fee.
If there occur problems with the scholarship calculator using the Internet Explorer, a change to Firefox or Chrome is recommended.
Scholarships from swiss foundations
There are a number of foundations in Switzerland and abroad offering scholarships to support students. It is worth searching for these funding opportunities online and/or making enquiries at your local authority.
