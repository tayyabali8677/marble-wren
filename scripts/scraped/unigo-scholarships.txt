What Are Scholarships?
Scholarships are forms of financial aid that are gifted to students to put toward their education-related expenses. These financial gifts don&#8217;t need to be repaid and can typically be used for expenses such as tuition costs, housing, and books.
How Do Scholarships Work?
If you&#8217;re like most college students, you&#8217;re probably wondering how scholarships work. Essentially, scholarships are financial awards given to students to put toward the cost of their education. In most cases, scholarships can be used to pay for college and university tuition, housing costs, and educational supplies such as books.
While some forms of financial aid such as grants must be repaid in some instances, scholarships typically never have to be repaid regardless of your GPA or if you fail to complete your program of study.
In most cases, scholarships are awarded on a merit basis to students who excel in academic, athletic, or extracurricular pursuits. However, some scholarships are awarded to students based on other factors such as minority status, field of study, and financial need. In other words, scholarships come in various forms, and it&#8217;s important for you to explore your options to lessen the financial burden of your college education.

While many high school students wait until their senior year to start applying for college scholarships, the truth is that you can start as early as you like. In fact, getting the process started in your freshman career will allow you to apply for more scholarships and potentially save more on your college education.
It&#8217;s important to keep in mind that while most scholarship application deadlines fall between September and October, each scholarship will have its own unique deadlines and application processes. By being mindful of these differences and planning accordingly, you can set yourself up for scholarship success.

Major-specific scholarships are financial awards given to students for their commitment to pursuing an education in a particular field.

Scholarships By Type
Much like the realm of higher education itself, scholarships can come in a wide variety of different forms and types. Given the broad range of scholarships out there, chances are you&#8217;re eligible to receive some form of financial award to put toward your higher education career.
Scholarships can come from many different types of organizations including non-profit foundations, private companies, and government agencies. While scholarships are typically awarded based on academic achievement, athletic ability, or extracurricular accomplishments, some are awarded to students based on other factors such as financial need and minority status.
Some key types of scholarships include athletic scholarships, full-ride scholarships, and need-based scholarships. No matter what type of student you are, there&#8217;s a significant chance that you&#8217;re eligible to receive multiple types of scholarships that can lessen your financial burden when obtaining a university-level education.
Athletic Scholarships
Athletic scholarships are financial awards given to high school athletes who display exceptional athletic skill, talent, or accomplishments. While many of these types of scholarships come from higher education institutions themselves, some come from foundations intent on promoting certain sports.
Like many other types of scholarships, athletic scholarships can be awarded to students in various forms. While some athletic scholarships may cover all of a student athlete&#8217;s college expenses, others may only cover a portion of them.
Some common types of student-athletes who receive athletic scholarships include golfers, basketball players, baseball players, and ice hockey players. Many of the most substantial athletic scholarships are merit-based and are only awarded to the most gifted and accomplished high school athletes. Despite this, there are many athletic scholarships out there that you may be eligible to receive as a high school athlete.
Company Offered Awards
While many college scholarships come from governmental organizations and non-profit foundations, some come from private companies. Oftentimes, these scholarships are awarded to students who plan on studying a subject closely related to the type of business that a specific company engages in.
Company offered scholarships can be awarded based on both academic merit as well as financial need. Much like private companies can be extremely diverse, the financial awards offered by different companies can vary significantly as well.
Some key industries that students can find company offered awards in include automotive manufacturing, computer manufacturing, and banking. If you know exactly what you&#8217;ll be majoring in, you have the potential to mitigate your university costs by exploring company offered awards in your field.
Grants for College
While scholarships are typically awarded to students based on factors such as grades and extracurricular achievements, grants are typically awarded to students based on financial need. Another key distinction to keep in mind about these different types of financial aid is that scholarships don&#8217;t ever need to be repaid while students must meet certain requirements, such as completing their educational program, to not have to repay grants.
The majority of grants awarded to college students come from the government, and the FAFSA government portal can be an excellent resource for college students looking to save on educational expenses. Oftentimes, students with the greatest financial need will receive the largest awards through grants.
No matter what your background is, it can be helpful to apply for all of the grants available to you. By putting in this extra effort, you can potentially be awarded thousands of dollars that you can put toward college tuition, room and board, and books.
Merit-Based Scholarships
Merit-based scholarships are some of the most common types of scholarships awarded to students at the high school and college levels. These scholarships are typically awarded to students based on academic merit, athletic potential, or artistic achievements.
Factors such as grades, SAT scores, athletic awards, and artistic accomplishments can play a key role in determining which students receive merit-based scholarships. Given the nature of these scholarships, they&#8217;re often some of the most competitive and difficult to obtain.
Despite them being more challenging to acquire than other types of financial aid, there are ways to increase your chances of landing a merit-based scholarship. These include practices such as applying early, making high SAT scores a priority, and seeking out the most relevant scholarships for your personal achievements.
Scholarships for Veterans and Families
Active-duty service members, veterans, and their families have a range of fantastic scholarships available to them. While many army-related scholarships are geared toward active service members, there&#8217;s no shortage of resources for veterans and their families.
Though some scholarships for veterans and their families are available to all service members of the army, others are specific to different branches of the military. Depending on which branch of the military you or a family served in, your access to army-related scholarships will change.
ROTC, or Reserve Officer Training Corps, scholarships are some of the most popular and sought-after army-related scholarships. Essentially, these scholarships are for high school students who agree to serve in the military for a specific amount of years in return for a financial award to put toward higher education.
Scholarships for Minority Students
Students from minority groups have access to many remarkable scholarships that can help them mitigate the costs of their college careers. Some common types of minority scholarships include those for students of certain ethnicities, those for students from the LGBTQ+ community, those for students with disabilities, and those for first-generation college students.
Each specific scholarship for minority students will have its own unique requirements, such as GPA requirements and financial standing. Some minority scholarships even require students to submit an essay to be eligible to receive a financial award.
While many students from minority groups face a significant amount of adversity in their lives and educational careers, scholarships for minority students can lessen the burden of college costs and make obtaining a degree more accessible for many people.
Religious Scholarships
While the majority of scholarships are awarded to students based on academic merit, extracurricular achievements, and financial need, some are awarded based on other determining factors. One such type is religious scholarships – available to students who practice a specific faith or religious tradition.
Though they&#8217;re often overlooked as a financial aid option, religious scholarships can help you cover a variety of college-related expenses such as tuition, housing costs, and educational supplies. In addition, these types of scholarships are available to students from a wide variety of faiths such as Christian, Catholic, Muslim, Jewish, and Buddhist students.
This means that there&#8217;s a great chance you&#8217;ll be eligible for some form of religious scholarship that can help you save big on your college education.

Scholarship Contests and Sweeps
Like any current or aspiring college student, you&#8217;re probably looking for ways to reduce the cost of getting a top-notch education. Fortunately for you, scholarship contests and sweepstakes are easily accessible tools for you to do exactly that.
When it comes to scholarship contests, you get an opportunity to let your personal skills and attributes shine. The reason for this is that these types of scholarship contests typically require you to submit some form of content, such as an essay or video, to be considered for an award.
If that sounds like a bit too overwhelming of a process — don&#8217;t worry. Sweepstake scholarships, on the other hand, are basically a contest or lottery in which you submit your name and hope you&#8217;re chosen. As long as you follow the rules and apply correctly, you have an equal chance of winning as every other applicant.
Weird and Unusual Scholarships
When you think of scholarships, your mind probably wanders toward criteria like grades, academic achievements, and extracurricular activities. While many scholarships do consider these factors, some scholarships are awarded to students for some pretty weird and unusual reasons.
Don&#8217;t believe us? Well, what if we told you there were unique scholarships meant for students with blue eyes, red hair, and even left-handedness? Though it might seem too good — or too odd — to be true, the truth is that there are a variety of weird and unusual scholarships out there for you to take advantage of.
And, who knows? Your hair color, hobby, or ambidextrous nature just might be your way to save thousands of dollars on your college tuition.
