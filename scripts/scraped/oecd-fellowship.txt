The OECD mission

From policy experts and economists to corporate functions and administrative support, the range of professionals who work in the OECD is vast, and we are constantly looking for talented and committed individuals with diverse backgrounds and expertise to join our more than 3 900 staff.  

Together, they work to raise global standards and develop solutions for global challenges, promoting policies to improve the economic and social well-being of people around the world. The OECD brings together governments, members of parliament, businesses, community leaders, civil society organisations and academics to seek solutions to common problems, develop global standards, share experiences and identify best practices in a collaborative effort towards better policies for better lives.

Staff categories
Associated Staff

This staff category comprises:

Persons on loan

Persons loaned to the OECD on a temporary basis by public or private institutions to contribute to the execution of the OECD Programme of work. They are appointed on fixed-term contract for up to 36 months.

Interns

Enrolled students offered a short-term temporary experience based on a Memorandum of Agreement between the OECD and a higher education institution, initially for up to six months, renewable up to 12 months.

Official Staff

Officials in Senior Executive Leadership roles (EL1–EL3) are hired on an initial fixed-term contract of up to three years, which may be renewed multiple times, each renewal being for a maximum period of up to three years.

All other officials are appointed on either open-ended or on fixed-term contract for a total continuous duration of three years maximum. 

Temporary Staff

Temporary staff members are hired to meet short-term needs, on an ad hoc basis (e.g., specialised work, long-term leave replacement, etc.) and are hired for a limited time period, normally up to two years of continuous service.

Executive leadership training programmes

The Emerging Leaders Programme (ELP) and the Global Management Academy (GMA) are executive education programmes that provide a unique opportunity for high-potential professionals to develop leadership and management skills tailored to the context of International Organisations (IOs) and International Financial Institutions (IFIs).

Diversity and inclusion

Diversity and inclusion are foundational to the work of the OECD, driving us to deliver better policies for better lives.

We welcome all individuals, regardless of their gender identity or expression, disability, neurodiversity, race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, religion or beliefs.
