Welcome
Find out if you're eligible for financial support.
Undergraduate scholarships
We are committed to supporting the best students irrespective of financial circumstances. Please also ensure you observe any application deadlines and submission requirements.
Postgraduate scholarships
Our scholarships, awards and bursaries are dedicated to supporting students from around the world. Other options for funding your studies may also be available.
International scholarships
Our scholarships and bursaries are either funded by the University, through partnerships with external organisations or through generous donations from alumni and friends of the University.
Alumni Fee Scholarship
This scholarship for Durham alumni will contribute part of the cost of tuition fees for a postgraduate degree or PGCE programmes across all faculties of the University.
North East scholarships
All financial support opportunities have associated eligibility criteria, such as household income, therefore it is essential applicants consider all requirements prior to submitting an application.
Scholarships for current students
Our scholarships, awards and bursaries are for students who have already commenced their studies at Durham.
College and department scholarships
When applying for a college scholarship you have the opportunity to also be considered for scholarships at the other participating colleges, so your application will be reviewed by each college.
Business School Masters scholarships
Our comprehensive package of scholarships celebrates and rewards exceptional candidates applying to our Masters programmes.
Business School MBA scholarships
Our comprehensive package of scholarships celebrates and rewards exceptional candidates applying to our MBA programmes.
Contact us
If you have any questions about whether you are eligible for a scholarship or the application process, get in touch with our Scholarships and Student Funding Office.
Bursaries and student financial support
The Bursaries and Student Financial Support team is responsible for the Durham Grant Scheme, the Independent Student Bursary Scheme and the Student Support Fund.
Funding database
We offer a range of support to help ensure that the brightest and best students can access a world-class education.

Getting here by road
Durham city centre is only two miles from the A1(M). Leave the motorway at Junction 62 on the A690 Durham - Sunderland road and follow signs to Durham City Centre.
Durham is 264 miles from London, 187 miles from Birmingham, 125 miles from Edinburgh and 67 miles from York.
Public transport
There are several express coach services daily from most major cities. Durham is well served by both regional express services and the local bus network. From the city bus station - a short walk from the railway station - a bus service runs every 15 minutes past the Colleges on South Road.
Queen's Campus directions
Getting here by air
Durham is 30 minutes' drive from Newcastle International Airport and about 40 minutes from Teesside International Airport . Both have regular domestic and international flights. Durham is linked to Newcastle Airport by rail and metro.
Getting here by train
A number of train operators offer direct and regular routes to Durham Railway Station, including London and Edinburgh. Durham is around 3 hours from London, just over 3 hours from Birmingham, 2½ hours from Manchester, 1½ hours from Edinburgh and 45 minutes from York.
A taxi will take you from the station to any College in about 5 minutes and you can walk to the city centre in 10 minutes.
Getting here by sea
Scheduled ferry services link the River Tyne to The Netherlands.

Summary
As a Durham graduate would you like a scholarship for further study at Durham?
Are you considering options for further study, retraining, and career advancement? We can help.
We are keen to support you in realising this through the Durham University Alumni Fee Scholarship . This Scholarship will contribute part of the cost of tuition fees.
Amount
10% discount for all programmes
Year of Entry
Applicants for programmes commencing in 2026-27, 2027-28 and 2028-29
Number of awards available
N/A
Level of study
Postgraduate
Mode of study
Full-time, Part-time
Programmes
All postgraduate taught Masters programmes across all faculties of the University - MA, MSc, LLM, PGCE and all MBA and other taught Masters programmes
For alumni who commence a new postgraduate research programme, including MRes, they will be eligible for a 10% fee discount for each year of their published programme length.
Duration
For students who commenced their studies prior to 2023-24 on a postgraduate course, they will continue to be eligible for the existing fee discount they received in previous years.
The Scholarship is available only for students commencing their programme in 2026-27, 2027-28 or 2028-29 and will last for the published programme length.
Eligibility
As long as you hold a Bachelor's or higher degree from Durham University and apply for a course that begins within the 2026-27, 2027-28, or 2028-29 academic year, you are automatically eligible for this Scholarship - you do not need to apply separately.
Those holding CHE/DHE or postgraduate/CPD credits only will, unfortunately, not be eligible to receive the Scholarship.
You must be self-funding (or majority self-funding) in order to receive this scholarship. The value of the contribution to your fees must exceed the value of this proposed scholarship. For example: if your fee is &pound;10,000 and you have a 10% Alumni Fee Discount you must intend to make a personal contribution of &pound;1,001 in order to receive the scholarship, excluding any other third-party support of fee sponsorship. Any repayable loans used towards fees would count as self-funding.
Those who have outstanding financial debt to the University will not be eligible until the debt has been cleared.
For the purposes of both schemes, alumni are defined as those who have been awarded a degree qualification by Durham University, and who studied at Durham University (i.e. not a degree conferred by the University for study at another institution, for example Common Awards at another institution). A student in receipt of government loans for study is considered self-funding. The scheme does not apply to students who are not majority self-funding.
If another scholarship specifically excludes stacking (combining) that scholarship with the Alumni Discount, then the Alumni Discount would not apply.
Not sure if you're eligible? Contact us for advice.
How to apply
Apply for a course
As long as you hold a degree from Durham University and apply for a course that begins within the 2026-27, 2027-28 or 2028-29 academic year, you are automatically eligible for this Scholarship - you do not need to apply separately.
If your invoice for your postgraduate programme does not reflect your alumni status
Please notify the University of your alumni status by e-mailing student.records@durham.ac.uk Once this has been confirmed and corrected then please request your invoice be corrected by using the contact details on the invoice.
If you have already received a scholarship from the Business School for one of their Masters or MBA courses, please email business.pgrecruit@durham.ac.uk for any clarification required.

If you need further guidance regarding completing the Finance section of your online enrolment or your eligibility, please contact us using the details below.
