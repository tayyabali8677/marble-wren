Scholarships
Come to TU Delft with a Scholarship!
At TU Delft, we embody the principal of creating impact for a better society and aim to attract the brightest, most talented, and highly motivated students worldwide to our MSc programmes. We offer 16 full scholarships for our regular MSc programmes from the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence foundation and from the MSc Electrical Engineering Programme 2 partial Scholarships .
The scholarships enable students to study in the Netherlands, contribute to a diverse and innovative academic community, and grow into future-proof engineers with a strong commitment to sustainability.
Every year, TU Delft receives far more applications for its scholarships than it can fund, leaving many students disappointed. Carefully evaluate your MSc application, ensure you meet all eligibility criteria, and consider the financial costs of a two-year programme if you do not receive a scholarship. Scholarships are extremely limited and highly competitive, with only the strongest candidates selected. For transparency, we recommend checking the Tuition Fees & Finances page to avoid unexpected financial challenges later in the process.

Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship?
Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships are financed by the legacy of Justus and Louise van Effen. Mr. van Effen studied at TU Delft in the 1940’s and strongly believed technological developments will contribute to solving societal issues. Awarded students will receive full tuition fees per year for a TU Delft MSc programme based on the Statutory or Institutional rate, according to the registered nationality, AND contribution for the living expenses.

Electrical Engineering Scholarship
The Microelectronics Scholarship is financed through the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS). The goal of the scholarship is to help financially challenged students with proven potential to study at the TU Delft. Awarded students will receive a partial scholarship towards the cost of their MSc studies at TU Delft.

Partial scholarship for up to two students

Extension of the scholarship to the second year is subject to maintaining an average grade of 8 (80%) during the first year.

The student must remain enrolled at TU Delft during the scholarship period.

Scholarships
Come to TU Delft with a Scholarship!
At TU Delft, we embody the principal of creating impact for a better society and aim to attract the brightest, most talented, and highly motivated students worldwide to our MSc programmes. We offer 16 full scholarships for our regular MSc programmes from the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence foundation and from the MSc Electrical Engineering Programme 2 partial Scholarships .
The scholarships enable students to study in the Netherlands, contribute to a diverse and innovative academic community, and grow into future-proof engineers with a strong commitment to sustainability.
Every year, TU Delft receives far more applications for its scholarships than it can fund, leaving many students disappointed. Carefully evaluate your MSc application, ensure you meet all eligibility criteria, and consider the financial costs of a two-year programme if you do not receive a scholarship. Scholarships are extremely limited and highly competitive, with only the strongest candidates selected. For transparency, we recommend checking the Tuition Fees & Finances page to avoid unexpected financial challenges later in the process.

Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship?
Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships are financed by the legacy of Justus and Louise van Effen. Mr. van Effen studied at TU Delft in the 1940’s and strongly believed technological developments will contribute to solving societal issues. Awarded students will receive full tuition fees per year for a TU Delft MSc programme based on the Statutory or Institutional rate, according to the registered nationality, AND contribution for the living expenses.

Scholarships from other providers
Have you been awarded a scholarship from an external provider/organisation? Follow the steps below if you have been admitted to TU Delft.
If you have been admitted to TU Delft and have received a (full) scholarship from an external provider, follow these steps to submit it for TU Delft approval. Without an approval from TU Delft, you will need to transfer the amount in your financial letter before the payment deadline.
Requirements
Approval deadline
Additional Conditions
Requirements
Submit your scholarship letter before 1 June to contactcentre-esa@tudelft.nl , including your student number.
The letter must contain the following details:
Name of the scholarship provider
Date of issue
Student’s name (as shown on passport)
Date of birth
Start and end date of the scholarship (duration)
Programme of study at TU Delft
Amount of the scholarship
Email address for TU Delft to send the tuition fee invoice
Approval deadline
TU Delft must approve the scholarship letter by 15 June .
If the letter does not meet the IND’s requirements, the scholarship will be rejected, and you must transfer the fees and costs before 1 July :
Tuition fees
Living costs (as stated in the Financial Letter)
Additional Conditions
If the scholarship does not cover the full first-year living costs , you must transfer the remaining amount before 1 July .
All students must pay the required service fees (housing and residence permit/visa) before 1 July .
Tuition fees must be fully covered for the entire duration of the master’s programme which is 2 years . Scholarship providers should account for an annual tuition fee increase of 2–3% .
