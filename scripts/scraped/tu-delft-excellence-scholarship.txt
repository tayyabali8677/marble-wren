Scholarships
Delft University of Technology (TU Delft) aims to attract the world’s brightest students to its international classrooms. For that reason, it has established several scholarship programmes specifically designed to offer talented and motivated international students the opportunity to pursue a MSc degree at TU Delft.
Do you want to apply for a Scholarship?
Submit your complete MSc application with the mandatory scholarship documents (including 2 reference letters and scholarship application form) before the deadline.
Your MSc application will then be reviewed and an admission decision given; at that moment you will also be considered for a Scholarship.
Not sure about the deadline?
For the correct deadline check which deadline comes first ( MSc programme application deadline or Scholarship deadline).
The first deadline is leading. Candidates must not wait for an admission decision to submit a scholarship application.
Missed the deadline?
If you wanted to apply for a scholarship but have missed the first deadline, your scholarship application will not be considered.
FAQ
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Heads up!
Missing the first deadline means your scholarship application won’t be reviewed.
TU Delft MSc Programme Scholarships – Academic Period 2027–2029
Justus & Louise van Effen
Excellence Scholarships
Faculty specific scholarships, academic period 2027-2029
Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science
Faculty scholarship: Microelectronics
Number of scholarships Up to two faculty scholarships offered by the Microelectronics research department
Eligibility
Promising excellent international students and (conditionally) admitted to the 2-year Regular TU Delft’s MSc programme in Electrical Engineering (track Microelectronics).
With a cumulative grade point average (GPA) of 80 percent or higher of the scale maximum in the bachelor’s degree from an internationally renowned university outside The Netherlands.
Application deadline 1 December 2025 (23:59 CET)
Granting
Only students who have been granted a scholarship will be informed by TU Delft via email by April 2026 .
Receiving the scholarship is conditional on the student fulfilling all admission criteria.
Extent of Scholarship
Full tuition fees per year for a TU Delft MSc programme based on the statutory fee or institutional rate, according to the registered nationality.
Extension of the scholarship to the second year is subject to maintaining an average grade of 8 (80%) during the first year.
Students must choose an MSc thesis project in the Microelectronics Dept, and after receiving a 9 (90%) or higher for this project, they will be offered a PhD position in the Microelectronics Dept.
Procedure
ALL DOCUMENTS REQUIRED MUST BE SUBMITTED AT ONCE with the MSc Application !
1. Complete your MSc application (check the admission requirements )
2. Upload the Scholarship Application Form
3. Upload or request TWO reference letters :
You can upload your reference letters during your MSc application (as one PDF document), according to the indicated requirements .
And/or
You can request the reference letters from your referee(s). Fill out the information about your referee/s and invite them by using the ‘send invitation button’. The referee(s) will receive an automatic email inviting him/her to upload a reference for you.
You can combine both procedures. E.g. 1 upload and 1 referee request.
Reference letters will not be considered if you have already submitted your application.
Reference letters will not be accepted after the Scholarship deadline.
4. Upload the English language certificate
Please note that Non-EU/EFTA students must include their English test with their application (if required).
PLEASE NOTE
All documents required for the scholarship must be submitted together with the MSc Application.
After submission no additional documents can be uploaded

Giving to TU Delft is an investment in the future
The challenges of today demand solutions for tomorrow. How do we tackle climate change? How do we ensure a future powered by clean energy? How do we keep our digital world secure? The answers to these questions start with technological innovation, and TU Delft is at the forefront of these developments worldwide.
The Delft University Fund supports TU Delft by contributing to research, education, talent development, and entrepreneurship. With donations from alumni, students, foundations, and companies, we do everything we can to help talented students and researchers achieve their ambitions and make a societal impact.
Yes, I want to participate and donate.
By contributing to the Delft University Fund, you are investing in groundbreaking research, innovative education, and top talent at TU Delft, ensuring that Delft technology continues to play a key role in addressing the major challenges of our time.

Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships
At TU Delft a number of scholarships are available for excellent international applicants. Justus & Louise Van Effen Excellence Scholarships are financed by the legacy of Justus and Louise van Effen. Mr. van Effen studied at TU Delft in the 1940’s and strongly believed technological developments will contribute to solving societal issues.
The Foundation Justus & Louise van Effen was established with the aim of stimulating excellent international MSc students and financially supporting them in their wish to study at TU Delft.
Number of scholarships 2 per faculty
Eligibility
You are an excellent international applicant (conditionally) admitted to one of the 2-year regular TU Delft MSc programmes (not an International Joint Education Programme).
You are not eligible for the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships if:
You are a TU Delft bachelor’s student (even if you are an international student).
You are an international student applying for a TU Delft MSc programme who has obtained their bachelor's degree at a Dutch University.
You have already started your MSc studies at TU Delft (the scholarship is only for new, incoming students).
Students must have achieved excellent academic results in their previous study programme(s), which must be relevant to the Master's programme for which they are applying. As an indication, the student is amongst the top 10% of graduates in his/her previous study programme. Note that proof of ranking is not required – this information is purely to indicate the level of competitiveness.
You have submitted a complete MSc application before the scholarship deadline.
Application deadline 1 December 2026 (23:59 CET)
Granting
Students who have been granted a scholarship will be informed by TU Delft via email by the end of March 2027.
Receiving the scholarship is conditional on the student fulfilling all admission criteria.
Extent of Scholarship
Full tuition fees per year for a TU Delft MSc programme based on the statutory fee or institutional rate, according to the registered nationality, AND contribution for the living expenses.
Procedure
ALL DOCUMENTS REQUIRED MUST BE SUBMITTED AT ONCE with the MSc Application !
1. Complete your MSc application (check the admission requirements)
2. Upload the Scholarship Application Form
3. Upload two reference letters
o You can upload your reference letters during your MSc application (as one PDF document), according to the indicated requirement .
And/or
o You can request the reference letters from your referee(s). Fill out the information about your referee/s and invite them by using the ‘send invitation button’. The referee(s) will receive an automatic email inviting him/her to upload a reference for you.
o You can combine both procedures. E.g. 1 upload and 1 referee request.
o Reference letters will not be considered if you have already submitted your application.
o Reference letters will not be accepted after the Scholarship deadline.
4. Upload the English language certificate
Please note that all applicants who wish to be considered for a scholarship are required to include their English test with their MSc application before the Scholarship deadline application (if required).
PLEASE NOTE
All documents required for the scholarship must be submitted together with the MSc Application. After submission no additional documents can be uploaded.
Student Story
Ömer Taha Döver (24) is a master’s student at TU Delft, the Netherlands where he is pursuing a degree in Strategic Product Design. Originally from Istanbul, Turkey, he was awarded the Justus and Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship by the Justus and Louise van Effen Foundation. Before embarking on his graduate studies, Ömer earned a Bachelor's degree from Istanbul Technical University and gained valuable experience working as a strategist in purpose-driven innovation for a year. He also completed an exchange program at TU Delft during his undergraduate studies, which sparked his passion for design and innovation. Ömer's design work has been recognized with numerous awards & accolades, and has been featured in exhibitions by leading organizations in the design and innovation fields. He is committed to using his skills and knowledge to make a positive impact on society and create a sustainable future.

Student Story
Berkay Onur Türk (he/him/his) is an alumnus of TU Delft's Technology, Policy and Management faculty. Born in Turkey in 1997, Berkay received a full scholarship based on merit to pursue his high school studies at American Collegiate Institute (ACI) in his home country. He subsequently pursued a bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering, with a minor in Economics, at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul. During his undergraduate studies in 2019, Berkay spent six months as an exchange student at Eindhoven University of Technology in the Netherlands. Berkay returned to the Netherlands in 2020 as a recipient of Justus and Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship, which allowed him to pursue his master’s studies at TU Delft. While completing his master's degree in Complex Systems Engineering and Management, Berkay also worked as a student assistant at the faculty of Technology, Policy and Management. In addition, he was elected as a member of the Faculty Student Council (FSC) in his second year of studies. Upon graduating cum laude from the master's program, Berkay embarked on his career as a strategy consultant. However, his passion for advancing the impact of education on society led him to take on a new challenge as a Project Manager at Eindhoven University of Technology.

Student Story
Adalberto de Paula , hailing from Brazil and born in 1992, is an alumnus of TU Delft Faculty of Architecture & the Built Environment. His remarkable accomplishments during his bachelor's studies at the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul led him to earn the Justus and Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship. This achievement came after gaining four years of professional experience as an Architect in São Paulo. Throughout his undergraduate journey, he secured two additional scholarships that provided him with the opportunity to partake in architectural exchange programs at the University of Wisconsin Milwaukee, the University of New Mexico, and the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul. For his TU Delft master's degree in Building Technology, Adalberto delved into researching automated strategies for computational design and digital fabrication. This research aimed to facilitate the advancement of robotic construction methods for discrete timber components used in housing. Adalberto's design prowess has garnered him award nominations and acknowledgments while has been prominently featured in exhibitions at innovation-focused events associated with TU Delft. Upon completing his education, Adalberto is committed to leveraging his expertise to drive positive, circular impacts on society and contribute significantly to the establishment of a sustainable future.
