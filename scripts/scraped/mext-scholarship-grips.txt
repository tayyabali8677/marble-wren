What is GRIPS Fund?
At GRIPS we nurture mid-career public administrators who will play key roles globally. We also promote interdisciplinary policy studies to develop wide-ranging solutions to policy-related challenges. GRIPS Fund aims to provide support for the advancement of these educational and research activities at GRIPS.

These donations will be used to aid GRIPS in broader forms including financial support for students, for educational and research activities, and for facility management.

Admissions
■ GRIPS’ goal is to foster policy professionals who
・have the ability to examine existing policy issues, conduct in-depth policy analyses, and provide practical solutions for policy-making;
・wish to become highly skilled specialists or researchers of policy studies or policy-making leaders working in international organizations, governments in individual countries, and other areas.
For information on our degree programs, go to Degree Programs .
■ GRIPS application evaluation
Our application evaluation consists of document screenings and online interviews. Please refer to the Admissions Policy of each program to learn about target students, screening methods and standards.
■ Before you apply
Check each of the web pages linked below for details about the program’s application procedures.
For information about application process, go to How to Apply .
For information about funding including scholarships, go to Scholarships / Tuition and Fees .
For information about deadlines, go to Application Deadlines .
For questions about application procedures and pre-admission matters, go to Application FAQs and Pre-arrival FAQs .
For information for current GRIPS students about academic and student life at GRIPS, go to GRIPS Bulletin ( Academic information and Student Life Guide ).
■ Number of applicants, successful candidates, and entrants by program for 2026 Admission　( PDF )
■ For information on our student demographics, including gender and nationality, please visit our Facts & Figures web page.
■ Support Systems for Study at GRIPS
Many of our international students receive scholarships from MEXT or international organizations (Scholarship for Foreign Nationals Scholarships / Tuition and Fees ). There are also scholarships for Japanese students (Scholarship for Japanese 奨学金 ).
■ Advance Consultation for Applicants with Disabilities
We provide reasonable accommodation for applicants with disabilities so that they will not be disadvantaged in the examination process or during their course of study.
We always accept consultation requests from those in need of reasonable accommodation regarding admissions.
Please note that depending on the situation, it may take some time for us to respond to such queries. In principle, e-mail consultation (contact info below) should be conducted at least one month prior to the relevant application deadline.
Special arrangements can be made in accordance with individual symptoms and conditions. Please note that depending on the nature of your request, we may not always be able to make arrangements exactly as requested, or we may offer an alternative. We assure that you will not be disadvantaged by your request.
Contact; Admissions Office
Email: admissions grips.ac.jp
[Example of reasonable accommodation]
Permission to bring and use magnifiers, hearing aids, canes, etc.
Permission to be accompanied by an attendant before the start of the interview
Extension of examination time (typically 1.3 times the standard time), etc.

Scholarships
We have a number of scholarships for which prospective GRIPS international students may apply; some of these full scholarships are available for outstanding applicants. Below is a simplified list of the scholarships that applicants may be matched with.
Scholarships at GRIPS are divided into the following three types based on application method:
(a) apply directly to GRIPS
(b) apply through a sponsoring organization
(c) apply to both the sponsoring organization and directly to GRIPS
For further details on each scholarship provider, see Scholarship Eligibility and Details .
Please review this information to learn more about these funding opportunities.

*JICA Scholarship:
If you are a candidate for MP1/2, PF(Tax), G-cube, PA or STI, you must be instructed by JICA to apply to GRIPS. Please begin your process by contacting the department in your organization responsible for overseas study to confirm that employees from your organization are eligible to apply for the JICA Scholarship.
Note: please do not contact the JICA office in your area directly.
** Please note the following update (September 29, 2026): MEXT plans to discontinue calls for applications for the MEXT Scholarship (General Category) starting with the 2026 application cycle. Although the formal decision is expected to be made toward the end of the year, following the Cabinet’s approval of the annual budget, MEXT has advised us to proceed on the assumption that the discontinuation will be implemented. Accordingly, for the 2027 intake, the MEXT Scholarships offered by GRIPS will be available only to doctoral students in the GRIPS Global Governance Program (G-cube, 5-year program) and the Policy Analysis Program (PA, 5-year program), and to master’s students in the One-year Master’s Program of Public Policy (MP1) and the Two-year Master’s Program of Public Policy (MP2).

NOTE: If you are;
(a) currently residing in Japan; or
(b) residing in a country other than Japan and plan to move to Japan in the near future,
you may be considered a domestic applicant.
