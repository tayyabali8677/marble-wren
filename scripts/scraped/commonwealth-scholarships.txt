## The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC) provides the main UK government scholarship scheme led by international development objectives.

![Image 1](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/csc-23FEB24-1027-CR2-scaled-1.jpg)

## Who we are

The CSC is an executive non-departmental public body, sponsored by the **[Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/foreign-commonwealth-development-office)**.

The CSC operates within the framework of the Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan (CSFP) and is a vivid demonstration of the UK’s enduring commitment to the Commonwealth.

![Image 2](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/IMG_2789.jpg)

## What we do

To date, over 34,000 people have taken up Commonwealth Scholarships since the first group of 175 Scholars arrived in the UK in 1960. Since then the scheme has constantly developed, adapting to the very different world of the present. **[You can read more about what we do in the CSC’s impact report](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Building-a-Common-Future-CSC-Impact-Report.pdf "https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Building-a-Common-Future-CSC-Impact-Report.pdf").**

## Our development impact

The CSC champions sustainable development and supports Commonwealth Scholars to drive innovation, lead change, and advance knowledge in their home countries and beyond.

By building a community of Scholars, Fellows, and Alumni who can use their expertise to address development challenges, the CSC aims to create a fairer, more prosperous, and sustainable world for all.
