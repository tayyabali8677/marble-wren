# Fellowships

![Image 2: Student biking past Sage Hall](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/1200_07_195_pano_select-1000x400.jpg)
A fellowship provides financial support to graduate students to pursue graduate studies without associated teaching or research responsibilities. Fellowships are generally merit-based internal or external awards to support a student in a full-time course of study. We offer [fellowship workshops](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/external-fellowships-and-grants/resources-and-workshops/), boot camps, consultation, and review sessions to help you in your pursuit of a fellowship.

The Graduate School funds a significant number of entering doctoral students with fellowships awarded through their graduate field. Explore the list below to learn more.

*   [New Student Fellowships](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/new-student-fellowships/)
*   [Continuing Student Fellowships](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/continuing-student-fellowships/)
*   [External Fellowships](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/external-fellowships/)
*   [NSF GRFP](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/nsf-grfp-fellowship/)

The Graduate School at the University of California, Los Angeles maintains one of the most extensive and up-to-date university databases on graduate school funding opportunities. Their database is called GRAPES and can be accessed through the [UCLA funding website](https://grad.ucla.edu/funding).

[Pathways to Science](https://pathwaystoscience.org/) is a portal managed by the Institute for Broadening Participation and includes funding, research, internship, and outreach opportunities in STEM for current and prospective pre-college, undergraduate, and graduate students as well as postdoctoral scholars.
