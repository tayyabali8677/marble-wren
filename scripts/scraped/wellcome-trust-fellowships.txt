Our strategic programmes
Discovery Research
Research in a wide range of disciplines generating new insights into life, health and wellbeing. Read what we do and don’t fund in discovery research
Climate and Health
Research that generates evidence of how people’s health is affected by climate change and by actions to address it. Learn more about our climate and health work
Infectious Disease
Research to inform and accelerate interventions that reduce the impact of infectious diseases. Learn more about our infectious disease work
Mental Health
Research to better understand anxiety, depression and psychosis and develop new ways to intervene earlier. Read what we do and don’t fund in mental health
Funding opportunities
We’re spending £16 billion in the decade to 2032 on a range of activities to enable discoveries and advance solutions for urgent health challenges. Our funding supports early-career, mid-career and established researchers across a broad scope of research, including physical and social sciences, clinical research and humanities.
