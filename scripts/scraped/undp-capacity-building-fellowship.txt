### [About us](https://www.undp.org/about-us)

UNDP works in about 170 countries and territories, helping to eradicate poverty, reduce inequalities and exclusion, and build resilience so countries can sustain progress. As the UN’s development agency, UNDP plays a critical role in helping countries achieve the Sustainable Development Goals.

### [Sustainable Development Goals](https://www.undp.org/sustainable-development-goals)

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), otherwise known as the Global Goals, are a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet and ensure that all people enjoy peace and prosperity.

### [Human Development Report](http://hdr.undp.org/)

Human development is about expanding the richness of human life, rather than simply the richness of the economy in which human beings live. It is an approach that is focused on people and their opportunities and choices.

### [#FutureSmartUNDP](https://strategicplan.undp.org/)

To meet the objectives of the Strategic Plan in line with the 2030 Agenda, UNDP is implementing six cross-cutting approaches to development, known as Signature Solutions. A strong, integrated way of putting our best work, or 'distinctive' skill set, to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals.

mission

#### UNDP works in 170 countries and territories to eradicate poverty while protecting the planet. We help countries develop strong policies, skills, partnerships and institutions so they can sustain their progress.

## What we do

UNDP is the United Nations’ lead agency on international development. We support countries and communities as they work to eradicate poverty, implement the Paris Agreement on climate change and achieve the Sustainable Development Goals. We advocate for transformative change, and we connect countries to the resources they need to help people build a better life.

[Learn more](https://www.undp.org/expertise "Expertise")

![Image 34: Construction worker in a neon safety vest and knit cap grips a lifting hook under blue scaffolding.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/human-development-healthy-planet.jpg?h=a1e1a043&itok=OLa19iHz)

### Human development on a healthy planet

UNDP works with countries to expand people’s choices for a future where prosperity and sustainability advance together, leaving no one behind. Through integrated, evidence-driven action aligned with national priorities, UNDP works to accelerate human development while easing pressures on the planet.

![Image 35: Photograph of four smiling children posing among rocky ruins under a bright blue sky.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/undp-af-prosperity_for_all.jpg?h=4521fff0&itok=LcM7xy8F)

### Prosperity for all

UNDP focusses on creating prosperity for all, while maintaining our strong footprint in poverty and inequality programming. This shift aligns with our partners' changing aspirations: moving beyond the goalpost of a minimum poverty line to build dynamic, resilient economies and societies where everyone can thrive.

![Image 36: Person in a light blue shirt holds a card with a circular logo outdoors; crowd and trees in back.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/effective_governance_pnud-mali-2018_election_01.jpg?h=4176ce04&itok=oVHSqei2)

### Effective governance

UNDP develops accountable, people-centred and rights-based governance. Using systems thinking, innovation and strategic foresight, UNDP helps countries develop more agile, anticipatory governance and policymaking and provide responsive services and economic opportunities, including increased access to services through digitalization and AI.

![Image 37: Two construction workers on a dirt site with chalk markings; one points, rock piles behind.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/crisis_resilience_undp_afghanistan_afghanistan_earthquake_102023_crisis_shelter_disaster-18.jpg?h=a1e1a043&itok=InHEGSDh)

### Crisis resilience

From day one of a crisis, UNDP focuses on protecting development gains and reducing risk, laying the groundwork for recovery and eventual exit from reliance on humanitarian assistance. UNDP acts as a “bridge-builder” between the humanitarian, development and peacebuilding systems, helping to maintain continuity across response phases and advance long-term development.

![Image 38: Person in a cornfield holds a round woven basket of yellow corn.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/healthy_planet_undp_sl_mariama_gendemeh_climate-smart_farm.jpg?h=b74a9fa5&itok=JjUR7omy)

### Healthy planet

UNDP is a leading connector at the nexus of planet and development, delivering integrated solutions on climate change, disasters, biodiversity loss, energy, chemicals and waste, while advancing development priorities. Translating global commitments into effective local action, we support countries to assess their options for achieving just and sustainable transitions.

![Image 39: Photograph of hands holding a smartphone displaying a colorful app interface.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/digital_and_ai_53815313574_422e1dcc35_o.jpg?h=8b23b808&itok=7WsWP8Ox)

### Digital and AI transformation

UNDP’s broad expertise and country-driven approach allow us to support holistic digital transformation in an integrated, systemic way. UNDP has worked with over 120 countries to leverage digitalization, including helping institutions and people develop the capabilities to access and use technology and data; and supporting countries to implement robust digital public infrastructure.

![Image 40: Three costumed performers on a wooden porch, center in a white headdress, brown-gold outfits.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/undp-kyrgyzstan-2018_strong_women_32511973338.jpg?h=99d542f5&itok=Peb0j2TG)

### Gender equality

Complementing the work of UN Women, UNDP designs its interventions for a deliberate impact on gender equality, seen by partners as one of its most effective areas of work. Through its trusted relationships with governments, UNDP is well placed to help advance women’s empowerment across all dimensions of development.

![Image 41: Market fruit stand with a shirtless vendor serving fruit to a customer, surrounded by melons.](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/styles/flu/public/2026-01/sustainable_finance_undp-seychelles-victoria-infrastructure-people-streets-36.jpg?h=c6980913&itok=25a-FjBD)

### Sustainable finance

UNDP works to accelerate development and expand investment opportunities in emerging and developing economies. We help countries mobilize domestic resources and strengthen their institutional frameworks, making financial governance more stable and transparent. We work with development finance institutions and the private sector to scale financing instruments such as blended finance, green bonds and insurance.
