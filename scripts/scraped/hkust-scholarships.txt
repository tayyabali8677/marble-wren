Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)

 HKUST has the largest HKPFS awardee community in Hong Kong, coming from **50+** countries and regions.

HKUST RedBird PhD Award (for International Students)

Recruitment Award for new PhD students: **HK$40,000** (~**US$5,130**) and a waiver of tuition fee in the first year.  
  
 Academic Excellence Award for current PhD students: **HK$20,000** (~**US$2,560**) per year, subject to academic performance.

 Scholarships covering **tuition, lodging and daily subsistence costs** throughout the normative study period.
