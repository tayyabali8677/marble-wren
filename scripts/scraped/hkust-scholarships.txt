## **Extensive**Scholarships

Our priority is not only to provide the best education and research opportunities, but also the means for that to be achieved. Financial concerns should not be a hurdle for you to pursue your dreams.

![Image 34: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/styles/fytgs_homepage_scholarship/public/2021-08/pic1.png?itok=jNw33BfJ)

![Image 35: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/2021-08/icon1%20.svg)

Postgraduate Studentship (PGS)

Up to **2 years** for full-time MPhil students and **4 years** for full-time PhD students.

**HK$229,620**/year (~**US$29,420**/year)

![Image 36: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/styles/fytgs_homepage_scholarship/public/2021-08/pic2.png?itok=v-fsvL1k)

![Image 37: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/2021-08/icon2.svg)

Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)

It is an **internationally recognized** award to draw the best PhD students around the globe to Hong Kong.

HKUST has the largest HKPFS awardee community in Hong Kong, coming from **50+** countries and regions.

![Image 38: RedBird](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/styles/fytgs_homepage_scholarship/public/2021-10/10.jpg?itok=eY-xIqRj)

![Image 39: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/2021-08/icon3.svg)

HKUST RedBird PhD Award (for International Students)

Recruitment Award for new PhD students: **HK$40,000** (~**US$5,130**) and a waiver of tuition fee in the first year.

Academic Excellence Award for current PhD students: **HK$20,000** (~**US$2,560**) per year, subject to academic performance.

![Image 40: HKPFS](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/styles/fytgs_homepage_scholarship/public/2021-10/HKPFS_20201120%20-1-.jpg?itok=vqbx70P7)

![Image 41: scholarship](https://pg.ust.hk/sites/default/files/2021-08/icon4.svg)

Asian Future Leaders Scholarship Program (AFLSP)

It is a scholarship for new students from East, Southeast and South Asia to pursue master's studies and cross-cultural enrichment.

Scholarships covering **tuition, lodging and daily subsistence costs** throughout the normative study period.
