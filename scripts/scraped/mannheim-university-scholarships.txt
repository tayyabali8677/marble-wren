Preventative shutdown of central IT services (security measure)
Due to current, critical security vulnerabilities in the
web technology PHP , potentially vulnerable IT systems at
the University of Mannheim have been taken offline. This is purely a
precautionary measure to ensure the security of the
University of Mannheim's data and IT infrastructure.
As a result, the following services, among others, are currently unavailable:
University of Mannheim Website
University of Mannheim Intranet
ILIAS ( Available again )
Exam ILIAS ( Available again )
bwSync&Share
Accomodation Bureau
Online Application
Language Tandem
EvaSys
Engage application platform eJourney ( Available again )
Portal² will continue to be available.
When will the systems be back online?
A security update from the manufacturer has been announced for later this
afternoon. As the University is dependent on the manufacturer's release, unfortunately
no exact time can be given at present for when the services will be
available again.
We ask for your understanding regarding this necessary security measure.
This statement was published on 21.10.2026 . Last updated: 6:10 p.m.
