Turn2us Edinburgh Trust
Turn2us Edinburgh Trust has been working alongside communities in the city since 2011 to tackle financial insecurity. By partnering with organisations across Edinburgh, we connect people to cash grants, information and practical support through our Edinburgh Together programme.
We strengthen networks across local organisations, public services and communities to reduce stigma and tackle the root causes of financial insecurity.
But our work goes beyond immediate grant-making.
Through our Thriving Futures Fund, we offer plans that combine cash grants with tools to build long-term financial security.
Our investment in the Regenerative Futures Fund aims to test resident-led models of funding that strengthen local networks of support and tackle the deeper causes of financial insecurity.
And our co-production work puts lived experience at the heart of decision-making, guiding our priorities and funding approaches.
Together with partners and residents, we're creating a more connected Edinburgh where everyone can access the support they need to thrive.
Contacting the Edinburgh Trust
Edinburgh Trust grants are not open to direct applications. All applications must be made through one of our Community of Partners organisations, as part of the support they provide.
If you are not already receiving support from one of our partners, you will not be able to apply for a grant from us at this time.
If you have applied for a grant via our Community of Partners and are looking for an update on your application, please contact the person who made the application for/with you.
If you are looking for financial support for yourself, you can search for grants and other support available to you via our Get Support page .
If you are an organisation, please read more about our Community of Partners .

Add your charity to the Grants Search
Can my organisation register to be listed?
If your charity/organisation offers direct financial support to individuals in financial need, please register below to appear on our Grants Search. This will allow people who might benefit from your help to find you.
Please note: we can only list charities in the UK that give financial grants to individuals in need.
We currently list the following on the Grants Search:
Not-for-profit organisations that offer financial assistance - directly or indirectly - to people in financial hardship.
Utility providers, such as water, gas and electric companies that offer support for their customers or the public. There must be no commercial obligation on the recipient in order to receive help.
What are the advantages of being listed?
Being on the Turn2us Grants Search is an easy and free way to reach more people that might be eligible for your help.
We ask users to answer a series of questions before showing them the results. This means that only people who have actively selected a category that your organisation is tagged against will see your details.

Grants Search FAQs
Is it free for my organisation to be on the Turn2us Grants Search?
Yes, it is free of charge to add your charity or organisation on the Turn2us Grants Search.
How do I register to be included on the Turn2us Grants Search?
Please contact us to register onto the Turn2us Grants Search .
Once we receive the completed registration form, we will contact you regarding the setting up of your charity user account. This may take up to five working days.
How do I log in to my Turn2us charity account?
You can log in via the Myturn2us login page .
If you are having trouble logging in, don't hesitate to contact us .
Who can access the charity account?
As a default, you will have a ‘provider user’ from your organisation.
They will have the login details for your charity account and will be able to make changes online.
It is possible to change the name, email address and password for this person via the account.
You can also add and delete users so that multiple people from your organisation can access the account.
What features are available within the Turn2us charity account?
From your charity account, you will be able to:
Manage your profile: this includes adding new users, update your login details, and remove your organisation from the Grants Search.
Update your profile: this includes updating your organisation’s contact details and updating your fund’s description.
How do I update my organisation&#x27;s contact details?
Start by logging in to your charity account .
Once you have done this, you will see the option to ‘update organisation contact details’ in the MyTurn2us navigation bar.
You will be prompted to fill out a form with your new contact details.
Can I update the funds and add new ones myself?
You can update existing charitable funds by going to the update your scheme tab within your charity account and selecting the fund/scheme you want to update.
By selecting ‘edit’, you will be able to change the name, description fields and status of your fund, as well as adding your own custom questions.
You can add a new charitable fund to the Grants Search by going to the update your scheme tab within your charity account and selecting ‘create new scheme’.

Turn2us Edinburgh Trust
Turn2us Edinburgh Trust has been working alongside communities in the city since 2011 to tackle financial insecurity. By partnering with organisations across Edinburgh, we connect people to cash grants, information and practical support through our Edinburgh Together programme.
We strengthen networks across local organisations, public services and communities to reduce stigma and tackle the root causes of financial insecurity.
But our work goes beyond immediate grant-making.
Through our Thriving Futures Fund, we offer plans that combine cash grants with tools to build long-term financial security.
Our investment in the Regenerative Futures Fund aims to test resident-led models of funding that strengthen local networks of support and tackle the deeper causes of financial insecurity.
And our co-production work puts lived experience at the heart of decision-making, guiding our priorities and funding approaches.
Together with partners and residents, we're creating a more connected Edinburgh where everyone can access the support they need to thrive.
Contacting the Edinburgh Trust
Edinburgh Trust grants are not open to direct applications. All applications must be made through one of our Community of Partners organisations, as part of the support they provide.
If you are not already receiving support from one of our partners, you will not be able to apply for a grant from us at this time.
If you have applied for a grant via our Community of Partners and are looking for an update on your application, please contact the person who made the application for/with you.
If you are looking for financial support for yourself, you can search for grants and other support available to you via our Get Support page .
If you are an organisation, please read more about our Community of Partners .
