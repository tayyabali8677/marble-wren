Expert insights on displacement in times of polycrisis
The displacement of people in Africa is increasingly shaped by overlapping crises. Drawing on consultations with 20 experts, this policy note identifies key areas for more effective responses, including how Nordic and international actors can support stronger African leadership and context-sensitive solutions.
