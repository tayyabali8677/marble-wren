Connect with Colleges and Scholarships
Colleges and scholarship programs are looking for students like you. Get noticed by joining Student Search Service.
Sign in to Connect
Create an Account to Connect
Why Choose Student Search Service?
Hear directly from colleges across the country
More than 1,200 colleges in the U.S. rely on Student Search to find the right students for their campuses.
Get Access to Millions in Scholarship Dollars
Scholarship partners and colleges use the data to help find candidates for their scholarships.
Your Privacy Is Prioritized
We only share specific information about you, with your consent. We never share your actual test scores, grades, disability status, or phone number. Visit here for a full list of information about you shared with participating nonprofit organizations, if you opt in.
No Commercial Advertising
Only approved nonprofit accredited colleges and universities (domestic and international), nonprofit scholarship providers, and government agencies administering educational programs can participate. Learn more here .
How to Join and Make It Work for You
Log in or Create an Account 📲
If you’ve taken the SAT or an AP Exam, you probably already have a College Board account. If not, signing up only takes a few minutes—quick and easy!
Make Your Profile You 🌟
Colleges and scholarship programs want to connect with students who stand out. Keeping your profile updated with your interests and background helps match you with opportunities that fit you best.
Keep an Eye on Your Inbox (and Mailbox) 📬
Your next scholarship or college opportunity could be waiting in your messages. Stay on top of them so you don’t miss out!
What Information Is Shared
When you join the Student Search Service, you agree to share with participating organizations certain information about you, including your College Board account information, exam score ranges, BigFuture college list, and details about your high school performance and college preferences. Participating nonprofit colleges, scholarship providers, and government agencies use this information to identify students who may be a good fit for their programs. The service is free for students, though organizations may pay College Board a licensing fee to access it.

Start Your College List
Opens: December of Sophomore Year
About This Step
Students who build a college list with three or more colleges on BigFuture will earn monthly entries for $500 and $40,000 scholarship drawings. The earlier you build your list, the more chances you’ll have at winning. ​
It’s never too early to begin looking for the college that's best for you. There are thousands of colleges in the United States. BigFuture can help you find yours.
How to Qualify
Login to your College Board account.
Click Qualify Now.
Search for colleges on BigFuture.
Add 3 or more schools to your list .
Dates and Deadlines
Scholarships for $500 and one for $40,000 are awarded monthly until winter of your senior year. Completing any step enters you in the drawings on the first day of every month.
Class of 2026 Deadline to Complete This step: Monday, June 30, 2025
Class of 2027 Deadline to Complete This Step: Tuesday, June 30, 2026
Class of 2028 Deadline to Complete This Step: Wednesday, June 30, 2027
Increase Your Chances
Students whose families earn less than $60,000 a year have double the chances of winning a scholarship with two entries earned per step completed. Half of all awards are reserved for students who meet this income criterion.

Start Your Career List​
Opens: Sophomore Year
About This Step
Starting December of their sophomore year, students who save a list with three or more careers will earn entries into monthly drawings for $500 and $40,000. The earlier you start your list, the more chances you’ll have at winning.
To start, take our short career quiz . It takes just a few minutes to answer questions about how you like to spend your time and what interests you most. After the quiz, you’ll receive a list of 30 potential careers to explore and add to your list.
You can also explore careers in different clusters, such as health sciences or marketing, or simply explore over 1,000 career profiles .
How to Qualify​
Login to your College Board account .
Take the Career Quiz or explore over 1,000 careers profiles.
Add three or more careers to your list .
Dates and Deadlines
Scholarships for $500 and $40,000 are awarded monthly until winter of your senior year. Completing any step enters you in the drawings on the first day of every month. Drawings occur on the first day of every month.
Class of 2027 Deadline to Complete This Step: Monday, November 30, 2026
Class of 2028 Deadline to Complete This Step: Tuesday, November 30, 2027
Class of 2029 Deadline to Complete This Step: Thursday, November 30, 2028
Increase Your Chances
Students whose families earn less than $60,000 a year have double the chances of winning a scholarship with two entries earned per step completed. Half of all awards are reserved for students who meet this income criterion.
