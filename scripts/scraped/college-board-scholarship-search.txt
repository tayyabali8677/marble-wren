Why Choose Student Search Service?
Hear directly from colleges across the country
More than 1,200 colleges in the U.S. rely on Student Search to find the right students for their campuses.
Get Access to Millions in Scholarship Dollars
Scholarship partners and colleges use the data to help find candidates for their scholarships.
Your Privacy Is Prioritized
We only share specific information about you, with your consent. We never share your actual test scores, grades, disability status, or phone number. Visit here for a full list of information about you shared with participating nonprofit organizations, if you opt in.
No Commercial Advertising
Only approved nonprofit accredited colleges and universities (domestic and international), nonprofit scholarship providers, and government agencies administering educational programs can participate. Learn more here .
How to Join and Make It Work for You
Log in or Create an Account 📲
If you’ve taken the SAT or an AP Exam, you probably already have a College Board account. If not, signing up only takes a few minutes—quick and easy!
Make Your Profile You 🌟
Colleges and scholarship programs want to connect with students who stand out. Keeping your profile updated with your interests and background helps match you with opportunities that fit you best.
Keep an Eye on Your Inbox (and Mailbox) 📬
Your next scholarship or college opportunity could be waiting in your messages. Stay on top of them so you don’t miss out!
What Information Is Shared
When you join the Student Search Service, you agree to share with participating organizations certain information about you, including your College Board account information, exam score ranges, BigFuture college list, and details about your high school performance and college preferences. Participating nonprofit colleges, scholarship providers, and government agencies use this information to identify students who may be a good fit for their programs. The service is free for students, though organizations may pay College Board a licensing fee to access it.

Strengthen Your College List
Opens: July Before Senior year
About This Step
Students who strengthen (or update) their college list to have at least three reach colleges, two match colleges, and one safety will earn entries in monthly drawings for $500 and $40,000 scholarships.
The goal is to help students like you make sure your list has a range of schools to consider for your future. You can use your SAT or ACT scores or your GPA to determine which schools are right for you.
How to Qualify
Login to your College Board account.
Click "Qualify Now" above.
Review your college list to make sure it has least 3 reach colleges, 2 match colleges, and 1 safety college.
If you’re missing any category, simply click “Start Now” above to see how your GPA, SAT, or ACT scores compare with students at different universities. From there, you’ll be able to see which schools are reach, match, or safety for you.
Dates and Deadlines​
Scholarships for $500 and $40,000 are awarded monthly until February of your senior year. Completing any step enters you in the drawings on the first day of every month. Drawings occur on the first day of every month. ​
Class of 2026 Deadline to Complete This Step: Friday, October 31, 2025
Class of 2027 Deadline to Complete This Step: Saturday, October 31, 2026
Class of 2028 Deadline to Complete This Step: Sunday, October 31, 2027
Increase Your Chances
Students whose families earn less than $60,000 a year have double the chances of winning a scholarship with two entries earned per step completed. Half of all awards are reserved for students who meet this income criterion.

Complete the FAFSA
Opens: October of Senior Year
About This Step
Filling out the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA®) is the most important step to pay for college.
Students who verify they completed the FAFSA during their senior year will earn an entry into monthly BigFuture scholarship drawings for $500 and $40,000. Students have until the winter of their senior year to confirm that they completed this step. Keep in mind that states and colleges have separate deadlines for FAFSA completion.
How to Qualify
Take action to unlock financial aid:
Submit the FAFSA . Watch for your FAFSA Submission Summary by email or mail.
Alternatively, submit your state’s financial aid form. ( Check here or talk to your counselor to see if your state has a state aid form you can submit.)
Log in to BigFuture. On the dashboard, confirm your qualifications for the drawing.
Confirm that you completed the FAFSA, submitted a state aid form, or are legally ineligible to submit both the FAFSA and a state aid form.
After confirming that any of these apply to you, you’ll be automatically entered into monthly drawings for $500 and $40,000 scholarships unless you opt out of participation.
Dates and Deadlines
Scholarships for $500 and $40,000 are awarded monthly until winter of your senior year.Completing any step enters you in the drawings on the first day of every month.
Class of 2026 Deadline to Complete This Step: Saturday, February 28, 2026
Class of 2027 Deadline to Complete This Step: Monday, November 30, 2026
Class of 2028 Deadline to Complete This Step: Tuesday, November 30, 2027
Increase Your Chances
Students whose families earn less than $60,000 a year have double the chances of winning a scholarship with two entries earned per step completed. Half of all awards are reserved for students who meet this income criterion.
