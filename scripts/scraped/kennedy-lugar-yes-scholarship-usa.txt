YES PROGRAM: SPRING SEMESTER 2027
High school students from countries of strategic importance around the world live and study in the United States for a spring semester 2027 (January – June) through the U.S. Department of State’s Kennedy-Lugar Youth Exchange and Study (YES) program. Learn more about the YES program.
