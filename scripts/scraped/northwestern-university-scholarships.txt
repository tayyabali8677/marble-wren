Consumer Information
Current students may use the upload tool below to submit completed financial aid documents securely to our office.
First-year and transfer applicants should only use this tool for documents that cannot be submitted via IDOC. Please refer to your&#160; application procedures &#160;for more information.
Instructions:
Please follow the instructions on the submission form to upload your documents.
Documents must be high-resolution and fully legible. Most document types are acceptable to upload.&#160;Please do not password protect your documents.
Once uploaded, you will see a message that says "Thank you for submitting your form."
You will receive an email confirmation/receipt. Please allow at least 2-3 business days for your CAESAR checklist to be updated.
If you have any questions, contact our office .

Consumer Information
Follow the steps below to apply for financial aid. Once all materials are received, the Financial Aid Office will review your application to determine your aid eligibility. You will be notified of your aid package shortly after or alongside your admission decision.&#160;
For more information on how we determine aid eligibility, see the Aid Basics & Eligibility section.
If you wish only to apply for federal aid, you need only to complete the FAFSA.
Task
Submit as early as
Submit no later than
Instructions
Complete Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)*
October 1
February 1
The FAFSA determines eligibility for federal student aid.
Visit the FAFSA website and complete the application. Northwestern&#8217;s federal school code is 001739. Additional Information for Individuals Without a Social Security Number (SSN)
In most years, the FAFSA is available October 1. Things will be delayed slightly this year due to a simpler application being implemented.
Complete College Scholarship Service (CSS Profile)
October 1
February 1
The CSS Profile determines eligibility for university aid.
Visit the College Board website , to complete the Profile. Northwestern&#8217;s CSS code is 1565.
Upon completion, you will receive a confirmation email. Any corrections must be submitted to our office.
If your biological/adoptive parents are divorced or separated, your custodial and your noncustodial parent will each complete a separate Profile application. If you have no contact with your noncustodial parent, contact our office .
Submit Tax Materials&#160;
October 5
February 5
After completing the CSS Profile, the College Board will provide instructions for submitting the custodial parents&#8217; and the student&#8217;s federal tax returns and&#160;W-2s.
These signed forms must be sent to the College Board&#8217;s Institutional Documentation Service (IDOC), not the Office of Undergraduate Financial Aid.
Those not required to file federal tax returns should complete the appropriate Non-Tax Filer's Statement on the IDOC site and , upon request, may be required to later send a written explanation of your circumstances to our office.
Your Award Package
If you have financial need, your financial aid package typically includes a combination of scholarship or grant assistance, student loans,&#160;and student employment.
If you have been offered a loan, you will receive information in June with instructions on how to apply.
If you would like to submit information about a change in your financial situation, please refer to our Special Circumstances Review webpage for instructions.

Follow the steps below to apply for financial aid. Once all materials are received, the Financial Aid Office will review your application to determine aid eligibility. You will be notified of your aid package shortly after your admission decision.
For more information on how we determine aid eligibility, see the Aid Basics & Eligibility section.
The 2026-27 FAFSA is available and please establish your ID at the FAFSA Site: FAFSA . A separate ID is required for yourself and for other persons providing information. Please review the information on the FAFSA site to see whom is required to provide information.
If you wish only to apply for federal aid, you need only to complete the FAFSA.

Submit no later than
Instructions
Complete Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)*
October 1
December 1
The FAFSA determines your eligibility for federal student aid.
Visit the FAFSA website and complete the application. Northwestern&#8217;s federal school code is 001739.
Complete College Scholarship Service Profile (CSS)
October 1
December 1
The CSS Profile helps determine your eligibility for institutional aid.
Visit the College Board website , register and complete the Profile. Northwestern&#8217;s CSS code is 1565.
Once processed, you will receive a Data Confirmation Report. Any corrections must be submitted directly to our office.
If your biological/adoptive parents are divorced or separated, your custodial and your noncustodial parent will each need to complete a separate Profile application. If you have no contact with your noncustodial parent, contact our office .
Submit Tax Materials&#160;
November 1
December 5
After completing the CSS Profile, the College Board will provide instructions for submitting the custodial parents&#8217; and the student&#8217;s federal tax returns and W-2s.
These signed forms must be sent to the College Board&#8217;s Institutional Documentation Service (IDOC), not the Office of Undergraduate Financial Aid.
Those not required to file federal tax returns should complete the appropriate Non-Tax Filer's Statement &#160;and, upon request, may be required to later send a written explanation of your circumstances to our office.
Your Award Package
If you have financial need, your financial aid package might include a combination of scholarship or grant assistance, student loans,&#160;and student employment.
If you have been offered a loan, you will receive information in June with instructions on how to apply.
If you would like to submit information regarding a change in your financial situation, please refer to our Special Circumstances Review webpage for instructions.
Upon being admitted to the Early Decision program, the expectation is that you will withdraw any other admission applications and deposit at Northwestern by February 1. While this is considered a binding contract, there is an exception in cases of financial hardship. If the financial aid award makes it impossible for you to attend, please petition the director of financial aid for release from the Early Decision contract.
