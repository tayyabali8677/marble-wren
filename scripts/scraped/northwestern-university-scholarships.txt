## Need-Based Aid Packages

You’ll have access to a wide range of financial aid resources. In addition to these, you can explore several financing options to help support your educational journey.

### [How Aid Works](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/how-aid-works/index.html)

Understand how our aid is decided and what eligibility looks like.

### [Estimate Your Cost and Aid](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/how-aid-works/estimate-your-cost-aid/index.html)

Use our Quick Cost Estimator or Net Price Calculator tools to see what you can expect to pay.

### [Scholarships and Grants](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/types-of-aid/scholarships-grants/index.html)

You might qualify for federal, state, institutional and outside scholarships that do not need to be repaid.

### [Work-Study](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/types-of-aid/work-study.html)

Earn funds for books and personal expenses while gaining work experience.

### [Aid for Service Members](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/types-of-aid/aid-for-service-members/index.html)

Support is available for qualifying students to use toward the cost of their programs.

## We’re Here to Help

Weighing the costs of college is a highly personal process, so we invite you to reach out and ask us questions.

### Office Hours

8:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.  
Financial aid counselors are available by phone, email and online during regular office hours when the University is open.

[Contact and Appointments](https://undergradaid.northwestern.edu/help-support/contact-appointments/index.html)

### Walk-In Availability

Counselors are available for walk-ins from 9:00 a.m. – 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. Families may also schedule a virtual appointment with a counselor.
