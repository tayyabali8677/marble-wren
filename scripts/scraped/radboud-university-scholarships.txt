ASML Technology Scholarship
ASML Technology Scholarship
The ASML Technology Scholarship supports outstanding students who are pursuing a technical Master's degree. The scholarship offers €5,000 per academic year (for two years), along with access to a personal ASML mentor and a professional development programme. Students also participate in technology outreach projects and gain valuable insights into one of the world's leading technology companies.
Type
Scholarship
Eligible countries or regions
All EU/EEA countries, All non-EU/non-EEA countries
Duration of scholarship
2 years
Coverage of scholarship
Partial scholarship
Faculty involved
Faculty of Science, Faculty of Social Sciences
Conditions
Students at Radboud University who are going to follow one of the following master's programmes may be eligible for this scholarship.
Artificial Intelligence
Computing Science
Mathematics
Molecular Sciences
Physics and Astronomy
These students:
have an average grade of at least 7
participate in extracurricular activities that demonstrate their motivation for personal development
Amount of scholarship
€5,000 per year
Professional development programme
Personal ASML mentor
Introduction to ASML's activities, specialists, and knowledge
How to apply
For the full terms and conditions, the application process, the selection procedure and deadlines, please visit the ASML website.

BAPPENAS Scholarship
BAPPENAS Scholarship
Available to Indonesian students, BAPPENAS scholarships are targeted specifically at staff from ten government agencies. The aim of the programme is to enhance organisational capacity and to improve the internal operating environment of these targeted agencies.
Type
Scholarship
Eligible countries or regions
Indonesia
Conditions
Scholarships are available for Master's programmes, PhD positions and short courses related to the aims of the BAPPENAS programme. Candidates should have at least two years of work experience and have graduated from university at least two years prior.
How to apply
For more information about BAPPENAS scholarships, including requirements and how to apply, please visit the BAPPENAS website (in Indonesian).
