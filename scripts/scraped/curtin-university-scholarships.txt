Explore scholarships
Our scholarships are designed to enhance your academic journey and support you while you study. You don’t need to be the highest performing student or experiencing financial hardship to be eligible; there are scholarships available for a variety of backgrounds and achievements.
Curtin Excellence Scholarship
The Excellence scholarship offers support ranging from $5,000 to $15,000 in value to current high school leavers who receive an ATAR of 96 and above.
Find out more information
Curtin STAR Scholarship
A cash payment ranging between $500 and $1,500 per semester, running the expected duration of your undergraduate degree. Awarded on a need basis.
Find out more information
Harry Perkins Memorial Scholarship
A $2,000 per-semester payment – awarded on a need basis – for undergraduate students relocating to Perth from a regional or remote area to study.
Find out more information
Indigenous scholarships
Explore scholarships specially tailored for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students – with options based on academic achievement and financial needs.

John Curtin Scholarship Program (JCSP)
This program includes the John Curtin Undergraduate Scholarship and Julia Gillard Women in Leadership Scholarship and provides course fee coverage, international study and an annual $2,000 payment.

2027 - Semester 1 - Curtin Excellence Scholarship (Future Students)
Status: Open
Applications open: 22/07/2026
Applications close: 20/12/2026
View printable version [.pdf]
About this scholarship
Description/Applicant information
The Curtin Excellence Scholarship ( Scholarship ) has been developed to encourage and reward students who have demonstrated academic excellence through their ATAR in 2026 and want to pursue study at Curtin University.
Application forms are not required, students apply by selecting an eligible Curtin degree through TISC as their first preference by the scholarship closing date.
Students who receive the StepUp bonus and therefore achieve the required ATAR will be eligible for this scholarship.

Value
The Scholarship will provide the following support:
For students who achieve an ATAR between 96 - 97.99:
A total value of $5,000 paid as a cash stipend of $2,500 per study period for up to one year.
For students who achieve an ATAR of 98 or above:
A total value of $15,000 paid as a cash stipend of $2,500 per study period for up to three years.
The amount can be used at the recipient's discretion to contribute towards educational related expenses.
Scholarship Details
Eligible courses
All undergraduate degrees, excluding the Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery
Eligibility criteria
Candidates must meet all of the following criteria to be considered for the Scholarship:
Australian citizen, Australian permanent resident, Australian permanent humanitarian or New Zealand citizen
Selected Curtin University as a first preference through TISC to undertake an eligible undergraduate degree by the scholarship closing date
Undertaking year 12 WACE in 2026
Is a high-school leaver in 2026
Complete a minimum of 4 ATAR subjects and receive an ATAR between 96 - 97.99 for the one year scholarship, or an ATAR of 98 or above for the three year scholarship
Receive an eligible course offer at Curtin for Semester 1, 2027 in main round course offers
Accept the main round course offer by the course offer lapse date
Will enrol full-time (at least 75 credits each study period)
Please note:
Students who receive the StepUp bonus and therefore achieve the required ATAR will be eligible for this scholarship
Students who are required to sit the STAT written English test may still be eligible for this scholarship, provided they meet all the other eligibility criteria and receive an offer of a place into an eligible course in second round offers.
This scholarship is deferrable for up to one year
Application process
Application form is not required for this scholarship.
Please follow the steps below.
Eligibility and Assessment
Step 1: Select preferences through TISC
Select an eligible degree through TISC and select Curtin as first preference for main round offers
Eligible degrees are listed in ‘Eligible Courses’ section
Step 2: Course Offer
Receive an offer for an eligible degree in main round offers at Curtin for Semester 1, 2027
Successful candidates must accept their course offer before the course offer lapse date
Step 3: Outcome
Scholarship(s) will be awarded to all eligible students
Successful candidate(s) are notified via email listed through TISC after main round offers have been dispatched
Step 4: Acceptance
Successful candidate(s) (including those who wish to defer their studies) accept scholarship by the deadline specified in offer email
Deferral of this scholarship is permitted (details included in scholarship terms and conditions)
Please note:
Scholarship will commence from first semester of study
Scholarship is not transferable to another university
Enrolment Requirements
Recipients must meet all of the following:
Remain enrolled in an eligible course of study
Maintain a full-time study load of at least 75 credits each study period at Curtin University
Maintain a course weighted average (CWA) of at least 70 each study period
