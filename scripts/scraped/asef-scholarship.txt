FAQs
What is the Asia-Europe Foundation (ASEF)?
ASEF is an intergovernmental not-for-profit organisation that was created to strengthen Asia-Europe relations through its various projects spanning 7 key thematic areas of work. Founded in 1997 by the partner countries of the  Asia-Europe Meeting (ASEM)  &#8211; an informal, political dialogue process comprising of over 50 countries in Asia and Europe &#8211; ASEF remains to this day the only established institution of ASEM.
Although based in Singapore, ASEF operates in all ASEM countries. ASEF is funded by voluntary contribution by all ASEM partners. Most of ASEF projects are also supported financially by civil society organisations, public institutions and businesses that ASEF works with.
For more information, please read about our  history  and  what we do .
What is the Asia-Europe Meeting (ASEM)?
ASEM is an informal dialogue process among governments currently representing 53 countries: 30 European and 21 Asian countries, the European Union and the ASEAN Secretariat.
It aims to forge stronger relations between Asia and Europe, based on equal partnership, mutual respect and mutual benefits. Its activities are grouped into 3 pillars:
political;
economic; and,
socio-cultural
The inaugural meeting of ASEM leaders was in March 1996 in Bangkok, Thailand. 1 year later, ASEF was born. Read more about ASEM and the history of ASEF .
Who are the ASEM partners?
Since its inception, ASEM has gone through several stages of enlargement. Currently the 53 ASEM partners are: Australia, Austria, Bangladesh, Belgium, Brunei Darussalam, Bulgaria, Cambodia, China, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Kazakhstan, Korea, Lao PDR, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Malta, Mongolia, Myanmar, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Pakistan, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russian Federation, Singapore, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand, United Kingdom, Viet Nam, the ASEAN Secretariat and the European Union.
Check out the  ASEM Infoboard Website
What are ASEF's areas of work?
ASEF activities cover the following areas:
Culture
Education
Governance
Sustainable Development
Economy
Public Health
Media
The activities under these thematic areas closely align to ASEM priorities. Read more about  what we do .
How is ASEF structured?
The  Board of Governors  is ASEF&#8217;s decision-making body and represents the respective ASEM partners. The Governors are nominated by partner governments for a renewable period of 3 years. The Board meets thrice in a 2-year cycle and sets out the policy directions of ASEF.
ASEF is headed by the Executive Director and the Deputy Executive Director, who take management decisions based on directions and policy guidelines set out by ASEM governments and the ASEF Board of Governors. They are supported by the Executive Office. ASEF also has 4 programme departments which are supported by the  Finance & Administration , Human Resources, and Communications Departments. ASEF&#8217;s programme departments are:
Culture Department
Education Department
Governance & Economy Department
Sustainable Development & Public Health Department
How are ASEF's projects supported and funded?
ASEF projects are generally co-organised with institutions from Asia-Europe Meeting (ASEM) partner countries. ASEF shares the cost of each project with the co-organiser(s).
Co-organisers and sponsors include:
parliaments
government agencies
think tanks
educational institutions
student and youth organisations
cultural and heritage promotion organisations (e.g. museums, arts festivals etc)
businesses
NGOs
trade unions and
other relevant civil society organisations
What is the procedure to organise a project in partnership with ASEF?
If you wish to organise a project in partnership with ASEF, please let us know at info@asef.org.
How can I participate in ASEF projects?
Participation in projects carried out by ASEF is open to nationals from ASEM countries. Exceptions can be made, where a non-ASEM national may be critical for a project’s success. As different projects may have different requirements, please go to the relevant project web page on our website or contact the respective department for further information. All calls for applications are available on ASEF’s website.
How can I join ASEF as an employee or an intern?
ASEM country nationals can apply for job and internship opportunities. Calls for job vacancies and internships are posted on our  Work for Us  page.
How can I keep up with developments within ASEF?
ASEF publishes a monthly eNewsletter. You can  subscribe to ASEF eNewsletter directly from the ASEF website or contact us at info@asef.org to be added to the mailing list.
What does the ASEF logo mean?
The ASEF logo is a pictorial representation of the letters &#8220;A&#8221; for Asia and &#8220;E&#8221; for Europe in a multi-coloured continuous strand of a twisted ribbon. The form of the letters also hints at the meeting of 2 continents. The colours reflects embracing diversity, bridging peoples, & shaping partnerships &#8211; our work over the past 2 decades, and for many more years to come.

Capacity-Building on Gender Equality and Women&#8217;s Participation

The Asia–Europe Foundation (ASEF) will be organising a three-day, in-person C apacity-Building Training Programme on Inclusive and Sustainable Development: Gender Equality and Women&#8217;s Participation.
Kunming, China 7-9 September 2026
The programme is designed to empower approximately 20 participants from ASEM partner countries who influence policy, programme design, institutional practice, advocacy, research, or public dialogue on gender equality. The primary objective is to place women&#8217;s participation at the centre of inclusive governance and sustainable development &#8211; recognising gender equality as a cross-cutting issue that underpins progress across many of the Sustainable Development Goals &#8211; while underscoring its significance to broader agendas including climate change, peace and security, and social inclusion. The programme is hosted by the Foreign Affairs Office of the People’s Government of Yunnan Province.
PROGRAMME OBJECTIVES
The Capacity-Building Training aims to convey to participants:
A deeper understanding of the structural, institutional, social, and cultural barriers that continue to constrain women’s participation and leadership.
Practical competencies in gender analysis, the use of gender-sensitive and gender-disaggregated data, gender-responsive budgeting, and monitoring approaches relevant to women’s participation.
An introduction to mainstreaming gender equality in their own organisations, policies, and programmes. Fourth, it will foster cross-regional learning and exchange among participants from Asia and Europe, enabling comparative reflection and peer-to-peer knowledge sharing.
Analysis of barriers to women’s participation using an intersectional lens, the application of practical tools for integrating gender equality into policy and programme processes, enhanced utilisation of gender-sensitive data and indicators, identification of strategies for promoting women’s leadership and participation in their own sector or institution, and the formulation of concrete follow-up actions that are feasible within their professional contexts.
TARGET PARTICIPANTS & ELIGIBILITY
The training is designed to engage a diverse range of stakeholders committed to advancing gender equality and women&#8217;s empowerment across the ASEM region.
Primary participants will include representatives from relevant government agencies and bodies with mandates in this area,  such as Ministries, Commissions, or Councils on Women, Departments of Social Welfare, Family Affairs and Development, gender focal points within ministries, and members of relevant inter-ministerial groups,  as well as representatives from civil society organisations actively engaged in promoting gender equality.
Secondary participants may include representatives from academic institutions, as well as relevant private sector organisations and agencies contributing to women&#8217;s participation and empowerment.
All participants are expected to be citizens of eligible ASEM Partner countries , and working proficiency in English is also required. Priority will be given to participants who are in positions to influence policy, programme design, institutional practice, advocacy, research, or public dialogue. In line with the programme’s objective of promoting gender equality as a whole-of-society agenda, gender-balanced participation will be encouraged.
TRAINING FORMAT
The Training format will include a series of comprehensive training sessions specifically developed to strengthen understanding of gender equality and women’s participation in policymaking, programming, advocacy, and community-level initiatives. These sessions will emphasise participatory, interactive, and strongly practice-based approaches, prioritising an inclusive and engaging learning process.
For further details, refer to the Training Leaflet &#8211; Capacity-Building on Gender Equality and Women&#8217;s Participation .
TRAVEL & ACCOMMODATION
The co-organisers will cover:
Airfare on point-to-point economy class to travel to/from Kunming, China through reimbursement, up to a maximum ceiling.
4 nights of accommodation at the training venue with check-in on 6 September and check-out on 10 September 2026.
APPLICATION
The application period for this programme has now closed; the deadline for submissions was Friday, 3 July 2026 (23:59 Singapore time).
Thank you to all who applied. All applicants, whether or not they are selected, will be notified of the outcome by the end of July 2026.
About the organisers
The training is hosted by the Foreign Affairs Office of the People’s Government of Yunnan Province and funded by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People&#8217;s Republic of China.
