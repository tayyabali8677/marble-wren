Bursaries + Emergency Funding
On This Page
Bursaries | Emergency Loans | Indigenous Student Financial Support | Other Fundings
The Office of the Registrar offers various forms of financial support designed to assist students experiencing financial shortfall for the current school year's academic and basic living costs or a temporary unexpected financial emergency during the academic year.
Review the information below to help you find a program you may be eligible for. Additional information on eligibility and how to apply is available on the appropriate program page. If you have questions or require assistance with the applications, please contact the Student Service Centre .
If you need accessibility support to complete your application, contact Accessibility & Accommodations &#160;for assistance.
Bursaries
To be eligible for bursary funding, you must demonstrate financial need for the current academic year after exhausting all available funding resources and meeting the academic and enrolment requirements. Bursaries, unlike loans, do not have to be repaid.
Domestic Supplementary Bursary
Domestic undergraduate and graduate students only. Apply for this if you have a financial shortfall for the current school year's academic and basic living costs.
International Supplementary Bursary
International undergraduate and graduate students only. Apply for this if you have a financial shortfall for the current school year's academic and basic living costs.
Graduate Students' Association Emergency Bursary
Open to graduate students only. Apply for this if you have a specific emergency expense and you have no way to pay for the expense; this is not for a financial shortfall for the current school year's academic and basic living costs.
Beginning in Fall 2026, this application will be integrated into the Domestic and International Supplementary Bursary application. Please submit an application to that program in order to be considered for the Graduate Students&#8217; Association Emergency Bursary.
Bursaries by faculty
Bursaries by faculty are available. By filling out the supplementary bursary applications, you are automatically considered for all bursaries you are eligible for, including faculty-specific bursaries.
Bursaries 101 workshops
Thinking you may need to apply for a bursary, but not sure where to start? Register for a Bursaries 101 workshop where our expert staff will walk you through basic bursary information, how to complete the application, general application timelines and more.
Student workshops typically run in the Fall term. Once scheduled, more information will be released on this page.
Emergency loans
Emergency loans are open to all students. Apply for this if you have a specific emergency expense and you will repay this loan at a later date .
Emergency expenses include support for temporary financial shortfalls, such as:
Temporary delay in your ability to cover essential living expenses, such as rent or groceries.
Unexpected expenses (e.g., emergency health costs or dental work).
Emergency loans will not be approved to pay for any university tuition/fees or debt of any kind (lines of credit, credit cards, etc).
Indigenous Student Financial Support
Indigenous Students&#8217; Support Fund
Domestic undergraduate and graduate students only. Apply for this if you are First Nation, M&#233;tis or Inuit and you are experiencing a short-term financial emergency.
The Indigenous Students&#8217; Support Fund (ISSF) is managed strictly for FNMI students who may be experiencing an immediate financial crisis and need money quickly. Funding is usually based on an emergency that may also result in a barrier to continuing your studies.
Other bursary funding available to students
Other types of bursaries are also available from faculties and other sources on campus:

Emergency Loan
On this page
Eligibility Requirements | How to Apply | Receiving Your Funds | How to Make Payments
The Emergency Loan program provides immediate funding support for temporary financial shortfalls such as the inability to pay for essential living expenses (e.g., rent or groceries) or unexpected expenses (e.g., emergency health costs or dental work). Emergency loans are not issued to pay for any university tuition/fees or debt of any kind (lines of credit, credit cards, etc).
In order to qualify for an Emergency Loan, you must demonstrate the ability to repay the loan. Your repayment timeline will be based on your registration and the details of your repayment plan (up to a maximum of six months). Your emergency financial situation will be assessed on a case-by-case basis, and approval is not guaranteed. If your financial shortfall is not temporary or short-term, we encourage you to explore the Supplementary Bursary program for non-repayable funding support.
Eligibility Requirements
In order to qualify for an Emergency Loan, you must:
Be an undergraduate or graduate student currently attending classes at the University of Alberta at the time of your application.
Have satisfactory academic standing or greater based on your most recent term grades (for most programs, this is a 2.0 GPA or higher).
Be able to demonstrate your ability to repay the loan; repayment deadlines will be based on your registration and/or repayment plan up to a maximum of 6 months.
Be able to demonstrate that you are facing urgent financial issues unrelated to tuition or other university costs. Emergency loans are not issued to pay for any university tuition/fees or debt of any kind (lines of credit, credit cards, etc).
How to Apply
Go to the Application for Financial Support page and log in using your Campus Computing ID (CCID) and password.
Complete and submit the Emergency Loan application.
An advisor will review your Emergency Loan application and assess your financial situation within three business days. Please monitor your application in the portal, as your application will be re-opened if additional information is needed.
Receiving Your Funds
If your application is approved, your Emergency Loan funds will be sent via Interac e-transfer along with an exclusive PIN by the end of the next business day. Funds can only be sent to University of Alberta email addresses.
If you have any complications with accepting your Interac e-transfer, please reach out to bursaries@ualberta.ca .
How to Make Payments
Payments can only be made online at this time. The following banks can be used for online payments: ATB, BMO, CIBC, RBC, Scotiabank, Servus Credit Union, Simplii, and TD Canada Trust.
If your bank is not listed here, please let us know by emailing us ( bursaries@ualberta.ca ) so that we can contact that bank to set up the University of Alberta (Emergency Loan) bill payment option.
To make payments online, follow these steps:
Log in to your bank's website or mobile application.
Navigate to the bill payment section (the pathway will be different for each bank). Select the option to add a payee/biller.
Search for the University of Alberta Emergency Loan payee option and click on the result; note that each bank has a different variation of this wording/word order.
The biller name variations can be seen below:
ATB: Univ of Alberta Emergency LN
BMO: University Of Alberta Emergency Loan
CIBC: University Alberta Emergncy LN
RBC: Univ Alberta Emergency LN
Scotiabank: Univ of Alberta Emergency LN
Servus Credit Union: Univ ofAlberta Emergency Ln
Simplii: University Alberta Emergncy LN
TD: University of Alberta - Emergency Loan Repayment
Enter your account number as your 7-digit student ID number plus the first 4 letters of your last name. If your last name is shorter than 4 letters, enter the letters of your last name followed by letters from your first name (up to 4 letters in total).
Example:
Suzy Smith -- 1234567SMIT
Suzy Cho -- 1234567CHOS
Make a payment.
Note : Other funding paid to your student account (such as government student loans, external funding, scholarships, bursaries, etc.) will not automatically pay off your loan balance.
Repayment expectations and impacts
Loans are expected to be fully repaid by your agreed-upon due date. This ensures that other students are able to benefit from the program. If there is a balance owing on your loan by your due date, a financial hold will be automatically placed on your student account. Financial holds restrict your ability to register in courses and access official documentation such as transcripts or degree parchments. For more information about this policy see the University Calendar ("Overdue Accounts") .

GSA Emergency Bursary
On This Page
Eligibility | How to Apply | Funding Amount | Processing Time | Receiving Funds | Notify Loan Provider
Starting in Fall 2026, funding from the GSA Emergency Bursary program will be distributed through the Domestic and International Supplementary Bursary program. Please submit an application to that program if you would like to be considered for GSA Emergency Bursary funding.
The Graduate Students' Association (GSA) Emergency Bursary program is a non-repayable bursary program for graduate students at the University of Alberta who need assistance due to an unanticipated emergency. Note that this bursary is not used for a financial shortfall for the current school year's academic and basic living costs.
For more information about this program, visit the GSA Emergency Bursaries webpage .
Apply for the GSA Emergency Bursary
Eligibility requirements
You must be a current GSA member.
You must have confirmed enrolment in the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies at the University of Alberta at the time of your application.
You must be in satisfactory academic standing or greater based on your most recent term grades.
You must have exhausted all other available funding options before applying for a GSA Emergency Bursary.
You must have experienced an unanticipated emergency expense.
Domestic students: You must be eligible for and receive the maximum available government student loans (exceptions for students presenting documentation of First Nations, M&#233;tis and Inuit identity may apply) prior to applying for a GSA Emergency Bursary.
International students: It is expected that all international students have sufficient funding support based on the Government of Canada Study Permit requirements ; you are required to provide documentation of the original financial plan for your entire study time period and what has unexpectedly changed.
Your application must demonstrate a financial shortfall for academic and basic living costs for the academic year in which you are currently enrolled.
How to Apply
Beginning in Fall 2026, the GSA Emergency Bursary application will be integrated into the Domestic and International Supplementary Bursary programs . The application dates and deadlines will match those of the respective Supplementary Bursary program.&#160;
Any questions about the application can be sent to bursaries@ualberta.ca .
Funding Amount
The maximum amount that will be awarded to a GSA member for a GSA Emergency Bursary is $4,000. Applications will be evaluated based on financial need calculated via the bursary common assessment; the maximum level of funding is not guaranteed and program availability is contingent on available funding.
Application Processing Time
Applications are assessed in the order they are received. You can expect your application to be initially reviewed within 7-10 business days of application submission. Notifications of the progress of your bursary application will be sent to your University of Alberta email address.
Receiving Your Funds
If your application is approved, your GSA Emergency Bursary funds will be sent via Interac e-transfer within 1-2 business days after approval. You will be emailed an exclusive PIN to deposit your Interac e-transfer.
Please note that your Supplementary Bursary portion of your application will still continue to be assessed and may not follow the same timelines as the GSA Emergency Bursary portion of the application. Please view the Domestic and International Supplementary Bursary portions of our website for more information on those programs and their associated timelines.
Notifying Your Student Loan Provider
Alberta Student Loan Recipients
You are not required to notify Alberta Student Aid of any bursary awards you receive.
Out-of-Province Student Loan Recipients
If you hold out-of-province government student loans for the current year, you must notify your provincial student loan provider to declare a change of income. When declaring your change of income, describe your bursary as "scholarship income." For more information or assistance, please contact the Student Service Centre .

Indigenous Students' Support Fund
Emergency Financial Support
First Peoples' House and Student Financial Support (Office of the Registrar) partner to provide funding for students who are First Nation, M&#233;tis or Inuit peoples of Canada (FNMI) and need emergency financial support.
The Indigenous Students&#8217; Support Fund (ISSF) is managed strictly for FNMI students who may be experiencing an immediate financial crisis and need money quickly. Funding is usually based on an emergency that may also result in a barrier to continuing your studies. Please note that students can only access ISSF funding once per annual academic cycle.
Students not experiencing an immediate financial emergency should apply to the Supplementary Bursary program to be assessed for financial support that spans that academic year. If you have questions about how to best utilize our available programs, please email us at bursaries@ualberta.ca .
Eligibility Requirements
You must be an undergraduate or graduate student currently attending classes at the University of Alberta at the time of your application.
You must be in satisfactory academic standing or greater based on your most recent term grades.
You must be able to provide documentation of your Indigeneity (if you have not already provided this information in the past). Please refer to the information about this requirement on the First Nations, M&#233;tis and Inuit Awards page .
You are facing an unplanned, unexpected financial emergency for which you currently don&#8217;t have access to any other resources to pay for.
How to Apply
Applying to the ISSF is quick, easy and confidential.&#160;
Students can login to the Application for Financial Support using their CCID and password and then select the Indigenous Students' Support Fund.
Examples of information required for the application form include:
A description of your emergency cost, including the dollar amount needed
A description of any other financial resources you currently have access to
A copy of your documentation of Indigeneity (see Eligibility Requirements)
The outcome of your application will be sent to you via email. Your information will be kept secure and will only be used for the purpose of this program.&#160;
Any questions about the application can be sent to bursaries@ualberta.ca .
Receiving Your Funds
If your application is approved, your ISSF funds will be sent via Interac e-transfer by the end of the next business day. You will be notified by email of your exclusive PIN to deposit the funds into your bank account.
