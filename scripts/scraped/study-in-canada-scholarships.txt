Notice to Canadian Institutions: Program Delivery Model Update - Effective 2026-2027 cycle
The Study in Canada Scholarships (SICS) will be administered under an adjusted delivery model in which Canadian institutions are responsible for the selection of students.
Key changes to the application process include:
Selection at the program level will be based on the institution&rsquo;s submission of a Management and Accountability Framework (MAF) .
The Privacy Notice Statement (PNS) will be the only supporting document required to be submitted by the institution on behalf of the student through the My EduCanada Portal.
Only Canadian post-secondary institutions are eligible to apply on this call
Participating Canadian post-secondary institutions are responsible for identifying and selecting eligible participants prior to submitting their application through the MyEduCanada portal. Direct applications from individuals are not accepted.
Objectives
The Study in Canada Scholarships (SICS) offer Canadian post-secondary institutions the opportunity to host international students from a diverse range of countries and territories for short-term study or research exchanges. Funded by Global Affairs Canada through the Academic Relations program , SICS aims to strengthen and diversify Canada&rsquo;s international linkages and partnerships.
The new connections created through the program will diversify and enrich Canada&rsquo;s education engagement with a diverse range of partners, strengthen bilateral ties, as well as deepen people-to-people ties and cultural connections between Canada and participating countries/territories.
Eligibility
To be eligible, applicants must:
be a Designated Learning Institution (DLI);
waive tuition fees for scholarship recipients.
Scholarship project scope and requirements
A scholarship project consists of one or more individual scholarships that support short-term inbound mobility to a Canadian post-secondary institution. A project commences upon the signing of a contribution agreement with Global Affairs Canada and must be completed no later than September 30th, 2027. The total funding from GAC will not exceed $150,000 per project .
Scholarships may only be awarded to individuals who are:
Citizens of the following eligible countries/territories:
Asia: Bangladesh, Nepal, Taiwan;
Europe: T&uuml;rkiye, Ukraine;
Middle East and North Africa: Algeria, Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, Tunisia;
Sub-Saharan Africa: Burkina Faso, Ethiopia, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda.
Enrolled full-time at a post-secondary institution in an eligible country/territory;
Paying tuition fees to that institution at the time of application and for the full duration of their mobility in Canada.
Able to complete all project activities no later than September 30, 2027.
Scholarships may not be awarded to individuals who:
Hold or have a pending application for Canadian citizenship or permanent residency;
Are already holding another grant or source of funding from Global Affairs Canada (GAC), through its legal name, the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development (DFATD), or from a Canadian federal granting agency (e.g. Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC), Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (SSHRC) or Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR). All additional sources of funding must be declared;
Are already enrolled in a degree, diploma or certificate program at a Canadian post-secondary institution. Scholarship value and duration
Scholarship value and duration must be as follows:
$10,200 for 4 months or one academic term for study or research at the college of undergraduate level
$10,200 for 4 months or one academic term for study or research at the graduate level (Masters and PhD)
$14,000 for 5 to 6 months for study or research at the graduate level (Masters and PhD)
Failure to comply with the project parameters may affect funding.
Canadian institutions will receive funding from Global Affairs Canada, through its legal name, the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development (DFATD), in the form of a non-negotiable contribution agreement. Each participating institution will be required to enter into an agreement with its selected students and will be responsible for disbursing the scholarship funds to them. Should there be changes to the planned mobility activities, funding may be adjusted accordingly.
Eligible expenses
Scholarship amounts.
$500 per implemented scholarship (i.e. where mobility occurs) to cover administrative costs of the Canadian institution, including an employer compliance fee paid to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), if applicable.
Additional Information
Tuition is not an eligible expense and must be waived by Canadian institutions How to apply
Canadian institutions are no longer required to upload supporting documents other than the Privacy Notice Statement (PNS) to the My EduCanada portal.
The application process consists of:
submitting one or more individual scholarship nominations through the My EduCanada portal; and
sending the following document by email to Scholarships-Info-Bourses@international.gc.ca by the application deadline:
Management and Accountability Framework outlining the measures and procedures the applicant will implement to ensure fair and transparent selection process for scholarship recipients. For more information on the Management and Accountability Framework see the &ldquo;Project selection criteria&rdquo; section below.
Application steps for Canadian institutions on how to submit an application on the My EduCanada portal:
Assign a program coordinator who will be responsible for completing the application.
To access My EduCanada, you must first sign in or create an account through Global Affairs Canada Single Sign-On Services. Creating a GCKey account is recommended for institutions. Login or Register to begin.
After creating your Global Affairs Canada Single Sign-On account, register your institution on My EduCanada. Be sure to select Institution for your account type.
Once your account is activated and you are logged in, select Study in Canada Scholarships and click Apply
Enter data in the required fields and follow the instructions on the application form.
Click Validate and save as you progress through the application form.
Upload the Privacy Notice Statement for non-Canadian participants , signed by the candidate and dated within the last 6 months.
Review all components of your application for accuracy and completeness.
Read and accept the conditions of the "Declaration and permission" statement. Click Submit . You will receive a confirmation message and a reference number for submitted applications.
Print or download the form for your records using the &ldquo;Print | Download&rdquo; function.
Visit Help with MyEduCanada for more information.
Project selection criteria
Projects will be selected based on the completeness and quality of the Management and Accountability Framework outlining the measures and procedures the applicant will implement to ensure:
a fair and transparent selection process for scholarship recipients;
eligibility of selected candidates;
authenticity and maintenance of supporting documentation;
broad representation of eligible countries and territories; and
equitable gender participation Key dates
Application deadline
The deadline for Canadian institutions to apply and email the completed Management and Accountability Framework to Scholarships-Info-Bourses@international.gc.ca is 11:59 p.m. EDT, March 31, 2026.
Notification of results
The scholarship administrator will communicate successful results to Canadian institutions in May 2026. The administrator will not provide feedback for unsuccessful applications. Frequently asked questions
If we are applying for both the Emerging Leaders in the America&rsquo;s Program (ELAP) and Study in Canada Scholarships (SICS), do we submit two Management and Accountability Frameworks?
Yes, if your institution is applying to both ELAP and SICS, a Management and Accountability Framework must be submitted for each scholarship program. Why has the $230 CIC reimbursement been incorporated into the $500 institutional payment? Does this change mean that institutions hosting research students will receive less than $500 per student in Canada?
The requirement for the $230 CIC fee is directly tied to each institution&rsquo;s internal regulations. Practices vary across institutions.
For example, some institutions use the LMIA exemption to classify the researcher as a visiting researcher or employee, which allows them to waive tuition.
There are several pathways available to institutions, particularly through the effective use of mobility agreements that exempt students from paying tuition. In cases where an institution decides to proceed with a LMIA exemption request, they may draw on the $500 per student allocation to cover these costs. Is the $500 administrative fee included in the $150,000 maximum?
The contribution agreement will include the approved project amounts for ELAP and SICS and the $500 administrative fee. Are institutions required to submit a ranking of their students?
No. Canadian institutions are responsible for the selection of students. Can you clarify some new terms such as &lsquo;project&rsquo;, &lsquo;mobility project&rsquo; and &lsquo;ultimate recipient&rsquo;.
Project: The application submitted by an institution that includes the Management and Accountability Framework and nominated students is a mobility project .
While selected institutions are the recipients of an awarded project, the student receiving the scholarship is the ultimate recipient . If the Canadian institution is awarded a total project amount lower than its original submission, how does it affect the original list of students?
Prior to signing the contribution agreement, institutions will be required to submit a final list of their selected students. If a replacement is required due to a withdrawal, the reserve student must be selected from the same pool of applicants originally submitted by the institution. Are Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) still required for participation in the program?
The existence of an MOU or exchange agreement is no longer an eligibility criterion, including undergraduate mobility.
Institutions are not required to upload any documents used in selecting the ultimate recipients other than the Privacy Notice Statement.
While MOUs are not mandatory for participation in the program, we recognize that institutions may require them in order to waive tuition. As such, we anticipate that institutions will continue to rely on their existing agreements to implement the program and will retain any documents used for this purpose in their own records. What is the earliest possible start date for scholarship recipients?
The earliest start date is at the signing of the contribution agreement. All project activities must be completed no later than September 30, 2027. Why are Scholarship Confirmation Letters no longer being issued by GAC?
As institutions are now responsible for selecting the scholarship&rsquo;s ultimate recipients, the Confirmation Letter must be issued directly by the institution. Contact us
Questions?
Contact the scholarship administrator at Scholarships-Info-Bourses@international.gc.ca or 613-237-4820.

Avis aux &eacute;tablissements canadiens : Mise &agrave; jour du mod&egrave;le de prestation des programmes &ndash; &Agrave; compter du cycle 2026-2027.
Le Programme des Bourses d&rsquo;&eacute;tudes au Canada (BEC) sera administr&eacute; selon un mod&egrave;le de prestation modifi&eacute; dans lequel les &eacute;tablissements canadiens seront responsables de la s&eacute;lection des &eacute;tudiants.
Les principaux changements apport&eacute;s au processus de candidature sont les suivants :
La s&eacute;lection au niveau du programme sera bas&eacute;e sur la soumission par l&rsquo;institution d&rsquo;un Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion .
L'&eacute;nonc&eacute; de confidentialit&eacute; (EDC) sera le seul document justificatif &agrave; soumettre par l&rsquo;institution au nom de l&rsquo;&eacute;tudiant via le portail Mon &Eacute;duCanada.
Seuls les &eacute;tablissements postsecondaire canadiens peuvent pr&eacute;senter une demande dans le cadre du pr&eacute;sent appel
Les &eacute;tablissements postsecondaire canadiens participants sont charg&eacute;s d'identifier et de s&eacute;lectionner les participants admissibles avant de soumettre leur demande sur le portail Mon &Eacute;duCanada. Les demandes directes provenant de particuliers ne sont pas accept&eacute;es.
Objectifs
Les Bourses d&rsquo;&eacute;tudes au Canada (BEC) offre aux &eacute;tablissements postsecondaires canadiens la possibilit&eacute; d&rsquo;accueillir des &eacute;tudiants internationaux d&rsquo;un grand nombre de pays et de territoires dans le cadre d&rsquo;&eacute;changes de courte dur&eacute;e afin de poursuivre des &eacute;tudes ou faire de la recherche. Financ&eacute; par Affaires mondiales Canada dans le cadre du Programme de relations acad&eacute;miques , le BEC vise &agrave; renforcer et &agrave; diversifier les liens et les partenariats internationaux du Canada.
Les nouvelles relations d&eacute;velopp&eacute;es dans le cadre du programme permettront de diversifier et d&rsquo;enrichir les engagements du Canada en mati&egrave;re d&rsquo;&eacute;ducation avec une vaste gamme de partenaires, de renforcer les relations bilat&eacute;rales, ainsi que d&rsquo;approfondir les liens interpersonnels et culturels entre le Canada et les pays/territoires participants.
Admissibilit&eacute;
Les candidats admissibles doivent :
&Ecirc;tre un &eacute;tablissement d&rsquo;enseignement d&eacute;sign&eacute; (EED);
Exempter les frais de scolarit&eacute; pour les boursiers
Port&eacute;e et exigences du projet de bourse
Un projet de bourse comprend une ou plusieurs bourses individuelles qui soutiennent la mobilit&eacute; entrante &agrave; court terme vers un &eacute;tablissement postsecondaire canadien. Un projet d&eacute;bute lors de la signature d&rsquo;une entente de contribution avec Affaires mondiales Canada et doit &ecirc;tre compl&eacute;t&eacute; au plus tard le 30 septembre 2027. Le financement total provenant d&rsquo;AMC ne d&eacute;passera pas 150 000 $ par projet .
Les bourses ne peuvent &ecirc;tre attribu&eacute;es qu&rsquo;aux personnes qui :
Sont citoyens des pays/territoires admissibles suivants :
Asie : Bangladesh, N&eacute;pal, Ta&iuml;wan;
Europe : T&uuml;rkiye, Ukraine;
Moyen-Orient et Afrique du Nord : Alg&eacute;rie, &Eacute;gypte, Jordanie, Maroc, Tunisie;
Afrique sub-saharienne : Burkina Faso, &Eacute;thiopie, Ghana, C&ocirc;te d'Ivoire, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, S&eacute;n&eacute;gal, Tanzanie, Ouganda.
Sont inscrits &agrave; temps plein dans un &eacute;tablissement d&rsquo;enseignement postsecondaire d&rsquo;un pays ou territoire admissible;
Paient tous les frais de scolarit&eacute; requis par cet &eacute;tablissement au moment de soumettre la demande et pour toute la dur&eacute;e du programme de bourses;
&Ecirc;tre en mesure de terminer toutes les activit&eacute;s du projet au plus tard le 30 septembre 2027.
Les bourses ne peuvent pas &ecirc;tre attribu&eacute;es aux personnes qui :
D&eacute;tiennent ou font actuellement une demande pour obtenir la citoyennet&eacute; canadienne ou la r&eacute;sidence permanente;
B&eacute;n&eacute;ficient d&eacute;j&agrave; une autre subvention ou sources de financement d&rsquo;Affaires mondiales Canada, par l&rsquo;entremise de son nom l&eacute;gal le minist&egrave;re des Affaires &eacute;trang&egrave;res, du Commerce et du D&eacute;veloppement (MAECD) ou d&rsquo;un autre organisme f&eacute;d&eacute;ral canadien de subvention (par exemple, Conseil de recherches en sciences naturelles et en g&eacute;nie du Canada (CRSNG), Conseil de recherches en sciences humaines (CRSH) or Instituts de recherche en sant&eacute; du Canada (IRSC). Toutes les sources de financement suppl&eacute;mentaires doivent &ecirc;tre d&eacute;clar&eacute;es;
Sont d&eacute;j&agrave; inscrit &agrave; un programme de dipl&ocirc;me, de dipl&ocirc;me ou de certificat dans un &eacute;tablissement postsecondaire canadien. Valeur et dur&eacute;e de la bourse
La valeur et la dur&eacute;e de la bourse doivent &ecirc;tre les suivantes :
10 200 $ pour 4 mois ou un semestre pour &eacute;tudier ou faire de la recherche au coll&eacute;gial ou au premier cycle universitaire.
10 200 $ pour 4 mois ou un semestre pour &eacute;tudier ou faire de la recherche aux cycles sup&eacute;rieurs (ma&icirc;trise et doctorat)
14 000 $ pour 5 &agrave; 6 mois pour &eacute;tudier ou faire de la recherche aux cycles sup&eacute;rieurs (ma&icirc;trise et doctorat)
Le non-respect des param&egrave;tres du projet peut affecter le financement.
Les &eacute;tablissements recevront un financement d&rsquo;Affaires mondiales Canada, par l&rsquo;entremise de son nom l&eacute;gal, le minist&egrave;re des Affaires &eacute;trang&egrave;res, du Commerce et du D&eacute;veloppement (MAECD), sous forme d&rsquo;une entente de contribution non n&eacute;gociable. Chaque &eacute;tablissement participant devra conclure une entente avec ses &eacute;tudiants s&eacute;lectionn&eacute;s et sera responsable de leur verser les fonds de bourse. S&rsquo;il y a des changements dans les mobilit&eacute;s pr&eacute;vues, le financement pourrait &ecirc;tre ajust&eacute; en cons&eacute;quence.
D&eacute;penses admissibles
Montants de bourses;
500 $ par bourse mise en &oelig;uvre (c&rsquo;est-&agrave;-dire lorsque la mobilit&eacute; a lieu) pour couvrir les co&ucirc;ts administratifs de l&rsquo;institution canadienne, y compris les frais de conformit&eacute; de l&rsquo;employeur pay&eacute;s &agrave; Immigration, R&eacute;fugi&eacute;s et Citoyennet&eacute; Canada (IRCC), le cas &eacute;ch&eacute;ant.
Information suppl&eacute;mentaire
Les frais de scolarit&eacute; ne sont pas admissibles et doivent &ecirc;tre exon&eacute;r&eacute; par les institutions canadiennes. Comment postuler
Les &eacute;tablissements canadiens ne sont plus tenus de t&eacute;l&eacute;charger d&rsquo;autres documents justificatifs que l&rsquo;&eacute;nonc&eacute; de confidentialit&eacute; (EDC) sur le portail Mon &Eacute;duCanada.
Le processus de candidature comprend :
Soumettre une ou plusieurs nominations individuelles de bourses via le portail Mon &Eacute;duCanada; et
Envoyer le document suivant par courriel &agrave; Scholarships-Info-Bourses@international.gc.ca par la date limite de candidature :
Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion en d&eacute;taillant les mesures et proc&eacute;dures que le candidat mettra en &oelig;uvre afin d&rsquo;assurer un processus de s&eacute;lection juste et transparent pour les b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaires de bourses. Pour plus d&rsquo;informations sur le Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion , consultez la section &laquo; S&eacute;lection des projets &raquo; ci-dessous.
&Eacute;tapes de la candidature pour les &eacute;tablissements canadiens afin de savoir comment soumettre une demande sur le portail Mon &Eacute;duCanada :
D&eacute;signez un coordonnateur/une coordonnatrice de programme qui sera responsable de remplir les demandes.
Pour acc&eacute;der &agrave; Mon &Eacute;duCanada, vous devez d&rsquo;abord vous connecter ou cr&eacute;er un compte en utilisant les Services d'authentification unique d'Affaires mondiales Canada. La cr&eacute;ation d&rsquo;un compte Cl&eacute;GC est recommand&eacute;e pour les &eacute;tablissements. Ouvrir une session ou s'inscrire pour commencer.
Apr&egrave;s avoir cr&eacute;&eacute; votre compte aupr&egrave;s des Services d&rsquo;authentification unique d&rsquo;Affaires mondiales Canada, inscrivez votre &eacute;tablissement sur Mon &Eacute;duCanada. Assurez-vous de s&eacute;lectionner &Eacute;tablissement comme type de compte.
Une fois votre compte activ&eacute; et connect&eacute;, s&eacute;lectionnez Bourses d&rsquo;&eacute;tudes au Canada et cliquez sur Postuler .
Remplissez les champs requis et suivez les directives du formulaire de demande.
Cliquez sur Valider et enregistrer au fur et &agrave; mesure que vous avancez dans le formulaire de demande.
T&eacute;l&eacute;chargez l&rsquo;&eacute;nonc&eacute; de confidentialit&eacute; pour les participants non canadiens , sign&eacute;e par le candidat et dat&eacute;e des 6 derniers mois.
Assurez-vous que tous les &eacute;l&eacute;ments de votre demande sont exacts et complets avant de la soumettre.
Lisez et acceptez les conditions de la d&eacute;claration &laquo; D&eacute;claration et permission &raquo;. Cliquez sur Soumettre . Vous recevrez un message de confirmation et un num&eacute;ro de r&eacute;f&eacute;rence pour les candidatures soumises.
Imprimez ou t&eacute;l&eacute;chargez le formulaire pour vos dossiers &agrave; l&rsquo;aide du formulaire &laquo; Imprimer|T&eacute;l&eacute;charger &raquo;.
Veuillez consulter la page Aide avec Mon &Eacute;duCanada pour plus d&rsquo;informations.
S&eacute;lection des projets
Les projets seront s&eacute;lectionn&eacute;s en fonction de l&rsquo;exhaustivit&eacute; et de la qualit&eacute; du Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion, qui d&eacute;finit les mesures et proc&eacute;dures que le demandeur mettra en &oelig;uvre afin de garantir :
Un processus de s&eacute;lection juste et transparent pour les b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaires de bourses;
L&rsquo;admissibilit&eacute; des candidats s&eacute;lectionn&eacute;s;
L&rsquo;authenticit&eacute; et la tenue de la documentation &agrave; l&rsquo;appui;
Une large repr&eacute;sentation des pays et territoires admissibles; et
Participation &eacute;quitable des genres. Dates cl&eacute;s
Date limite de candidature
La date limite pour que les institutions canadiennes puissent postuler et envoyer par courriel le Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion compl&eacute;t&eacute; &agrave; Scholarships-Info-Bourses@international.gc.ca est &agrave; 23 h 59 HAE, le 31 mars 2026 .
Annonce des r&eacute;sultats
L&rsquo;administrateur de la bourse communiquera avec les &eacute;tablissements canadiens en mai 2026 pour les informer des candidats retenus. L&rsquo;administrateur de la bourse ne donnera pas de r&eacute;troaction aux candidats non choisis. Foire aux questions
Si nous postulons &agrave; la fois au Programme des futurs leaders dans les Am&eacute;riques (PFLA) et aux Bourses d'&eacute;tudes au Canada (BEC), devons-nous soumettre deux Cadres de responsabilisation et de gestion ?
Oui, si votre &eacute;tablissement postule &agrave; la fois &agrave; PFLA et au BEC, un cadre de gestion et de responsabilit&eacute; doit &ecirc;tre soumis pour chaque programme de bourse. Pourquoi le remboursement de 230 $ li&eacute; &agrave; CIC a-t-il &eacute;t&eacute; int&eacute;gr&eacute; au montant institutionnel de 500 $? Ce changement signifie-t-il que les &eacute;tablissements accueillant des &eacute;tudiants chercheurs recevront moins de 500 $ par &eacute;tudiant ?
L&rsquo;obligation concernant les frais de 230 $ li&eacute;s &agrave; CIC d&eacute;coule directement des r&egrave;glements internes de chaque &eacute;tablissement.
Les pratiques varient : certains &eacute;tablissements utilisent, par exemple, l&rsquo;exemption relative &agrave; l&rsquo;EIMT pour classer le chercheur comme chercheur invit&eacute; ou employ&eacute;, ce qui permet d&rsquo;exempter les frais de scolarit&eacute;.
Plusieurs options s&rsquo;offrent aux &eacute;tablissements, notamment l&rsquo;utilisation efficace des ententes de mobilit&eacute; qui exemptent les &eacute;tudiants de payer les frais de scolarit&eacute;. Si un &eacute;tablissement d&eacute;cide de pr&eacute;senter une demande d&rsquo;exemption d&rsquo;EIMT, il peut utiliser le montant allou&eacute; de 500 $ par &eacute;tudiant pour couvrir ces co&ucirc;ts. Les frais administratifs de 500 $ sont-ils inclus dans le plafond de 150 000 $ ?
L&rsquo;accord de contribution inclura les montants de projet approuv&eacute;s pour PFLA et BEC ainsi que les frais administratifs de 500 $. Les &eacute;tablissements doivent-ils soumettre un classement de leurs &eacute;tudiants ?
Non. Les &eacute;tablissements canadiens sont responsables de la s&eacute;lection des &eacute;tudiants. Pourriez-vous clarifier certains nouveaux termes tels que &laquo; projet &raquo;, &laquo; projet de mobilit&eacute; &raquo; et &laquo; b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaire ultime &raquo; ?
Projet : La demande soumise par un &eacute;tablissement, comprenant le Cadre de responsabilisation et de gestion et les &eacute;tudiants nomm&eacute;s, constitue un projet de mobilit&eacute; .
Bien que les &eacute;tablissements s&eacute;lectionn&eacute;s soient les b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaires d&rsquo;un projet financ&eacute;, l&rsquo;&eacute;tudiant qui re&ccedil;oit la bourse est le b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaire ultime . Si l&rsquo;&eacute;tablissement canadien re&ccedil;oit un montant total de projet inf&eacute;rieur &agrave; celui soumis initialement, comment cela affecte-t-il la liste d&rsquo;&eacute;tudiants propos&eacute;e ?
Avant de signer l&rsquo;accord de contribution, les &eacute;tablissements devront soumettre une liste finale de leurs &eacute;tudiants s&eacute;lectionn&eacute;s. Si un remplacement est n&eacute;cessaire en raison d&rsquo;un d&eacute;sistement, l&rsquo;&eacute;tudiant substitut doit &ecirc;tre choisi parmi le m&ecirc;me groupe de candidats initialement soumis par l&rsquo;&eacute;tablissement. Les protocoles d&rsquo;entente (MOU) sont-ils toujours requis pour participer au programme ?
L&rsquo;existence d&rsquo;un MOU ou d&rsquo;une entente d&rsquo;&eacute;change n&rsquo;est plus un crit&egrave;re d&rsquo;admissibilit&eacute;, y compris pour la mobilit&eacute; au premier cycle.
Les &eacute;tablissements ne sont pas tenus de t&eacute;l&eacute;verser les documents utilis&eacute;s pour s&eacute;lectionner les b&eacute;n&eacute;ficiaires ultimes, &agrave; l&rsquo;exception de l&rsquo;&eacute;nonc&eacute; de confidentialit&eacute;.

Help with My EduCanada
My EduCanada is an online portal for applying for scholarships offered by Global Affairs Canada.
To register a My EduCanada account, you will first need to sign in using Global Affairs Canada Single Sign On Services. You have an option to sign in with your Canadian bank ( Interac &reg;) or to create a GCKey account.
Help with GCKey or Interac&reg; sign-in services Contact GCKey or Interac&reg; sign-in services
Only Canadian citizens and permanent residents can create an individual account to apply for scholarships. International students, researchers and faculty cannot apply directly for scholarships offered by Global Affairs Canada. Canadian host institutions must apply on their behalf.
Create a My EduCanada account
My EduCanada offers two types of user accounts.
Create an institution account
(Canadian institutions only)
Go to the application guidelines for the program you wish to apply to
Find the &ldquo;Application process&rdquo; section
Click &ldquo;Login or Register&rdquo;
Sign in with your Canadian bank ( Interac &reg;) or create a GCKey account (recommended)
On &ldquo;Register your My EduCanada account&rdquo; page:
provide your email address
select &ldquo;Institution&rdquo; as your account type
Read the Privacy Notice Statement carefully
Select your Canadian academic institution from the drop-down menu
Complete the Canadian institution representative information
Click &ldquo;Create your My EduCanada account&rdquo;
Create an individual account
(student, researcher or faculty)
Go to the application guidelines for the program you wish to apply to
Find the &ldquo;Application process&rdquo; section
Click &ldquo;Login or Register&rdquo;
Sign in with your Canadian bank ( Interac &reg;) or create a GCKey account
On &ldquo;Register your My EduCanada account&rdquo; page:
provide your email address
select &ldquo;Individual&rdquo; as your account type
select your profile from the drop-down menu
Read the Privacy Notice Statement carefully
Complete your personal information
Select your preferred language of correspondence
Click &ldquo;Create your My EduCanada account&rdquo;
Activate your My EduCanada account
Once you create your My EduCanada account, an invitation code will be sent to the email address you provided at the registration. You must activate your account first to be able to apply for scholarships.
To activate your account
Go to your email account that you provided during the registration process.
Locate the email with the invitation code and follow the instructions.
Can&rsquo;t find the activation email?
Check your spam folder.
Resend an invitation code to your email address and follow the instructions.
