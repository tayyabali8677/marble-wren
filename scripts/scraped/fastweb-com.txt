What are Scholarships?
Many high school students, parents and current college students are shocked to hear there are thousands upon thousands of college scholarships available to college students in the United States.
A scholarship is FREE money (money you will not have to pay back after graduation). It’s not a student loan; scholarships are a gift that does not have to be repaid. As the cost of college continues to rise, applying for scholarships should be a priority for all college-bound students and current college students.
Scholarship money can be used to pay for tuition and fees, but also room and board and the hidden (sometimes forgotten cost) of pricey university textbooks.
Uncovering scholarships for college students and college bound students can feel overwhelming. When looking for ways to pay for college, parents and students want to know how to get scholarships for college, how to apply for college scholarships, and how to find college scholarships. The best way to do this is to look through a complete list of college scholarships—even better,
let your Fastweb profile find scholarships for you .

Tips to Become a Scholarship Winner
In time, you could be the next student featured for a big scholarship win! But first, you must put in the work. Below are tips, tricks, and strategies to maximize your chances of
winning a scholarship.
Update your Fastweb Profile .
New scholarships are added to our database every day; and each semester, you experience changes in your GPA, extracurricular activity schedule, and employment. For these two reasons, you need to
update your Fastweb profile
frequently. In doing so, you’re ensuring that we’re matching you to every scholarship opportunity that you qualify for from our database of 1.5 million scholarships.
Check your email for scholarship notifications.
Each time a new scholarship is added to our database, we’ll send you an email notification that a scholarship you qualify for is now available. Make sure you are
signed up to receive scholarship notifications as well as newsletters
, so that you can be the first to know about new scholarship opportunities.
Make applying to scholarships a habit.
Consistency is key in the scholarship search and setting realistic goals can help you maintain your commitment. Make it a practice to work on one
scholarship application
each day, or commit to applying to one or two scholarships per week. Keep in mind that the more scholarships you apply to, the greater your chances of winning.
