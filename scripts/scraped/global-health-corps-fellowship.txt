Changing the who and the how of leadership is key to transforming global and public health.
We have the medicine. We have the technology. We need the leaders.
The right kinds of leaders – those with humility , empathy , flexibility , and adaptiveness – aren’t born. They’re developed intentionally through sustained support, training, mentorship, and lifelong access to resources and opportunities.
Since 2009, GHC has cultivated 1,400+ health leaders through our fellowship and alumni programs, and we’re just getting started.
