Tono whakauru
Apply and enrol
Find out how to join us—apply for your chosen programme of study and select your courses to complete your enrolment.
Step 1: Apply for a new qualification
Apply for a place on a new certificate, diploma, Bachelor’s, Honours, or Master’s degree programme using our Pūaha portal.
Apply now Not sure where to start?
Step 2: Select your courses online
Complete your enrolment by selecting your courses—once you’ve received your Offer of Place.
Select courses now Not sure what to do?
Current and returning students
You need to select your courses and re-enrol for each year of a multi-year degree. If you're returning to do Honours or start a Master's degree, your first step is to apply for the new qualification.
How to re-enrol
How to apply
Upcoming dates
Apply by 20 January 2027 to start a new programme of study in Trimester 1 (some programmes may have an earlier application deadline).
Check if you can commence your study in Trimester 3 . Admission applications are due by 19 November 2026.
There are different deadlines for international students .
Dates and deadlines
Find all the dates you need, including deadlines for submitting your application to study and for completing the enrolment process&mdash;and when each University trimester starts.
Key dates
New international students
Apply online using our Pūaha portal, or apply as an agent through our agent portal. Find out more about applying (and other useful information) on our site for international students.
Apply as an international student
Agent portal
PhD and doctoral students
If you want to do a PhD or other doctorate, apply through the Wellington Faculty of Graduate Research. It's the same process for both domestic and international students.
How to apply
How to apply and enrol Getting into university Dates and deadlines Course advice Explore degrees Explore courses
Need help?

Types of scholarships
Find out about the different types of scholarships available at Victoria University of Wellington.
Undergraduate scholarships
Find scholarships for undergraduate students—from school-leaver and first-year students to those already partway through a bachelor's degree.
Postgraduate scholarships
The University offers scholarships supporting postgraduate study at Honours, Master’s, and doctoral (PhD) levels.
International scholarships
Find scholarships for international students studying at Te Herenga Waka—Victoria University of Wellington, including how to apply and who to contact.
Summer Research Scholarship Programme
If you’re in third-year or above, interested and skilled in research, you could spend the summer supporting a research project and earn a valuable scholarship.
Other scholarships
A list of other New Zealand award databases and tertiary scholarship providers.
Financial hardship scholarships
If you're struggling financially, there are a number of scholarships and grants that could help. You can search for financial hardship scholarships on our database, or apply for:
the University's Hardship Fund
a Victoria Hardship Fund Equity Grant
a Victoria Doctoral Hardship Scholarship .
You can also get in touch with one of our student finance advisers for personal advice and support with money issues.
