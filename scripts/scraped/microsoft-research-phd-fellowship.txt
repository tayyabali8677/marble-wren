About
The Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship is a global program that identifies and empowers the next generation of exceptional computing research talent. Microsoft recognizes the value of diversity in computing and aims to increase the pipeline of talent receiving advanced degrees in computing-related fields to build a stronger and inclusive computing-related research community. We currently offer PhD fellowships in Asia-Pacific, Canada and the United States, EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa), Latin America, Australia and New Zealand.
Over the last two decades, the Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship program has supported over 700 fellows around the world, many of whom have gone on to work at Microsoft. Others have gone on to perform pioneering research elsewhere within the technology industry or accept faculty appointments at leading universities.

What are the pricing options for Microsoft 365 Education and Office 365?
Both Microsoft 365 Education and Office 365 Education have various pricing options for schools. View the product comparison table and contact a sales partner to discuss your school’s needs.
02/
How do I get Microsoft Office 365 Education?
Microsoft donates  Office 365 Education  for the classroom to eligible institutions at no cost to ensure all students have access to tools for learning and building skills for the future. Office 365 Education provides web versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Microsoft Teams, and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, plus additional AI-based tools for work and learning. Enter your school email address  to get started . If you’re a school seeking additional assistance in signing up your school for Microsoft Office 365,  Contact a sales partner .
03/
How can I get a Microsoft Education discount?
Students and educators are eligible for a 10% discount on select items in the Microsoft Store with an active Microsoft account. (A Microsoft account is an email address and password that you use with Outlook.com (Hotmail), Office, OneDrive, Skype, Xbox, and/or Windows.) If you don’t have an active Microsoft account, see the Microsoft Store for discount details .

01/
How does ISV Success support AI application development?
ISV Success provides resources for building AI-powered apps with Microsoft Foundry, access to Azure Cognitive Services, and training sessions for using AI tools. Copilot developers can also take advantage of additional software company support, including events, expert consultations, and product benefits.
Learn more
02/
Who’s eligible for ISV Success benefits and what’s the duration?
Software development companies who are building business-to-business (B2B) applications on the Microsoft Cloud and intend to publish through Microsoft Marketplace are eligible for ISV Success benefits—free for 12 months with no commitment to renew.
After 12 months, participants are eligible for a discounted renewal package with a $1,550 (USD) annual fee.
Apply now
03/
Can benefits from ISV Success be combined with other programs?
Yes, ISV Success benefits can complement other Microsoft offerings. You can’t stack Azure sponsorships, but you can toggle between subscriptions.
Learn more
04/
What are the criteria for additional ISV Success benefits?
For renewal, you need to continue developing B2B apps, meet publication requirements, and maintain your Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program status. The annual renewal fee for the Core Package is $1,550 (USD).
For the Expanded Package, you’ll be invited by your ISV Success Engagement Manager based on performance criteria and intent to transact through Marketplace.
Learn more
05/
How does ISV Success enhance application sales in Marketplace?
Through Marketplace Rewards, software development companies receive sales and marketing support, including listing optimization, social promotion, and Azure Sponsorship to create and run demos—helping you significantly boost marketplace sales.
Learn more
06/
How does ISV Success aid in meeting Azure IP co-sell requirements?
ISV Success helps facilitate the publication of transactable offers through Microsoft Marketplace, which is essential for accessing co-sell benefits, including technical consultations that can help boost your listing and accelerate sales.
Additionally, you can access on-demand resources, Office Hours, and the Marketplace Community to get questions answered about transactable publishing.
Learn more
07/
Does ISV Success support professional services?
ISV Success supports software development companies that offer transactable professional services only if the service is attached to a transactable app owned by the same software company. Publishing a professional service alone does not qualify for enrollment or renewal.
For Marketplace Rewards, Marketplace Billed Sales (MBS) from professional services will not count towards the MBS tier. Only MBS from transactable apps will unlock Marketplace Rewards benefits.
Learn more
