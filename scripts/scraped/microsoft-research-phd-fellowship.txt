About
The Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship is a global program that identifies and empowers the next generation of exceptional computing research talent. Microsoft recognizes the value of diversity in computing and aims to increase the pipeline of talent receiving advanced degrees in computing-related fields to build a stronger and inclusive computing-related research community. We currently offer PhD fellowships in Asia-Pacific, Canada and the United States, EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa), Latin America, Australia and New Zealand.
Over the last two decades, the Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship program has supported over 700 fellows around the world, many of whom have gone on to work at Microsoft. Others have gone on to perform pioneering research elsewhere within the technology industry or accept faculty appointments at leading universities.

01/
What are the pricing options for Microsoft 365 Education and Office 365?
Both Microsoft 365 Education and Office 365 Education have various pricing options for schools. View the product comparison table and contact a sales partner to discuss your school’s needs.
02/
How do I get Microsoft Office 365 Education?
Microsoft donates  Office 365 Education  for the classroom to eligible institutions at no cost to ensure all students have access to tools for learning and building skills for the future. Office 365 Education provides web versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Microsoft Teams, and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, plus additional AI-based tools for work and learning. Enter your school email address  to get started . If you’re a school seeking additional assistance in signing up your school for Microsoft Office 365,  Contact a sales partner .
03/
How can I get a Microsoft Education discount?
Students and educators are eligible for a 10% discount on select items in the Microsoft Store with an active Microsoft account. (A Microsoft account is an email address and password that you use with Outlook.com (Hotmail), Office, OneDrive, Skype, Xbox, and/or Windows.) If you don’t have an active Microsoft account, see the Microsoft Store for discount details .

## Build & Publish

### Build a Marketplace-ready solution—faster

Use development credits, sandboxes, and technical guidance to prepare your solution for Microsoft Marketplace publication and transactability. Build & Publish benefits include:

*   Technical and presales deployment consultations for hands-on guidance during upfront solution development.
*   AI commercialization skilling to guide buyer- and offer-type alignment.
*   A development sandbox and credits, including Microsoft Foundry and GitHub Copilot.

[Browse resources](https://partner.microsoft.com/asset/collection/frontier-acc-mktplace-resource#/)

### Drive revenue with targeted go-to-market (GTM) activation

Use targeted GTM benefits and resources to broaden your reach and work toward early Marketplace revenue. Grow demand and accelerate sales with:

*   Earned $30,000 Azure sponsorship for no-cost trials and accelerated deal closure.
*   Personalized listing optimization to drive greater visibility.
*   GTM resources designed to enable demand generation.
*   Engagement manager guidance on co-sell requirements and readiness.

[Browse resources](https://partner.microsoft.com/asset/collection/frontier-acc-mktplace-resource#/)

Expand your go-to-market (GTM) motions with milestone-based investments and solution differentiation as your Marketplace momentum grows. Scale with:

*   Solutions Partner* with certified software** designations to signal solution credibility and differentiation.
*   Enterprise propensity scoring to prioritize deal opportunities.
*   Accrued GTM benefits to drive new customer acquisition and expanded deal size.

### **Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace Premium**

This paid path is for partners seeking additional technical guidance, Azure sponsorship, go-to-market benefits, and co-sell readiness guidance. This path offers all Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace offerings, plus:

*   Development environments and tools, including $30,000 of Azure sponsorships to build and test solutions
*   Technical presales and deployment consultations throughout solution development
*   Enhanced go-to-market engagement and benefits
*   Co-sell readiness guidance

[Find out more about Premium](https://aka.ms/FA-for-Marketplace-Playbook "Go to the partner playbook.")

*   Become an active [Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program](https://partner.microsoft.com/partnership "Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program") member.
*   Once enrolled in the program, continue to meet program requirements.

*   Reach a global audience and transact at scale by enrolling in [Microsoft Marketplace](https://partner.microsoft.com/partnership/marketplace "Microsoft Marketplace").
