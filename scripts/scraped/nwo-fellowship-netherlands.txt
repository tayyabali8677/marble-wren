Find Funding
Each year, NWO invests almost 1 billion euros in curiosity-driven research, research related to societal challenges and research infrastructure. Researchers and research institutions can submit an application for funding for research projects, large equipment and databases as soon as NWO publishes a call for project proposals. Here you find all funding opportunities that NWO offers.
All calls
The call for proposals describes the aim of a grant round, who can apply, the amount of money available, which criteria your application needs to meet, how and when you can submit your application.
Search in all calls
Recently modified calls
An overview of the calls that have been changed in the past four weeks. This usually concerns the deadline, a text change or replaced files.
View the recently modified calls
Funding lines
NWO processes all applications for funding for a research proposal according to a fixed procedure. Read how the application procedure is organised, from the request to the assessment of an application to the decision.
View the funding lines
Applying for funding, but how does it work?
As soon as the Dutch Research Council publishes a call open for application, you can apply. Read more about the application process.
How does it work?
Laws and regulations concerning funding
Here you can consult legislation, policy rules and codes of conduct that define NWO's tasks and responsibilities .
View the laws and regulations
Project management
Once NWO has approved your research proposal, there are several phases of project management. Read more about the start-up, duration and closure phases and the associated guidelines.
Project management
Partners in science
With the Dutch Research Agenda (NWA) and participation in the Knowledge and Innovation Covenant (KIC), NWO aims to achieve scientific breakthroughs and stimulate innovation.
Partners in science
Consortium formation
NWO encourages and supports collaborations between different research disciplines, between practice-oriented and fundamental research, and with civil society organisations and businesses by organising meetings and workshops. Read more about consortium formation.
Consortium formation
