Funding lines
NWO harmonises the current funding instruments as much as possible to facilitate collaboration. As a result, researchers, irrespective of the research domain, will be subject to the same conditions as much as possible. Harmonisation will facilitate both applicants and NWO to work more across the boundaries of disciplines and domains.
NWO will provide a limited palette of funding instruments with a clear number of modules. These modules can be combined in accordance with the objectives of the programme or call concerned. This approach will provide the flexibility needed to meet the needs of the various disciplines.
Objective each funding line
The harmonisation of instruments will provide the following palette of funding lines and each line will have a distinct objective:
Open Competition
Funding of curiosity-driven research in the four scientific domains, thus enabling researchers to conduct research into a subject of their own choice without being bound by thematic conditions.
Choose one of the following domains:
Science
Social Sciences and Humanities (SSH)
Applied and Engineering Sciences (AES)
ZonMw (Health research)
Talent Programme
Personal funding for individual researchers, whether working in teams or otherwise. For example, we finance curiosity-driven research aligned to the various stages in the careers of researchers through the Talent Programme.
NWO Talent programme Veni | Vidi | Vici
Rubicon
Collaboration with knowledge users and society
Projects or programmes for thematic research and valorisation in partnership with external public and/or private parties. These projects or programmes are designed to increase and accelerate the economic or social impact of the research. Examples are programmes or projects falling under the auspices of the National Research Agenda, the Knowledge and Innovation Covenant (focusing on major challenges facing society which require more knowledge and ground-breaking innovations) and the National Growth Fund (large-scale investments to promote long-term economic growth). The international and European thematic calls also fall under this funding line.
KIC (Knowledge and Innovation Covenant)
NWA (Dutch Research Agenda)
NGF (National Growth Fund)
International programme
Practice-oriented research
Investment in the professionalisation, quality improvement and self-organisation of practice-oriented research in the universities of applied sciences. This funding is provided using different instruments managed by the Taskforce for Applied Research SIA .
Infrastructure
We are committed to a balanced and future-proof ecosystem of high-quality scientific infrastructure for all fields of science. This includes highly specialised equipment, data collections and digital infrastructure. Access, innovation and continuity are always key. Read more
Innovation Accelerator
We accelerate the path from research results to applications in society. We create space for the development and further development of innovative services and products based on knowledge in a wide range of sectors. We do this through a range of different options, such as grants, training courses and loans, tailored to the needs of researchers. Read more
Modules
In the majority of cases, the programmes and projects from the aforementioned funding lines will be built up from the following modules:
Personnel
Researcher in training/PhD, postdoc, researcher, non-scientific personnel
Materials
Investments: infrastructure and data files
Knowledge utilisation
Internationalisation
Citizen Science

Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership: Fourth Joint Transnational Co-funded Call
Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership: Fourth Joint Transnational Co-funded Call
Building a sustainable and innovative Blue Economy: mastering digital opportunities and fostering resilience
Experienced researchers can apply for funding for international research projects that contribute to finding solutions towards a climate-neutral, sustainable, productive and competitive blue economy in Europe. This is the fourth call within the European co-funded partnership Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership (SBEP) under Horizon Europe.

2
Prepare
3
Submit
Purpose
The SBEP call 2026 for proposals pools national and regional funding for the development of knowledge and innovation that boost the transition to a climate neutral, sustainable and productive Blue Economy.
This SBEP Call 2026 co-funded call aims to support transnational research and innovation addressing the priority areas below:
Transitioning the blue economy sectors for sustainability and strategic autonomy
Managing sea-uses by advancing digital and ecosystem-based approaches
Promoting sustainable blue bioresources through innovation in marine production and harvesting
Building resilience in coastal communities with infrastructure and businesses
The proposals are strongly recommended to consider a minimum of two EU sea basins which are: the Atlantic Ocean, Baltic Sea, Black Sea, Mediterranean Sea, and North Sea. Proposals can target European regional seas such as Adriatic Sea, Aegean Sea, the Arctic, Barents Sea, Celtic Sea, etc. but they must be from a minimum of 2 different EU sea basins.
Applications are submitted to the joint call secretariat via the European EPSS system. For more information, see: Funding opportunities | Bluepartnership . For Dutch participation in applications, submission of national documents is mandatory; see under ‘Preparation’.
Who can apply
Applications need to be submitted by international research consortia including teams from at least three countries participating in the call. For more details refer to the call for proposals.
For Dutch participation in proposals, funding can be requested at NWO by professors, associate professors, assistant professors, professors of applied sciences, and other researchers holding a comparable appointment at universities, universities of applied sciences, university medical centres, KNAW and NWO institutes, TO2 institutes, and the research institutes listed in Annex B of the Call for Proposals: The National/Regional Regulations, The Netherlands.
An application for NWO funding has one national main applicant, responsible for scientific and financial management. National co-applicants within an application for NWO funding are also allowed.
Applicants may be involved in a maximum of one proposal as national main applicant and/or national co-applicant (as part of a European consortium).
More information on the eligibility criteria, including the requirements from other countries, can be found in the 4th SBEP Call 2026 Documents.
Consortium building
To find European partners you can use the partner search platform . This is not obligatory.
What to apply for
A total of €1,400,000 is made available for Dutch participation in the 4th SBEP Call 2026.
Per application, a maximum of €400,000 can be requested from NWO if there is one national main applicant, and up to €600,000 if there is both a national main applicant and a co-applicant. The application concerns a project with a maximum duration of 3 years.
There is no limit on the size of the international consortium (neither financially nor in terms of the number of partners); the national funding organisations only impose a limit on the amount that can be requested nationally by each partner.
The 4th SBEP Call 2026 also allows for the inclusion of 'self-funded partners' in the international consortium.
Eligible costs for NWO funding can be found in the SBEP Call 2026, Annex B: National Contact Points and National/Regional regulations. Please note: PhD positions cannot be applied for in this call, due to the maximum project duration of 3 years. Moreover, NWO funds project-related costs. Therefore overhead costs are not eligible for NWO funding.
When to apply
Deadline for submitting a pre-proposal (compulsory) is November 16th, 2026, 15:00 CET .
Deadline for submitting a full proposal is June 14th, 2027, 15:00 CEST .
Proposals need to be submitted through the electronic submission system of SBEP:
https://proposals.etag.ee/sustainable-blue
More information can be found in the 4th SBEP Call Documents.
Please note: Applicants requesting funding from NWO must submit the completed ‘NWO budget template’ separately to NWO when submitting the full proposal. The format for this form can be found under ‘Downloads for your application’ in the section ‘Preparation’. It is recommended to share this document with NWO already at the time of submitting the pre-proposal, in order to receive feedback that can still be incorporated into the full proposal."
Assessment
Criteria
Eligible Pre-proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following two criteria:
Excellence
Impact
Eligible Full proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following three criteria:
Excellence
Impact
Quality and efficiency of the implementation
Please see the full SBEP Call 2026 Documents for the criteria including the sub-criteria.
Procedure
The assessment in this call will be done in two stages. In the first stage, consortia are invited to submit a pre-proposal. Only consortia that were successful in the first stage can submit a full proposal. Eligible proposals – both in the first and in the second stage – will be assessed by an independent Evaluation Committee consisting of experts from relevant scientific fields. The proposals will be ranked based on the criteria in the call text. Based on the ranking and the budget available, the national and regional funding agencies will take the funding decision. At NWO the Executive Board will take this decision.
More information
More information can be found on the SBEP website
Submitting an application can be done via the electronic submission system of SBEP ( https://proposals.etag.ee/sustainable-blue/ ).
Don’t forget to also submit the mandatory NWO budget form.
