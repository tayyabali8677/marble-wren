Reasons to Choose Japan
The cultural heritage of Mt. Fuji, Japanese cuisine, and much more; the spirit of Omotenashi (hospitality)
Japan’s rich natural environments, from mountain forests to beaches and coastlines, offer all-new sights with the coming of each season. The country consists of eight major regions that each have their own distinct culture, traditions, and natural scenery. When selecting where you want to study in Japan, it would also be interesting to know about the regional uniqueness of each school’s location.

Scholarship and tuition reduction system
You may search for scholarships and tuition reduction/exemption programs for international students
offered independently by each school, as well as scholarships provided by local governments,
international exchange organizations, and private scholarship organizations. Please note that the search
information is supplied by the schools or the scholarship-granting organizations, but not all
scholarship programs may be searchable. Additionally, please directly confirm the latest information
with the schools or scholarship organizations. Scholarship information provided by scholarship
organizations is compiled in the 'Scholarship for International Students in Japan.' *Here is the
'Scholarship for International Students in Japan'.
