Scholarships for international students
At Birmingham, we believe in removing all barriers to education, so all of our talented students can achieve their full potential. Explore our wide variety of scholarships available for international students.
More than &pound;10 million in scholarships is available for international students coming to study at Birmingham.
At Birmingham, we are dedicated to supporting international students to join our university and recognise those who excel in their subject through our competitive scholarships offer.
There may also be funding available from other sponsors, governmental or funding bodies to help you fund your studies.
All of these scholarships celebrate outstanding achievement and recognise talent from around the world who want to study at a global top 100 university.

International fees
As an international student, studying at the university will require you to pay for your tuition fees and cover your living expenses.
We charge an annual tuition fee, covering the cost of your tuition, examinations, access to IT facilities and the library. It also includes your membership with the Guild of Students.
The cost of your tuition fee depends on the programme you choose to study, and the level at which you study. If you are made an offer to study by the University, the yearly tuition fee will be quoted on your offer letter.
You&rsquo;ll pay your fees at the start of your programme.

Commonwealth Shared Scholarship Scheme
The Commonwealth Shared Scholarship Scheme provides scholarships for students from Commonwealth countries who would not otherwise be able to study in the UK.

Birmingham (Edgbaston)
The Commonwealth Shared Scholarship Scheme is a joint initiative between the UK Department for International Development (DFID) and UK universities, including the University of Birmingham, to jointly support scholarships for students from developing Commonwealth countries who would not otherwise be able to study in the United Kingdom.
Applications for 2026 entry open on 12 November 2025 and close on 9 December 2025 at 16:00 (GMT); we strongly encourage you to check the Commonwealth Shared Scholarships webpage for more information: Commonwealth Shared Scholarships - Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK
The award is financed jointly by the University and the DFID. The award will cover:
Full tuition fees
Maintenance stipend
Air fare to and from the United Kingdom
Immigration Health Surcharge costs
A one-off arrival allowance payment
Thesis expenses
Study travel grant
Optical and Dental allowance
Who can apply?
Candidates should be commencing a course in September 2026.
Candidates must also certify that they:
are nationals of a Commonwealth developing country, and not at present living or studying in a developed country;
have not undertaken studies lasting one year or more in a developed country;
are themselves, or through their families, unable to pay to study in the United Kingdom;
agree to return to their own country to work or study as soon as the award ends. The commission will only consider requests to extend leave to remain in the UK post-award for those wishing to move from Masters to PhD study. Strict conditions will apply.
DFID requires that the University certifies when submitting its shortlist of candidates that they are sufficiently proficient in the English language to cope with the course for which they have applied. This will normally mean that you will have to have satisfied any requirements concerning competence in the English language which are a condition of your offer of admission.
You can read more about the eligibility criteria by visiting the Commonwealth Scholarship Comission's website.
Candidates must also have been offered a place at the University of Birmingham on one of the following postgraduate programmes:
MA Africa and Development
MSc Environment, Development and Politics
MSc International Development
MSc Respiratory Medicine
MSc Conflict, Security and Development
Eligible nationalities:
Bangladesh, Botswana, Belize, Cameroon, Ghana, Dominica, Fiji, Gabon, Gambia, Grenada, Guyana, India, Kenya, Kiribati, Lesotho, Malawi, Malaysia, Maldives, Mauritius, Mozambique, Namibia, Nauru, Nigeria, Pakistan, Papua New Guinea, Rwanda, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Samoa, Sierra Leone, Solomon Islands, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Swaziland, Tanzania, Togo, Tonga, Tuvalu, Uganda, Vanuatu, and Zambia
How to Apply
In order to apply for this scholarship, you must
Make an application on the Commonwealth Shared Scholarship website by the deadline given by Commonwealth Scholarships
Have made an application to study one of the approved programmes at the University of Birmingham
Ask a Question
For further information on the Commonwealth Shared Scholarship please contact Funding, Graduation and Awards at fga@contacts.bham.ac.uk
Alternatively more information can be found on the Commonwealth Commissions website .

Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027
Automatic £5,000 scholarships are available for international students from select countries choosing to pursue a Masters degree with us in January 2027.

The Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027 offers an automatic &pound;5,000 tuition fee discount to international-fee-paying students from select countries choosing to study a taught Masters programme at the Birmingham campus in January 2027.
I believe that the scholarship will do credit to my resume and benefit my future career coming from a Russell Group University.
Zhenyu Xu
Who can apply?
To be eligible for this scholarship, applicants must meet the following criteria:
You must be intending to study full-time on one of the below eligible programmes in January 2027 on our UK campus:
Advanced Engineering Management MSc
Computer Science MSc
Digital Media and Communications MA
Digital Media and Creative Industries MA
Education MA
Financial Management MSc
Human Resource Management MSc
International Business MSc
Marketing MSc
Masters in Public Administration MPA
Public Management MSc
You must be domiciled in one of the select countries listed below.
You must be classified as an international-fee-payer for tuition purposes by the University.
You must be self-funding your tuition fees. Students in receipt of a full or partial tuition fee scholarship or sponsorship from an external organisation are not eligible. If the University becomes aware that a student is sponsored by a third-party organisation, the student will be disqualified from receiving the Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship. If the scholarship has already been awarded, it will be withdrawn. Please ensure you accurately declare your funding source during enrolment.
Eligible countries
Open to all nationalities but must be living in (domiciled) one of the following countries:
Argentina, Bangladesh, Bolivia, Cameroon, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Egypt, Ghana, India, Indonesia, Iran, Ivory Coast, Jamaica, Kenya, Malaysia, Mauritius, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Peru, Senegal, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, Thailand, Trinidad & Tobago, Turkey, Uganda, United Arab Emirates (UAE), Vietnam, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
How to Apply
For the &pound;5,000 Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship, no separate scholarship application is required. If you meet the eligibility criteria, the scholarship will automatically be deducted from your tuition fees. You must accept your unconditional offer by 23:59 (UK time) on 7 December 2026 .
Please consult the FAQ's below and our International Recruitment Scholarships general terms and conditions for further information on conditions and exclusions.
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the value of the award?
The Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027 offers a tuition fee discount of &pound;5,000 for eligible international students studying one of the eligible Masters degrees in January 2027 at our UK campus.
Do I have to make an application?
No. The &pound;5,000 Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027 is awarded automatically once you accept your unconditional offer by the deadline.
To receive an unconditional offer, you must meet all the conditions in your offer letter, including paying your deposit.
Is there a deadline?
Yes, the final deadline is 23:59 (UK time) on 7 December 2026. You must have accepted your unconditional offer by this time.
To receive an unconditional offer, you must meet all the conditions in your offer letter, including paying your deposit. You are strongly encouraged to accept your unconditional offer as soon as possible to ensure that your status is recorded in sufficient time as unconditionally accepted within the University's admissions system.
The University reserves the right to withdraw the scholarship offer to new applicants at any time.
What does &#x27;domiciled&#x27; mean?
Domicile means the country where you are permanently resident. It is not the same as nationality or citizenship.
In this context, being classed as &lsquo;domiciled&rsquo; in one of the selected countries means you must be a permanent resident in one of those countries.
How is the scholarship awarded and when will I hear if the scholarship has been confirmed?
If you meet the eligibility criteria, you will receive an email confirming your scholarship award once you have accepted your unconditional offer by the scholarship deadline.
You will also receive a Notification of Award email as confirmation of the scholarship being awarded to you. You should use this email in your visa application to UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI). The scholarship will also be reflected in your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)* which is used for visa application purposes. If you&rsquo;ve already received your CAS, there&rsquo;s no need for a new one&mdash;you can proceed with your visa application as usual.
The &pound;5,000 scholarship discount will then be applied against your tuition fees once you complete Online Registration and are marked as 'Normally Registered'. You will receive an email confirming the application of the &pound;5,000 discount. You will need to manually deduct the &pound;5,000 from the standard tuition fees outlined in your study offer letter.
* Please ensure you read the International Recruitment Scholarships general terms and conditions as the scholarship may not be valid under certain circumstances even if it is on your CAS.
Will I receive an updated tuition fee invoice?
No. If you are successfully awarded the Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027, your &pound;5,000 tuition fee waiver will be applied directly to your tuition fee account.
However, you will not receive an updated invoice reflecting the discounted value. During Online Registration, when setting up fee payment, you will need to manually deduct the &pound;5,000 discount from your full tuition fees to calculate the outstanding fees that you are liable for.
Please note invoices issued at the outset of the academic year from the Finance Office will always display the full tuition fee amount before any scholarship deductions are applied.
If you have already paid your full tuition fee in advance, you can request a refund for the scholarship amount once you've enrolled. Details on the refund process can be found on the University Intranet .
If you wish to check your tuition fee balance, please contact the Finance Office through their online portal.
For any questions related to tuition fee payments, please contact the Finance Office Accounts Receivable Team at: accountsreceivable@contacts.bham.ac.uk.
Am I eligible if I’m currently studying a Masters?
No, the scheme is not available to students already enrolled on a Masters-level taught programme.
Am I eligible if I will be studying at the University of Birmingham Dubai campus?
No, this scholarship is only available to students studying at the University of Birmingham&rsquo;s UK campus on one of the eligible full-time programmes.
I am from one of the eligible countries but living in the UK, am I eligible?
Applicants will not be eligible for the scholarship if they are permanently domiciled in the UK at the time of application as specified on their application. Applicants must be domiciled in one of the eligible countries at the time of application.
Does the course have to be full-time or does a part-time programme also qualify?
Only full-time study on one of the eligible January 2027 postgraduate taught Masters programmes qualify.
Do courses delivered remotely (online) qualify?
No. Courses advertised as solely being delivered as a &lsquo;distance&rsquo; or &lsquo;remote learning&rsquo; programme are not eligible for this scholarship. Only full-time on-campus study of one of the select programmes at our UK campus are eligible.
I’ve already applied for one of the taught Masters programmes, am I still eligible?
Yes. If you have already applied for an eligible taught Masters programme at the University of Birmingham&rsquo;s UK campus with a January 2027 start, you are still eligible for the Postgraduate High Flier Scholarship for January 2027, provided you meet all the other eligibility criteria.
I have been awarded another UoB tuition fee scholarship, am I eligible for this award?
This scholarship can be held in conjunction with scholarships issued by other University of Birmingham departments, provided the terms and conditions of both scholarships allow to do so. This award cannot be held in conjunction with any full-fee scholarships.
When reviewing and accepting scholarships, please review the terms and conditions for each scholarship as they may differ. Please notify the team issuing your scholarship of your acceptance or rejection as applies.
If the scholarship has already been awarded or is noted on your CAS, but cannot be held in conjunction with another scholarship, it will be withdrawn. Please ensure you accurately declare your funding source during enrolment.
I am receiving a third party tuition fee scholarship, am I eligible for this award?
Students in receipt of a partial or full tuition fee scholarship from an external organisation are not eligible.
If the University becomes aware that a student is in receipt of partial or full tuition fee funding by a third-party organisation, the student will be disqualified from receiving the Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship.
When reviewing and accepting scholarships please review the terms and conditions for each scholarship as they may differ. Please notify the team issuing your scholarship of your acceptance or rejection as applies.
If the scholarship has already been awarded and/or is noted on your CAS, the University reserves the right to withdraw the scholarship if it later becomes aware of information that makes the student ineligible.
How can I support the university if I accept this award?
By accepting this scholarship, awardees are expected to reasonably support the University by providing profiles, testimonials, and participating in ambassadorial activities if requested.
What are the Terms and Conditions for this scholarship?
Please consult our dedicated Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship for January 2027 terms and conditions .
In addition, please consult our International Recruitment Scholarships general terms and conditions for further information on conditions and exclusions.
