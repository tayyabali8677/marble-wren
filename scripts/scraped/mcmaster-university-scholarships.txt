Supplementary Applications
Supplementary Applications
What are Supplementary Applications?
Supplementary applications are extra components that accompany some of our program applications. They help us learn more about you.
The format and requirements for each supplementary application vary depending on the program. Some are called “Electronic Creative Submissions” or “CASPer Assessments” which have different requirements. Make sure that you’re aware of the requirements and deadlines for each program application.
What programs have Supplementary Applications and when are they due?
Level I Programs with a Supplementary Application:
Arts & Science (due February 1, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET)
Leadership & Civic Studies (Wilson College) (due February 1, 2026)
Business *optional submission* (due April 17, 2026)
Integrated Business & Humanities (due February 2, 2026)
Bachelor of Technology (due January 29, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET)
Computer Science (due January 29, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET)
Engineering (due January 29, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET)
iBioMed – Integrated Biomedical Engineering & Health Sciences (due January 29, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET)
Honours Health Sciences (due early/mid-February 2026)
Midwifery (Identity and Admissions Survey due February 1, 2026)
Nursing (Students sign up via CASPer for testing date)
iArts – Integrated Arts (due February 2, 2026)
Honours Integrated Science (due February 2, 2026, at noon ET)
Above Level I Programs with a Supplementary Application:
Actuarial & Financial Mathematics (due May 8, 2026)
Engineering (due April 16, 2026)
Computer Science (due April 16, 2026)
Honours Health Sciences (due early May 2026)
Health Sciences Biomedical Discovery & Commercialization Program (due February 1, 2026)
Justice, Political Philosophy & Law (due April 30, 2026)
Nursing – Accelerated Stream (applicants sign up for a CASPer testing date)
Physician Assistant (The supplementary application will be released by February 20 and must be completed by noon (ET) on February 26, 2026)
Social Work (Honours) (due February 2, 2026)
Important information about Supplementary Applications
If you’re submitting a supplementary application, here are a few important considerations:
Your supplementary application status may not show as “Complete” on your checklist until after the supplementary application deadline.
Faculty of Engineering supplementary applications are completed through the Kira Talent platform. If you’re applying to multiple programs within the Faculty, you only need to complete the Kira Talent supplementary application once.
If you’re applying to Midwifery and/or multiple McMaster Nursing sites (McMaster/Mohawk), you only have to complete CASPer once.

English Proficiency
English Proficiency
Acceptable Language Tests & Minimum Scores
The language of instruction at McMaster University is English. Below are the only English Language Proficiency tests accepted by McMaster, along with their respective minimum score requirements are listed below.
NOTE: Typically, the English language proficiency test result must be written within two years of starting at McMaster. For the September 2026 entry, we will accept a test result as long as it is valid at the time you submit your application.
If you are applying to a graduate program, please go to the  School of Graduate Studies website  for all information regarding Language Requirements and application instructions.
Expandable List
Testing Exemptions
At the discretion of the university, you may be exempted from McMaster’s English proficiency testing requirements if you meet one of the following requirements  (exemption is not automatic) :
Attended, in full-time academic studies (non-ESL), an accredited Secondary School (High School) or Post-Secondary College in an  English-speaking country  for  at least   four years  immediately prior to your anticipated start date at McMaster,  OR
Attended, in full-time academic studies (non-ESL), an accredited English medium Secondary School (High School) or Post-Secondary College for  at least four years  immediately prior to your anticipated start date at McMaster,*  OR
Attended, in full-time academic studies (non-ESL), an accredited English medium University for  at least one year  immediately prior to your anticipated start date at McMaster,  OR
Resided in an English speaking country for at least four years immediately prior to your anticipated start date at McMaster.
*Students completing certain international curricula (i.e. GCE, IB, AP) may be considered for an English Language Proficiency Test waiver with high grades in acceptable courses.
Important Instructions/Notes
McMaster only accepts the tests listed below for English language proficiency.
It is your responsibility to make all arrangements regarding the writing of the English Language Proficiency tests and to have the official score report forwarded to the Admissions Office directly from the testing centre in a timely manner.
Please refer to our  deadlines  and  documentation  pages.
The McMaster University institution code for  ETS/TOEFL  is  0936.
The IELTS Organization ID for McMaster University undergraduate admissions is  114599 .
IELTS, DUOLINGO and TOEFL must be sent directly to McMaster electronically from the testing centre.
Canadian citizenship, in and of itself, is not enough to satisfy the English Language Requirements, nor does a credit in Grade 12 English (or the equivalent)
Students attending a Canadian curriculum offshore school in China are required to submit an English Language Proficiency test
Students currently attending an Ontario high school and previously attended a Canadian curriculum offshore in China will be required to provide an English Language Proficiency test
The University reserves the right to  require applicants with an English Language Proficiency score disparate from their academic English  prerequisite achievement to  present further evidence of proficiency .
If evidence of misrepresentation of fraudulent or falsified documentation is found, the penalty is severe.  McMaster University reserves the right to rescind any Offer of Admission and/or scholarship.
McMaster English Language Development Diploma (MELD)
If you are a student who meets all academic requirements but  does not meet  the minimum English-language proficiency scores detailed above, you may still qualify for an offer of admission that is conditional upon the successful completion of the MELD program.
The MELD Diploma is a 1-year (two academic terms) intensive transition program designed for students whose primary language is not English. MELD focuses on the development of academic English, acculturation, and engagement with the university community.
For more information about the program and how to apply, please see the  MELD website.
Summer Transition Through English Prep Certificate (STEP)
If you are a student who meets all academic requirements but  almost meet  the minimum English-language proficiency scores detailed below, you may still qualify for a conditional offer of admission upon the successful completion of the STEP program.
The STEP certificate is an intensive seven-week academic bridging program designed for English-language learners who wish to pursue undergraduate studies, and  almost meet  the university’s language proficiency requirements. During the STEP program, students will develop the skills necessary for a successful transition in September to their undergraduate program:
Reinforce literacy skills
Heighten academic skills
Learn to convey ideas effectively
Strengthen critical and analytical skills
Develop best practices for academic communication
For more information about the program and how to apply, please see the  STEP website.
My Test Score Didn't Pass What Now?
If your English language test results do not meet our minimum requirements, your checklist will remain as ‘Required’. To proceed you have three options:
Rewrite the English language proficiency test and submit the results again. You may rewrite the test as many times as you like but results must be submitted by April 1, 2026 . If your new scores meet the minimum requirements, your checklist will be updated with ‘Complete’ and we will continue to process your application. You cannot proceed with your application until this requirement is met.
Apply to the McMaster English Language Development (MELD) diploma program . This  8-month program  is designed to improve your English skills while giving you university credit. Upon successful completion, you will progress to the level 1 undergraduate program for which you are applying. You can add one of the MELD programs as an option to your application via the  OUAC  if you would like to be considered.
Opt-in to the Summer Transition through English Prep (STEP) certificate program.  This intensive  6-week academic English bridging program  is designed to develop students’ academic English skills necessary for a successful transition in September. Applicants to the MELD program may receive a conditional offer notifying your eligibility for STEP, together with an opt-in form into the program. Upon successful completion of STEP in the summer, you will immediately progress to your undergraduate studies in September.
Minimum Acceptable Scores
IELTS – International English Language Testing System (Academic and Academic UKVI)
6.5 overall  with a minimum of 6.0 in each of the four components (Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening); results valid for 2 years
5.0 overall  for  MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program
6.5 overall  for  STEP  (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 6 in reading and writing, and minimum score of 5.5 in speaking and listening
NOTE: results must be sent directly to McMaster electronically from the testing centre. Select IELTS Organization ID 114599.
Please note: we accept the IELTS One Skill Retake (OSR).
TOEFL – Test of English as a Foreign Language
Tests completed on or after January 21, 2026
IBT: 4.5 overall with a minimum score of 4.0 in the Reading, Writing, Speaking components and a 4.5 Listening. Valid for 2 years
3.5 overall  for  MELD (McMaster English Development) Program
4.5 overall for  STEP (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 4.0 in Reading & Writing and minimum score of 3.0 in Speaking & Listening.
NOTE: The institution code for ETS/TOEFL is 0936. Results must be sent directly to McMaster electronically from the testing centre
Please note we  do  accept TOEFL Home Edition. We  do not  accept TOEFL Essentials or TOEFL MyBest scores.
TOEFL – Test of English as a Foreign Language
Tests completed before January 21, 2026
IBT: 86 overall  with a minimum score of 20 in each of the four components (Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening); valid for 2 years
70 overall  for  MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program
86 overall  for  STEP  (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 20 in reading & writing, minimum score of 16 in speaking, and minimum score of 11 in listening.
NOTE: The institution code for ETS/TOEFL is 0936. Results must be sent directly to McMaster electronically from the testing centre
Please note we  do  accept TOEFL Home Edition. We  do not  accept TOEFL Essentials or TOEFL MyBest scores.
Duolingo English Test
Duolingo English Test
120 overall  for all direct-entry undergraduate programs with a minimum score of 115 in Reading, 125 in Writing, 110 in Listening, and 115 in Speaking
115 overall  for  STEP  (Summer Transition through English Prep) with a minimum score of 115 in Reading, 125 in Writing, 100 in Listening, and 105 in Speaking
75   overall  for  MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program (no minimum sub-score requirements)
NOTE: results must be sent directly to McMaster electronically from the testing centre
CAE – Cambridge Advanced English (C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency)
180 overall  with a minimum score of 176 in each component. Valid for 2 years.
160 overall for  MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program
180 overall  for  STEP  (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 176 in reading and writing, and a minimum score of 160 in speaking and listening.
CAEL – Canadian Academic English Language
70 overall  with no component score below 60; valid for 2 years
50 overall  for  MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program
70 overall  for  STEP  (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 60 in reading and writing, and a minimum score of 50 in speaking and listening.
Please note McMaster University accepts both CAEL online and CAEL at a test centre.
PTE – Pearson Test of English Academic
60 overall  with minimum score of 60 in Writing and Speaking; valid for 2 years
40 overall for MELD  (McMaster English Development) Program
60 overall for STEP with minimum score of 50 in Reading, 45 in Listening, 45 Speaking, and 55 in Writing
LanguageCert Academic
70 overall with a minimum of 65 in each of the four components (Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening); results valid for 2 years
54 overall for MELD (McMaster English Development) Program
70 overall for STEP (Summer Transition through English Prep) Program, with a minimum score of 65 in reading and writing, and minimum score of 60 in speaking and listening
NOTE: To send your results to McMaster University, log into your LanguageCert account, go to the ‘Exam History’ section, select ‘Send Online’, search for ‘McMaster University – Undergraduate Admissions’, and share your results directly from the testing centre
