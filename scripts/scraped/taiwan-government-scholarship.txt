JUSTCOOL PhD Scholarships 2027 in China (Fully Funded)
By Saida Noreen
October 10, 2026

JUSTCOOL PhD Scholarships 2027 in China offer an exciting opportunity for talented students who want to pursue doctoral research in China. The programme brings together three leading universities in Shanghai: Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and Tongji University . Moreover, it welcomes international students from different countries who want to build a career in scientific research. Since the programme focuses on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, it can be a valuable opportunity for students with a strong interest in these subjects. Also apply for MBZUAI Postgraduate Scholarships 2027 in UAE (Fully Funded) .
Unlike a regular university scholarship, JUSTCOOL follows a joint admission process for international PhD students. In addition, the programme provides financial support, including a PhD fellowship, a full tuition waiver, free accommodation, medical insurance, and research or teaching assistantship opportunities. The programme plans to admit up to 90 students in 2027. However, applicants must meet the academic and English-language requirements and pass the required selection stages to receive an offer.
About the JUSTCOOL PhD Scholarships
JUSTCOOL stands for Joint Examinations and Admissions for International Students to Top Chinese Universities: PhD Program . It is a joint doctoral recruitment initiative established by Fudan University in partnership with Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Tongji University .
Through this programme, international students can apply for doctoral study in selected scientific disciplines. Furthermore, the programme uses English as its language of instruction, making it accessible to qualified international applicants who meet its language requirements.
The initiative aims to attract promising young researchers and prepare them for long-term academic and scientific careers. Therefore, it may suit students who want to gain advanced research experience, work with experienced supervisors and use modern research facilities in China. Participating Universities
JUSTCOOL brings together three major research universities in Shanghai.
1. Fudan University
Fudan University offers a strong academic environment for students interested in scientific research. Its broad research community can help doctoral students explore new ideas and develop their research skills.
2. Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)
Shanghai Jiao Tong University is known for its research activities across science, engineering, medicine and other disciplines. Consequently, it can be an attractive option for students who want to work in a research-focused environment.
3. Tongji University
Tongji University provides research and academic opportunities across several scientific and technical areas. Moreover, students can benefit from the wider academic environment created by the programme&#8217;s three university partners.

The official JUSTCOOL programme lists four main academic fields:
Mathematics
Physics
Chemistry
Biology
Eligibility Criteria for JUSTCOOL PhD Scholarships:
Applicants must meet the programme&#8217;s academic, nationality, and English-language requirements. Therefore, review the following criteria before starting your application.
Currently study Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, or Biology at the undergraduate level.
Obtain their bachelor&#8217;s degree between June 2026 and July 2027 .
Rank within the top 20% of their undergraduate cohort at the time of application.
Demonstrate strong academic ability and research potential.
Show a clear interest in pursuing doctoral research in their chosen discipline.
English Language Requirements:
Applicants must meet at least one of the following conditions:
Come from an English-speaking country where English is their first language.
Hold an IELTS score of at least 6.5 .
Hold a TOEFL iBT score of at least 90 .
Hold a Duolingo English Test score of at least 110 .
Hold a PTE Academic score of at least 61 .

Complete the online application form.
Submit your application before November 14, 2026.

IPPA Chiang Mai Winter School 2026 for International Students:
Applications are invited to apply for the IPPA Chiang Mai Winter School 2026 in Thailand . The five-day program is especially beneficial for doctorate students preparing dissertations and early-career researchers looking to improve their academic work since it blends theoretical learning with actual research development. The goal of the Chiang Mai International Winter School is to improve the research skills of up-and-coming academics in public policy and related fields. Also, apply for Erasmus Mundus EMMIR Scholarship 2027 (Funded) .
Chiang Mai International Winter School on Public Policy 2026 is a five-day academic training event intended for PhD candidates and early-career researchers and is now being accepted by the International Public Policy Association (IPPA). In addition to obtaining professional evaluation on their own research projects, the program provides an intensive learning environment where participants can enhance their comprehension of public policy theories, ideas, and research procedures.
Co-organized with the Chiang Mai University School of Public Policy, the Chiang Mai International Winter School on Public Policy will take place from November 30 to December 4. Students will take part in case studies and seminars where they can talk about their research topics and get input from renowned public policy experts as well as their peers. Participants will interact with internationally renowned scholars and work with colleagues from other nations and research backgrounds through lectures, specialized courses, seminars, and case studies. The Winter School offers up-and-coming researchers a special opportunity to improve the caliber of their work as well as their international academic ties.
